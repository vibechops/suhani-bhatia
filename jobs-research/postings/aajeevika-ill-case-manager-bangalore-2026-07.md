India Labour Line Case Manager – 4 positions (language tracks)
Location: Bangalore (Case Manager – Kannada), (Case Manager – Tamil & Telugu) & (Case Manager – Hindi)
Apply by: 31 Jul 2026

Aajeevika Bureau seeks to recruit experienced and committed colleagues to join the India Labourline (ILL) as Case Managers. The India Labourline is a phone and field-based programme to provide information, counselling, distress support, and legal aid to informal workers throughout India. It is also a powerful Grievance Redressal Forum to address labour rights violations faced by all categories of waged and self-employed workers in construction, factories, urban services, garments & textiles, farm work, street vending, and other sectors. ILL helps thousands of workers resolve their wage disputes and ensures that they do not lose their rightful earnings to fraud and theft. ILL is operationalised by Aajeevika Bureau and a network of civil society organisations working on labour issues across the country. For more information: www.indialabourline.org.

Language tracks (all based in Bangalore):
- Case Manager – Kannada: Fluency in Kannada and Hindi essential; familiarity with one or two south Indian languages desirable.
- Case Manager – Hindi: Fluency in Hindi essential; familiarity with one or two south Indian languages desirable.
- Case Manager – Tamil & Telugu: Fluency in Tamil, Telugu and Hindi essential.

Role will entail:
- Answering calls coming into India Labour Line, providing first counselling and advice to workers
- Engaging with employers / contractors and encouraging them to join the mediation process
- Conducting mediation between workers and employers /contractors
- Issuing legal notices and representations on behalf of aggrieved workers
- Follow up on issues registered by workers and referring cases to concerned departments and NGO networks
- Mediation and conflict resolution skills to resolve disputes between workers and employers / contractors
- Enhance skills by studying labour laws, labour policies and schemes; capacity building training provided
- Experience of working in labour and other rights based civil society organisation programmes / projects will be a major advantage

Compensation noted in posting in the range of Rs. 3.5 - 4 lakhs p.a.

How to Apply: Send CV with Cover Letter to jobs@indialabourline.org by 31 July 2026. Applications reviewed on a rolling basis. Mention position language track in subject line (e.g. “ILL Case Manager – Kannada”).
