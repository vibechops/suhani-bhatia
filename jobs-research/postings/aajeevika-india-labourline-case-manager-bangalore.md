India Labour Line Case Manager
Organization: Aajeevika Bureau
Location: Bangalore, Karnataka

Aajeevika Bureau seeks to recruit two experienced and committed colleagues to join the India Labourline (ILL) as Case Managers. The India Labourline is a phone and field-based programme to provide information, counselling, distress support, and legal aid to informal workers throughout India. It is also a powerful Grievance Redressal Forum to address labour rights violations faced by all categories of waged and self-employed workers in construction, factories, urban services, garments & textiles, farm work, street vending, and other sectors. ILL helps thousands of workers resolve their wage disputes and ensures that they do not lose their rightful earnings to fraud and theft. ILL is operationalised by Aajeevika Bureau and a network of civil society organisations working on labour issues across the country. For more information: www.indialabourline.org

Two Case Manager (CM) positions are available. One position requires fluency in Kannada and Hindi, while the other requires fluency in Tamil, Telugu, and Hindi. All positions are based in Bangalore. Preference will be given to candidates proficient in multiple South Indian languages along with Hindi.

The Case Managers will be handling calls on the India Labour Line to help provide information, and legal counselling to the informal sector and to register labour dispute cases.

- Experience of working in labour and other rights based civil society organisation programmes / projects will be a major advantage.
- The programme requires and expects that the Case Managers will enhance their skills and competence by studying labour laws, Labour policies and schemes, as well as other resources on labour issues. Capacity Building training and resources will be provided.

Aajeevika Bureau is an equal opportunity employer and encourages persons with physical disabilities, diverse gender identities and under-represented groups to apply.
