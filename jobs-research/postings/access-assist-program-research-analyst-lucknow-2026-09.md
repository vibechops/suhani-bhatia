About ACCESS ASSIST
ACCESS ASSIST is a development-sector consulting and advisory organization working at the intersection of financial services, financial inclusion, livelihoods, economic opportunity and digital transformation. ACCESS ASSIST works with financial institutions, governments, development partners, investors, industry bodies and other ecosystem stakeholders to undertake research, provide strategic and technical advisory, design innovative programs and support implementation of solutions that expand economic and financial opportunities for underserved and emerging segments.

About the Role
ACCESS ASSIST is looking for a Program & Research Analyst to join its consulting and research team in Lucknow. The position will involve working across a range of research, consulting, advisory and program assignments, with a particular focus on the financial services sector.

Key Responsibilities include:
Conduct market, sector and institutional research across banking, insurance, NBFCs, fintech, digital financial services and financial inclusion.
Analyse industry trends, regulatory developments, competitive dynamics, emerging technologies and new business models.
Undertake market assessments, landscape studies, benchmarking exercises, customer and institutional research, opportunity assessments and sector scans.
Conduct secondary research using regulatory publications, annual reports, financial statements, investor presentations, industry reports, databases, public datasets.
Support and conduct primary research, stakeholder consultations, interviews and surveys.
Collect, clean, structure and analyse quantitative and qualitative data.
Develop Excel-based analytical models, dashboards, charts and data visualizations and, where relevant, use tools such as Power BI, Tableau, SQL, Python or R.
Prepare high-quality PowerPoint presentations, reports, research papers, market assessments, case studies, concept notes and other client deliverables.
Support project planning, workplans, timelines, deliverable tracking and quality assurance.
Contribute to proposals, EOIs, concept notes and technical approaches.

Candidate Profile
Minimum of 2 years of relevant professional experience. Direct, hands-on experience in the financial sector—particularly banking, insurance, NBFCs, fintech—strongly preferred. Experience in financial-sector consulting, strategy, advisory, market research or development consulting with significant engagement with financial institutions also considered relevant.

Strong proficiency in MS Excel is required. Experience with Power BI, Tableau, SQL, Python, R or other analytical tools will be an advantage. Excellent written and verbal communication skills.

Qualifications
A postgraduate qualification in Finance, Economics, Business Administration, Public Policy, Statistics, Data Analytics, Development Studies or a related discipline is preferred. Candidates with a relevant undergraduate degree and strong practical experience will also be considered.
