Energy Strategy Group Analyst (Ahmedabad)
Adani Energy Solutions | Ahmedabad | Posted 14 Sep

Objective: To support strategic decision-making and business growth across the energy portfolio by providing data-driven insights, market intelligence, financial analysis, competitive assessments, and strategic recommendations while enabling innovation, stakeholder engagement, and long-term value creation.

Work Experience (Range of years):
- 3-6 years of experience in energy strategy, corporate strategy, consulting or regulatory affairs and business development in the energy/power/utilities sector with a profound understanding of latest trends and ongoing developments in the sector.
- Demonstrated analytical skills, with the ability to interpret financial statements, build excel models and deliver data-driven insights.
- Solid communication and presentation abilities, capable of supporting senior stakeholders in strategy discussions and decision-making processes.

Educational Qualification:
- B.E./B.Tech. and /or M.E./M.Tech./MBA/CA (Preferred Engineering with MBA)

Roles & Responsibilities:
- Support business strategy development through market research, data analysis, and financial modeling.
- Conduct industry, regulatory, and competitor analysis across the power and energy sector.
- Identify growth opportunities, evaluate business models, and perform feasibility studies.
- Track strategic initiatives, prepare performance metrics, and provide insights to senior leadership.
- Analyze trends in power generation, renewable energy, transmission, distribution, energy storage, and power markets.
- Develop scenario analysis and data-driven recommendations for strategic decision-making.
- Support innovation initiatives by assessing new revenue streams and market entry strategies.
- Prepare presentations, reports, white papers, policy notes, and management updates for senior stakeholders.
- Engage with internal stakeholders including Business Development, Projects, O&M, Finance, HR, IT, and Techno-commercial teams.
- Coordinate with external stakeholders such as government bodies, industry associations, consultants, and think tanks.
- Support policy advocacy efforts and represent organizational viewpoints through strategic documentation.
- Facilitate cross-functional collaboration to ensure alignment with business goals and strategic priorities.
