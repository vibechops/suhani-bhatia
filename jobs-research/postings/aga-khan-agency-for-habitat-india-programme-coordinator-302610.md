Job Description 
 
Job Title: Programme Coordinator 

Department: Developmental Programmes 

Reporting Line: Programme Manager 

Position Location: Chatrapati Sambhaji Nagar 

Nature of Contract: Full time 

Period of Consultancy: 1 year plus extension up to three years 

Contact: jobs.akahi@akdn.org 

Scope of work and deliverables: 
• Support the Programme Manager in ensuring the smooth, effective, and timely implementation of projects focused on environmental sustainability. 
• Provide guidance and field-level support to community mobilisers to facilitate effective community engagement and project implementation. 
• Conduct regular field visits to monitor and support project activities, identify implementation challenges, and prepare periodic progress reports as required. 
• Support the development and strengthening of partnerships with public and private sector agencies, while identifying new opportunities and avenues for collaboration. 
• Build and maintain strong relationships with target communities, government authorities, NGOs, community-based organizations, and other relevant stakeholders. 
• Develop Information, Education and Communication (IEC) materials and support documentation for training, knowledge sharing, wider dissemination, and stakeholder engagement. 
• Prepare Project data MIS. 

SPECIFICATIONS (MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS) 

Education: Post Graduation in Development Studies | B.Sc. / M.Sc. Environment Sciences | Environment Sustainability, Water Policy | B.E. Civil would be an added advantage. 

Experience: Minimum of 2 to 5 years of experience 

Technical Skills: MS Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) 

Additional: Should be proficient in English, Hindi and Marathi. 

Level of Responsibility: 

Budget: Yes 

Direct Reports: Yes 

Key Relations: 

Internal relations: Programme Manager 

External relations: Community, Vendors, Government Agencies (Municipal Corporation) 

To apply share position applied for in subject line with updated CV to: jobs.akahi@akdn.org
