PAID INTERNSHIP ALERT | PAID RESEARCH & PROJECT INTERN | AGAMI

May 20, 2026

Internship Description:

Agami is inviting applications for a paid internship opportunity for individuals interested in working on emerging questions surrounding legal systems, coexistence, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

This internship offers an opportunity to work closely on projects exploring how legal systems can better respond to questions of coexistence while engaging with practitioners, researchers, ecological experts, community actors, and institutions across different fields.

The role is ideal for individuals who are curious, reflective, research-oriented, and interested in meaningful interdisciplinary work at the intersection of law, society, and ecology.

Details:

• Role: Research & Project Intern • Organisation: Agami • Duration: 6–8 Months • Nature: Paid Internship • Weekly Commitment: Approximately 15–18 Hours • Joining: Immediate

Key Responsibilities:

• Supporting projects relating to legal systems and coexistence-related questions • Assisting in research, coordination, and interdisciplinary project work • Engaging with practitioners, researchers, institutions, and community stakeholders • Contributing to discussions and analytical work across legal and ecological themes • Supporting ongoing project development and collaborative initiatives

Eligibility & Preference:

• Strong interest in law, society, governance, ecology, or interdisciplinary policy work • Curious, thoughtful, and willing to learn in a collaborative environment • Good research, analytical, drafting, and communication skills • Ability to work independently while engaging effectively with diverse stakeholders • Candidates interested in long-term meaningful engagement and emerging legal questions will be preferred

What This Internship Offers:

• Opportunity to work closely on meaningful and emerging interdisciplinary issues • Exposure to practitioners, researchers, institutions, and community actors • Regular mentorship and collaborative learning support • Hands-on experience in research and project-based work • Opportunity to build relationships across legal and ecological ecosystems
