About American India Foundation Trust

The American India Foundation Trust (AIF) is committed to improving the lives of India’s underprivileged, with a special focus on women, children and youth. The Foundation works through high-impact interventions in education, health and livelihoods.

Founded over two decades ago following the Gujarat earthquake, AIF has impacted 20.47 million people across 35 States and Union Territories of India.

AIF is registered as a Trust in India with an independent Board of Trustees and is registered under 80G and 12A.

About Digital Equalizer Program

Digital Equalizer (DE) is AIF’s flagship education program. It envisions a world where children from underserved communities, especially girls, are prepared for their careers and lives.

The program works to bridge India’s digital divide by:

- Providing      and integrating technology into underperforming and under-resourced public      schools.

- Training      teachers in techno-pedagogy and STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering      and Mathematics) methodology.

- Transforming      classrooms into collaborative and interactive learning environments.

Since its inception in 2004, the program has:

- Empowered      8.14 million children with interactive STEM experiences.

- Trained      212,585 teachers in STEM pedagogy.

- Transformed      46,596 schools across 35 states and union territories.
