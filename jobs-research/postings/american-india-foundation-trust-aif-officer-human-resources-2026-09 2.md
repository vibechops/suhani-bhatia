About American India Foundation Trust

The American India Foundation Trust (AIF) is committed to improving the lives of India’s underprivileged communities, with a special focus on women, children and youth. Its high-impact interventions span education, health and livelihoods.

Founded over two decades ago following the Gujarat earthquake, AIF has impacted 20.47 million people across 35 States and Union Territories of India.

AIF is registered as a Trust in India with an independent Board of Trustees and is registered under 80G and 12A.

About the Position

The HR Officer will support activities and initiatives addressing the human resource needs of AIF’s Education Program.

The incumbent will work closely with programme teams to support HR management requirements across India and coordinate with Program HR to ensure that the Education Program’s HR activities and processes are aligned with organization-wide policies and practices.

The role offers significant learning and contribution opportunities for someone interested in working with fast-growing social-impact teams operating at the grassroots level.

Key Responsibilities

Recruitment & Onboarding – 70%

- Own      the end-to-end recruitment lifecycle, from requisition intake      through offer closure, ensuring quality hiring within defined timelines.

- Drive      the complete joining and onboarding journey for new employees, ensuring      documentation and joining formalities are completed accurately and on      time.

- Coordinate      with the HR SPOC to follow up on structured induction and onboarding for      new joinees.

- Maintain      and update recruitment- and employee-related trackers, ensuring data      accuracy and real-time visibility for stakeholders.

- Ensure      a consistently positive experience for all stakeholders in the hiring      process, including candidates, new joinees, hiring managers and      internal departments.

HR Operations – 30%

- Set      up and maintain personnel files for all employees, ensuring completeness      and audit readiness.

- Draft      and issue HR letters and documentation, including:

- Offer       letters

- Appointment       letters

- Experience       letters

- Confirmation       letters

- Maintain      accurate, confidential and up-to-date employee records across personnel      files, HRMS and HR databases, including updates relating to      employee movements and contractual changes.

- Prepare      HR reports, MIS, trackers and related documentation.

- Support      periodic data and documentation audits and drive timely closure of      identified gaps.

Qualifications, Experience & Skills

- Bachelor’s      degree in Human Resources, Business Administration or a related field.

- MBA/PGDM      in HR will be an added advantage.

- 2–5      years of relevant experience in HR operations, recruitment, HRMS/HRIS      or employee lifecycle management.

- Good      understanding of HR processes, employee lifecycle, HR documentation and HR      operations.

- Proficiency      in managing employee data, HR MIS and records using MS Excel and HR      systems.

- Experience      working in a dynamic, fast-paced environment.

- Exposure      to the development/social sector will be an added advantage.

- Strong      coordination, communication and stakeholder-management skills.

- Strong      attention to detail with the ability to maintain accuracy, manage multiple      priorities and meet timelines.

Skills & Competencies

- Strong      HR operations, data-management and documentation skills with high      attention to detail.

- Proficiency      in MS Office, particularly Excel and PowerPoint, and familiarity      with HRMS/HRIS.

- Good      written and verbal communication, coordination, interpersonal and      stakeholder-management skills.

- Ability      to manage multiple priorities, work independently, meet timelines and take      ownership of assigned responsibilities.

- Demonstrate      professionalism, confidentiality, integrity and ethical conduct in      all HR activities.
