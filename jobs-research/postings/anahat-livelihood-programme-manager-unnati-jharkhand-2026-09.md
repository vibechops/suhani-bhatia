Livelihood Programme Manager - Project Unnati
Anahat for Change Foundation
Location: Kolkata / Jharkhand field
Application Deadline: Sept 10, 2026

Requirements:
1. Experience: Minimum 5–8 years of professional experience in the Social Development Sector, with a proven track record in Livelihood programs and grassroots community work. Having experience in the sustainable menstrual product sector will add value.
2. SHG & JSLPS Expertise: 4 to 10 years of hands-on experience working alongside State Rural Livelihood Missions (JSLPS) and Self-Help Group (SHG) federations.
3. Value Chain / Market Linkage: Proven ability in creating demand-and-supply networks and enterprise models at the community level.
4. Team Leadership: Prior experience managing and supporting field teams.
5. Education: Graduate/Post-Graduate degree in Sales & Marketing, Social Work (MSW), Rural Development, Management, or related social science disciplines.

Preferred / Added Value: Prior experience in the Menstrual Health & Hygiene (MHM) space, specifically in promoting, distributing, or marketing reusable cloth pads or sustainable health solutions.

Essential Assets & Operational Requirements:
- Own Vehicle: Must possess a functional two-wheeler (Bike / Scooty) with a valid driving license for regular field visits.
- Own Laptop: Must have a working laptop for documentation, reporting, and team coordination.
- Travel Flexibility: Willingness to undertake extensive field travel within Jharkhand.
- Language Proficiency: Fluency in Hindi is required; knowledge of local Jharkhand dialects/languages is a strong plus.

Position Type: Full-time (1-Year Renewable Contract based on performance)
Travel: High (Extensive intra-state travel across districts in Jharkhand)
Compensation: 45k (negotiable)
