## What APCO's India Project Consultant role covers (from APCO India team pages and comparable APCO Project Consultant JDs)

Project Consultant is APCO's first full-time consulting grade, working under the supervision of a Consultant and interfacing with all levels of staff and clients. Typical primary responsibilities:

- Support at least 3-4 projects across tasks from policy research, issue monitoring, stakeholder outreach facilitation to report and presentation development.
- Government policy analysis and on-ground research on government policies in various sectors as part of the Government Relations and Corporate Advisory team.
- Preparing client deliverables, meeting materials, briefings and proposals; project coordination.

Typical standards (from APCO Project Consultant, Government Affairs listings): University degree or higher, preferred in Journalism, Political Science, International Affairs, Economics or related fields; excellent English research, writing and editing skills; minimum of 6 months' internship or full-time work experience preferably in an industry association, think tank, government or consulting; familiarity and keen interest in the country's political and economic development; strong knowledge of any of APCO's focus sectors (technology, healthcare, retail, education) is a plus.

## About APCO in India

In 2007, APCO began operations in India and offerings include corporate advisory, government relations, strategic communications, coalition building, crisis management and management consulting services. Our clients in India include multinational companies, industry associations, non-profit organizations and governments.

We take an integrated approach to consulting, which is based on our strong public affairs heritage, our nuanced understanding of strategic communications and remarkable research, creative and digital capabilities.

Our team in India comprises a significant bench of senior-level staff with deep expertise coming from a range of journalistic, business, management consulting, communications, academic and government backgrounds. The team is based in New Delhi, Mumbai and Bengaluru (about 60 professionals across three offices plus a project office in Jaipur). New Delhi office: Level 3B, DLF Centre, Sansad Marg, Connaught Place, New Delhi 110001, +91.11.6128.9400.
