Position Title: Manager - Communications & Operations

Location: New Delhi, India

No. of Open Position(s): 01

Experience Required: 4-6 years

Role Overview:

Arcus Policy Research is seeking a creative, proactive, and organised Communications & Operations Manager to lead the organisation’s external and internal communications, build and manage its brand presence, and ensure the smooth day-to-day functioning of office operations. The role has two core pillars: (1) Communications & Creatives - owning Arcus’s content, brand voice, social media, and visual communications; and (2) Office Operations - ensuring seamless administrative and facility management. The ideal candidate brings a strong communications and creative background, with the organisational capability to manage day-to-day operational needs.

Key Responsibilities

Communications & Creatives

• Own and execute Arcus’s content strategy across digital channels, with primary ownership of LinkedIn and other relevant platforms.

• Write, edit, and package research outputs, policy briefs, and organisational updates into compelling communications for diverse audiences (policymakers, funders, media, and the public).

• Design and produce visual assets (presentations, infographics, reports, social media creatives) using tools such as Canva, Adobe suite, or equivalent, ensuring consistency with the Arcus brand identity.

• Manage the editorial calendar; plan, schedule, and publish content across channels in a timely and consistent manner.

• Manage external communications including newsletters, press releases, event promotions, and stakeholder outreach materials.

• Drive internal communications - ensuring organisational announcements, updates, and knowledge are disseminated clearly and promptly across the team.

• Track engagement metrics and generate insights to continuously improve the reach and impact of Arcus’s communications.

Office Operations

• Ensure smooth day-to-day office operations including facilities, housekeeping, maintenance, and vendor coordination.

• Maintain accurate records of organisational assets, contracts, licenses, and operational documents; manage asset inventory and tracking.

• Coordinate domestic and international travel arrangements, logistics, and accommodation bookings.

• Develop and maintain operational processes, SOPs, and documentation standards; monitor operational efficiency and recommend improvements.

• Support onboarding and offboarding processes; coordinate with the relevant team for access, equipment, and HRMS workflows (e.g., Zoho People or equivalent).

• Serve as the first point of contact for basic IT coordination - liaising with external vendors for hardware/software issues, managing software subscriptions, and tracking IT assets.

• Support organisational events, meetings, and other administrative initiatives as required.

• Ensure compliance with company policies, administrative procedures, and operational guidelines; drive high standards of responsiveness and accountability.

Required Skills & Qualifications

• Bachelor’s degree in Communications, Journalism, Media Studies, English, Business Administration, or a related field.

• Strong understanding of business operations, process management, and organizational communication.

• Demonstrated ability to write clearly and compellingly for diverse audiences - including social media posts, newsletters, reports, and stakeholder communications.

• Hands-on experience with design tools (Canva, Adobe Express, or equivalent) and confidence producing professional visual assets with minimal supervision.

• Good understanding of social media strategy and content management, particularly LinkedIn; familiarity with scheduling tools and engagement analytics.

• Working familiarity with Microsoft 365 tools (Outlook, Teams, OneDrive, Word, PowerPoint) for day-to-day productivity and communications.

• Self-driven, proactive, and capable of taking ownership of communication
