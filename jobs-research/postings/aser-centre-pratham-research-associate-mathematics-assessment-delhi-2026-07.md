# Research Associate (Mathematics), Assessment Unit, ASER

A. About Pratham:

Pratham was founded in 1995, to provide preschool education to children in the slums of Mumbai. Over the last 30 years, Pratham has grown to be one of India's largest NGOs working to provide quality education to underprivileged youth and children in over 25 states and union territories across the country, with a range of interventions.

Pratham is a widely recognized organization, having received notable awards such as the Lui Che Woo Prize, the WISE Prize for Innovation, Skoll Award for Social Entrepreneurship, the Henry R Kravis Prize in Leadership and the CNN-IBN Indian of the Year for Public Service. For more details, refer to www.pratham.org.

B. About Program:

Established in 2008, ASER is the measurement, assessment, and research arm of Pratham Education Foundation. ASER generates evidence for action and focuses on outcomes and processes in education and other social sectors. It builds the capacity of individuals and institutions to measure at scale, understand, communicate and act upon the findings of assessments and research, with the objective of improving the quality of social sector programs. It aims to create a culture where rigorous measurement of outcomes is integral to action, thus bridging the gap between theory and practice, assumption and reality.

The Assessment Unit at ASER specializes in designing innovative assessment tools for various educational programs, both nationally and internationally. Our expertise spans Early Childhood Education (ECE), Primary, and Upper-Primary groups, as well as EdTech initiatives. We develop assessments to measure student learning outcomes, teacher effectiveness, and impact of educational interventions with a focus on continuous improvement. Our recent endeavors include creating tools to evaluate the impact of technology-driven learning interventions and designing adaptive assessments on Android applications.

C. Position Summary:

The Research Associate will be responsible for all aspects related to designing educational assessments and frameworks pertaining to Mathematics oriented projects of the Assessment Unit. The role may additionally involve project management duties which include setting and delivering on project timelines and milestones, and coordinating closely with various units at ASER and Pratham and/or external clients/donors.

D. Key Responsibilities:

1. Design and development of mathematics assessments
- Develop the Mathematics assessment frameworks, theory of change, and technical report through extensive literature review and field pilots
- Develop simple, standardized, and discriminating items/assessment tools to measure children's knowledge and skills across multiple programs and languages, through extensive literature review, and field pilots.
- Translate and validate items and content to enhance the quality of the assessment tool

2. Development of training material and plan
- Create print and audio-visual material for training of trainers and volunteers
- Create detailed training plans for different levels of training
- Review and finalize the regional translations of training material

3. Planning and implementing at scale
- Support in identifying resource requirements for projects and allocate them adequately
- Create a plan, timeline, and budget for assessment roll-out from literature review to implementation
- Troubleshoot problems during assessment roll-out with support from the Senior Researchers and Teammates.

4. Data interpretation and report writing
- Explore different interpretations and visualizations of collected data
- Write and edit reports and visualize report design

5. Miscellaneous
- Create content such as proposals, articles, research papers and evidence briefs as required
- Participate in any other research, assessment, survey project of ASER on need basis
