Associate, Policy Communications
ICPP / Ashoka University

ICPP is looking for an Associate, Policy Communications to help shape and strengthen its external presence — from its digital channels and website to the visual identity of its published research. The Associate will work closely with the research and leadership teams to ensure ICPP’s work is packaged in a way that is on-brand, accessible, and resonant with professional and policy audiences. The role also involves coordinating communications with ICPP’s client ministries and agencies, and supporting outreach for institutional events and programmes.

Responsibilities (from listing):
• Own and maintain ICPP’s website — update content, coordinate with developers for structural changes, and ensure the site accurately reflects ICPP.
• Design and adapt communication materials across formats — carousel posts, graphics, snippets, and newsletters — calibrated to professional and policy audiences.
• Work with researchers and the leadership team to translate findings and project updates into accessible, shareable content formats.
• Write sharp, audience-aware captions, summaries, and short-form policy explainers for digital channels, ensuring policy nuance is retained without compromising readability.
• Coordinate newsletter production — copy, layout, mailing lists, and scheduling.

Requirements:
• A bachelor’s degree in any discipline; a background in communications, design, journalism, public policy, or social sciences is preferred.
• 1–3 years of relevant experience in institutional communications, policy outreach, or a design-adjacent role.
• Excellent writing and editing skills in English, with demonstrated ability to calibrate communications for professional and policy audiences.
• Experience managing an institutional social media account, particularly LinkedIn.
