Job Title: Executive
Location: Delhi
Experience: 0-3 years

The role focuses on driving financial resource mobilization through sponsorship acquisition, delegate engagement, and membership development, while supporting the planning and execution of industry-focused events and policy research initiatives.

Responsibilities:
- Lead efforts in revenue generation through sponsorships, delegate acquisition, and membership drives.
- Plan, manage, and execute events, conferences, conclaves, and webinars centered on developments in:
  - Companies Act and Rules
  - Corporate Governance
  - SEBI Regulations
  - Competition Act
  - Other relevant legislative and regulatory frameworks impacting industry performance
- Develop and curate high-quality content, including research papers, reports, and supporting collateral.
- Conduct policy research by identifying key industry issues through stakeholder interactions, meetings, and conferences.
- Engage and liaise with stakeholders, including industry leaders, government bodies, and professional institutions to:
  - Promote participation
  - Drive delegate registration
  - Undertake tele-marketing initiatives
  - Secure sponsorships
- Oversee event logistics and coordination, ensuring seamless execution.
- Draft and present industry recommendations and policy inputs to regulators and policymakers.

Candidate profile:
- Self-starters with high motivation and the ability to thrive in dynamic and demanding environments are encouraged to apply.
- Working knowledge of corporate laws is essential.
- Understanding of event planning and coordination.
- Exposure to organizing training programs, legal events, or professional conferences will be an advantage.
- Proficiency in MS Office tools and desktop research is required.
