ASSOCHAM Global Research - Job Opportunities

ASSOCHAM Global Research invites applications for the positions of Research Officer, Senior Research Officer, and Economist. Candidates should have strong academic credentials and relevant experience in economic research, policy analysis, industry research, and public policy advocacy.

The roles involve in-depth research across macroeconomics, trade, industry, investment, taxation, and emerging business trends, along with data analysis and the preparation of reports, presentations, and policy briefs. Senior positions will additionally require strong stakeholder engagement and policy advocacy capabilities, including interaction with government, regulators, industry, and research institutions.

Candidates should possess excellent analytical, communication, and report-writing skills, along with the ability to independently manage research assignments and deliver high-quality outputs within timelines.
