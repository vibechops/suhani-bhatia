About the Organization

We are a not-for-profit organisation with a vision to contribute towards a just, equitable, humane and sustainable society. Our work is focused on India.

Our vision guides us to work through a range of initiatives that seek to make a difference to the lives of people in our country and contribute to developing a society that we have pledged to ourselves in the Constitution.

Job Description

You will work with Government school teachers, helping them build their professional abilities and become better teachers. Our current focus is on Primary School teachers alongside our work with teachers in Upper Primary Schools.

Job Responsibilities

Design and conduct programs for continuous professional development of Teachers and Head Teachers on:

- Perspectives on education and society

- Subject knowledge (Early Language and Math is a specific focus area currently)

- Pedagogy and assessment

- Academic leadership

Develop a community of teachers interested in improving the quality of learning for children through:

- Peer learning - Working in groups with teachers on different subject specific topics as per the curriculum

- Collaborative work - Mobilizing and working with teachers to organize workshops and melas

- Reflective practices - Writing a daily diary, critically reviewing programs and one’s own contribution, engaging in collective dialogue to bring creativity in one’s own work

- Contributing towards management of our Teacher Learning Centres in different blocks

- Contribute towards building the value and culture of the Foundation by:

Practicing democratic values

- Supporting colleagues on various projects

- Developing yourself both academically and as a better human being

- Contributing to various initiatives, programs, events and annual plan of your District

Education Qualification/Required Skills & Experience

- Graduation with minimum 5 years teaching experience OR Postgraduation with a minimum of 2 years teaching experience.

- Candidates with relevant on-field experience in the social sector may also apply.

- Willingness to travel within the district/state.

- Language proficiency in Kannada and English.
