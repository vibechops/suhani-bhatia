Hiring Block Resource Person (BRP)
Bandhan-Konnagar is inviting applications for the position of Block Resource Person (BRP) under the SJY Programme.

Location: As per project allocation in any block of Bihar

Eligibility:
- Graduates with experience in rural development/Social mobilization.
- Minimum 3 years of grassroots field experience in rural Bihar.
- Ability to communicate effectively in Hindi and local dialects.
- Willingness to travel within the assigned blocks & additional blocks as per need.
- Commitment to working with ultra-poor and marginalized communities.
- Good documentation & reporting skills.

Role Highlights: This is a field-intensive role requiring extensive travel, engagement in remote and hard-to-reach areas, and sustained handholding of vulnerable households.

Role includes:
- Support the Rural Livelihoods Ecosystem of JEEViKA at block level
- Coordinate with community institutions and stakeholders
- Deliver trainings to project staff, cadre & households
- Documentation and reporting
- Handholding support to MRPs & SJY program participants towards development of sustainable livelihoods

How to apply: Send updated CV with subject line “Application for BRP – SJY”
Mail Id: sjy.admin@bandhan.org
Last date: 26th February, 2026
First preference to candidates who previously served as BRP in SJY with overall good track record.
