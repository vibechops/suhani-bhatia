# BCG Senior Analyst – BCG Vantage 2026 in Gurgaon: Join the Social Impact Education and Skilling Practice at Boston Consulting Group

Posted by Ning Ma | Aug 17, 2026 | Jobs, Asia | 0 | 

Boston Consulting Group is recruiting a Senior Analyst – BCG Vantage to join its Social Impact Practice Area in Gurgaon, Haryana, India. The full-time position is designed for experienced professionals with deep expertise in education and skilling, particularly candidates who have worked across K–12 education, higher education, technical and vocational education and training, government transformation programmes, donor-funded initiatives, or consulting.

The role sits on BCG Vantage’s Client Focus path and combines sector expertise, consulting-style problem solving, client engagement, research and analysis, business development, knowledge creation, and support to project teams delivering large-scale education and skilling transformations.

Boston Consulting Group is seeking candidates with approximately 4–6 years of relevant industry experience, while consulting experience is strongly preferred. A bachelor’s degree is required, and an advanced degree is considered advantageous.

The position is based in Gurgaon and will involve direct interaction with clients, BCG Project Leaders, Associates, Consultants, global topic leaders, and other colleagues across the firm. Travel to client locations is expected when required to deliver projects and workshops.

The vacancy is listed under BCG’s Expertise and Insights category with Job ID 58923.

No specific application closing date is stated in the information provided. Interested applicants should therefore consider applying as early as possible while the vacancy remains open.

## About Boston Consulting Group

Boston Consulting Group, widely known as BCG, is a global management consulting firm that works with leaders across business, government, and society to address complex challenges and identify opportunities for transformation and growth.

Founded in 1963, BCG was an early pioneer of business strategy consulting. Its work has since expanded considerably beyond traditional strategy.

Today, BCG supports organisations with areas including:

- Business transformation
- Corporate strategy
- Digital transformation
- Technology
- Organisational change
- Competitive advantage
- Innovation
- Corporate ventures
- Public-sector transformation
- Social impact
- Sustainability
- Data and analytics
- Artificial intelligence
- Organisational purpose

BCG emphasizes the combination of human expertise with digital capabilities and works through multidisciplinary teams that bring together sector knowledge, functional expertise, analytical capabilities, and technology.

The Senior Analyst position in Gurgaon forms part of this wider model while concentrating specifically on the education and skilling sector within BCG’s Social Impact Practice Area.
