About BharatCares

BharatCares (SMEC Trust), the social impact arm of the CSRBOX Group, stands amongst the Top 50 Social Impact Organizations in India. Established for program delivery, BharatCares' commitment revolves around the principles of innovation, technology, and scalability, aiming to address societal challenges comprehensively.

Functioning as a social solution bridge, BharatCares facilitates collaboration among communities, innovators, funders and government to implement the best impact solutions for the last mile. Its flagship initiatives focus on improving education, employability, rural and healthcare infrastructure, entrepreneurship, environmental sustainability and road safety.

About the Position

BharatCares is looking for an Associate Manager – CSR Tech Programs to lead the planning, implementation, monitoring and delivery of technology-focused CSR and skilling programs in Hosur and surrounding industrial clusters.

The role will primarily focus on programs related to:

CNC | Mechatronics | Electric Vehicles (EV) | Automotive Technologies | Industrial Automation | Industry 4.0

The ideal candidate should have hands-on experience managing technical skilling, vocational training, employability or industry-linked programs in one or more of these domains.

The candidate should understand both the technical training ecosystem and program management requirements, including training centre operations, trainers, candidates, industry partners, assessments, certifications, placements and CSR stakeholders.

Key Responsibilities

1. Technical CSR Program Management

- Lead      end-to-end implementation of CSR-funded technical skilling programs      focused on CNC, Mechatronics, EV, Automotive, Industrial Automation and      related technologies.

- Develop      detailed implementation plans, timelines, milestones and program delivery      strategies.

- Manage      multiple training centres/batches and ensure timely achievement of program      targets.

- Ensure      programs are delivered as per approved SOW, budgets, quality standards and      CSR objectives.

- Identify      implementation challenges and proactively develop corrective action plans.

2. CNC, Mechatronics & EV Program Operations

Manage technical training programs involving areas such as:

- CNC      Machine Operations & Programming

- CNC      Turning/Milling

- Mechatronics

- Industrial      Automation

- PLC      and Automation

- Robotics

- Electric      Vehicle Technology

- EV      Service & Maintenance

- Automotive      Electronics

- Battery      Technology

- Related      Industry 4.0 skills

Additionally:

- Coordinate      with technical experts and trainers to ensure curriculum relevance and      quality.

- Ensure      availability and effective utilization of labs, machines, tools,      simulators, EV equipment and training infrastructure.

- Monitor      practical training, assessments, certifications and industry exposure.

3. Stakeholder & Client Management

- Act      as the key point of contact for corporate CSR teams, implementation      partners, training institutes, technical partners, government stakeholders      and industry partners.

- Conduct      regular review meetings with CSR partners and share program progress.

- Prepare      presentations, MIS, dashboards, reports and impact updates for clients.

- Understand      client expectations and translate them into actionable program      deliverables.

4. Industry & Placement Linkages

- Build      relationships with manufacturing, automotive, engineering, EV and      industrial companies in Hosur and nearby industrial clusters.

- Develop      industry partnerships for:

- Placement       opportunities

- Apprenticeships

- Internships

- Industrial       visits

- Guest       lectures

- OJT

- Curriculum       inputs

- Industry       exposure

- Track      placement outcomes and support the team in achieving placement targets.

- Understand      local industry manpower requirements and align training programs      accordingly.

5. Team & Centre Management

- Manage      and mentor project teams, centre coordinators, trainers, mobilization      teams and placement teams.

- Ensure      adequate staffing and timely deployment of trainers and technical      resources.

- Conduct      regular centre visits and field reviews.

- Monitor      attendance, batch progress, trainer performance, infrastructure and      learner engagement.

- Ensure      adherence to organizational policies, safety standards and operational      processes.

6. Monitoring, Evaluation & MIS

- Establish      effective monitoring mechanisms for program performance.

- Track      key indicators including:

- Mobilization

- Enrolment

- Attendance

- Training       completion

- Certification

- Assessment       scores

- Placement

- Retention

- Industry       engagement

- Prepare      weekly/monthly MIS and management reports.

- Identify      trends, gaps and deviations and recommend improvements.

- Ensure      documentation and compliance requirements are completed on time.
