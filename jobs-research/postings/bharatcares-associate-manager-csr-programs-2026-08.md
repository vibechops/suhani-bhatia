About BharatCares

BharatCares (SMEC Trust), the social impact arm of the CSRBOX Group stands tall amongst the Top 50 Social Impact Organizations in India. Specifically established and curated for program delivery, BharatCares' commitment revolves around the principles of innovation, technology, and scalability, aiming to address societal challenges comprehensively.

Functioning as a social solution bridge, BharatCares facilitates collaboration among communities, innovators, funders and government to implement the best impact solution for the last mile. Our flagship initiatives focus on improving education, employability, rural and healthcare infrastructure, entrepreneurship, environmental sustainability and road safety.

For more information, visit: www.bharatcares.org

Location: Guwahati
 No. of Position: 1

Role Overview

The Associate Manager – CSR Programs will be responsible for end-to-end management of CSR projects across multiple thematic areas such as education, livelihoods, agriculture, environment, and health.

This role requires strong project management, stakeholder engagement, and on-ground execution capabilities, along with a willingness to travel extensively and engage with local communities.

Key Responsibilities

1. Project Management & Implementation

- Plan,      coordinate, and execute multiple CSR projects across sectors.

- Ensure      timely delivery and quality implementation at the ground level.

- Manage      project timelines, budgets, and deliverables.

2. Stakeholder Engagement

- Liaise      with NGOs, corporates, government departments, and community stakeholders.

- Build      and maintain partnerships with local authorities and institutions.

- Ensure      effective coordination among all stakeholders.

3. Monitoring, Evaluation & Reporting

- Prepare      project reports, updates, and impact documentation.

- Manage      MIS and data tracking systems.

- Document      case studies, success stories, and field insights.

4. Community Mobilization

- Conduct      need assessments and beneficiary mobilization.

- Facilitate      awareness sessions and capacity-building activities.

- Ensure      active participation from local communities.

5. Cross-Functional Collaboration

- Coordinate      with internal teams such as communications, research, operations, and      tech.

- Contribute      to program design and strategic inputs.

6. Research & Insights

- Conduct      baseline, midline, and end-line studies.

- Stay      updated on sector trends, policies, and innovation models.

7. Flexibility & Ownership

- Take      ownership of projects and identify areas for improvement.

- Handle      additional responsibilities as required in a dynamic environment.

Key Requirements

- Master’s/Bachelor’s      degree in Social Work, Development Studies, Management, or related fields.

- 3–6      years of experience in CSR programs / development sector / project      implementation.

- Strong      communication and stakeholder management skills.

- Ability      to work across multiple thematic areas.

- Willingness      to travel extensively as per project requirements.

- Proficiency      in local language (Assamese) is preferred based on location.

What We’re Looking For

- A      hands-on professional with strong execution capability.

- Passion      for community engagement and social impact.

- Ability      to manage multiple projects in dynamic environments.

- Strong      sense of ownership and accountability.
