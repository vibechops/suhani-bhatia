About BharatCares

BharatCares (SMEC Trust), the social impact arm of the CSRBOX Group, stands amongst the Top 50 Social Impact Organizations in India. Specifically established and curated for program delivery, BharatCares' commitment revolves around the principles of innovation, technology and scalability, aiming to address societal challenges comprehensively.

Functioning as a social solution bridge, BharatCares facilitates collaboration among communities, innovators, funders and government to implement the best impact solutions for the last mile. Its flagship initiatives focus on improving education, employability, rural and healthcare infrastructure, entrepreneurship, environmental sustainability and road safety.

About the Position

BharatCares seeks a dedicated Associate Manager – Waste Management and Circular Economy to support and implement impactful, sustainable urban waste management programs across Maharashtra, Gujarat and Karnataka.

The candidate will assist in identifying, developing and operationalizing waste management solutions and circular economy initiatives through CSR and philanthropic collaborations.

Key Responsibilities

- Support      the identification, design and implementation of urban waste management      and circular economy projects aligned with BharatCares’ strategic      objectives.

- Collaborate      closely with the Manager and domain experts to execute projects      effectively in Maharashtra, Gujarat and Karnataka.

- Engage      and coordinate with local businesses, philanthropic organizations and      government bodies to facilitate resource mobilization under CSR and ESG      frameworks.

- Assist      in conducting research and analysis of innovative technologies, best      practices and viable waste management solutions.

- Develop      project proposals, concept notes, detailed presentations and operational      plans for potential collaborations.

- Provide      on-ground coordination with municipal corporations, regulatory authorities      and community stakeholders, ensuring program alignment with local and      regional policy frameworks.

- Conduct      regular monitoring, evaluation and documentation of project activities,      generating comprehensive reports for stakeholders.

- Contribute      to capacity-building activities and technical guidance within project      communities and partner organizations.

- Participate      in sector-related networks, forums and discussions to advocate best      practices and promote knowledge exchange.

Qualifications and Experience

- Bachelor’s      degree or higher in Environmental Science, Waste Management,      Sustainability or related fields.

- Minimum      3 years of relevant experience working on urban waste management,      circular economy solutions or sustainability projects.

- Familiarity      with policy, regulatory and institutional frameworks governing waste      management in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Karnataka and broadly in India.

- Experience      collaborating with local government authorities, corporate CSR teams and      community groups.

Skills and Competencies

- Strong      organizational and project coordination skills.

- Effective      communication and interpersonal skills for stakeholder engagement.

- Analytical      thinking with the ability to execute solution-oriented actions.

- Proficiency      in proposal writing, documentation and reporting.

- Fluency      in English and Hindi; proficiency in Marathi or Kannada is      advantageous.
