About BharatCares

BharatCares (SMEC Trust), the social impact arm of the CSRBOX Group, works towards creating sustainable and scalable solutions across communities through CSR and development programs. Our work spans areas including water and sanitation, livelihoods, education, healthcare, skilling, sustainability, and community development.

Position Overview

BharatCares is looking for an experienced and dynamic Manager – CSR & Water Programs to lead the implementation and management of water-focused CSR initiatives in Hyderabad and project locations.

The role will involve project implementation, team management, stakeholder engagement, government liaisoning, proposal development, MIS and reporting, along with regular field engagement. The ideal candidate should have strong project management skills, excellent communication abilities, and be comfortable travelling extensively as required.

Key Responsibilities

1. Project Planning & Implementation

- Lead the end-to-end implementation of CSR and      water-related programs.

- Develop and execute project plans, activity      schedules, and implementation strategies.

- Monitor project progress against agreed timelines,      targets, and deliverables.

- Ensure quality and timely execution of field      activities.

- Identify implementation challenges and develop      appropriate solutions.

2. Water Program Management

- Support and manage initiatives related to water      conservation, water access, WASH, groundwater management, rainwater      harvesting, community water systems, and related interventions.

- Coordinate with technical teams, implementation      partners, and communities for effective execution.

- Monitor field-level outcomes and ensure alignment      with project objectives.

3. Stakeholder & Government Engagement

- Build and maintain relationships with government      departments, local authorities, communities, CSR partners, NGOs, and other      stakeholders.

- Coordinate with relevant government officials and      departments for project implementation and approvals.

- Manage government liaisoning and convergence      opportunities for project activities.

- Represent BharatCares in stakeholder meetings,      consultations, and project reviews.

4. Team Management

- Lead, mentor, and supervise project team members      and field staff.

- Allocate responsibilities and monitor individual      and team performance.

- Conduct regular team reviews and provide guidance      to ensure project targets are achieved.

- Build a collaborative and accountable team      environment.

5. Proposal & Report Writing

- Develop project proposals, concept notes, budgets,      presentations, and donor documents.

- Prepare periodic project reports, progress updates,      and impact reports.

- Develop case studies, success stories, and project      documentation.

- Ensure reports are accurate, evidence-based, and      submitted within timelines.

6. MIS, Data & Monitoring

- Maintain project MIS and ensure timely and accurate      data collection.

- Monitor project indicators, outputs, outcomes, and      targets.

- Review field data and prepare dashboards and      management reports.

- Use data and field insights to identify gaps and      recommend corrective actions.

7. Field Coordination & Travel

- Conduct regular field visits to monitor project      activities and interact with communities.

- Coordinate with field teams and implementation      partners across project locations.

- Be open to frequent travel as required by project      requirements.

Key Requirements

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Social Work, Rural      Development, Development Studies, Environmental Science, Water Management,      Sustainability, Management, or a related field.

- 4–5 years of relevant experience in CSR, NGO,      development-sector, water, WASH, sustainability, or community development      programs.

- Demonstrated experience in project implementation      and team management.

- Experience in stakeholder management and government      liaisoning.

- Strong proposal, concept note, report writing, and      documentation skills.

- Good understanding of MIS, data tracking, and      project reporting.

- Fluency in the local language is      mandatory/preferred.

- Strong communication and interpersonal skills.

- Willingness to travel extensively to project      locations.

- Proficiency in MS Office, particularly Excel and      PowerPoint.
