About BharatCares

BharatCares, the implementation and programs arm of the CSRBOX Group, works closely with corporate CSR teams, foundations, government departments, and implementation partners to design and execute high-impact, outcome-oriented CSR programs across India.

Our work spans sectors such as healthcare, education, livelihoods, skilling, water resilience, sustainability, and community infrastructure, with a strong focus on partnership-driven implementation and measurable impact.

BharatCares is part of the broader CSRBOX ecosystem, which integrates Impact Practice, Platform, and Programs to support the full lifecycle of CSR and philanthropy initiatives.

Role Overview

The Manager - CSR Programs will be responsible for managing end-to-end implementation of CSR projects, working closely with corporate partners, field teams, implementation organizations, and internal program teams.

The role requires a professional with strong program management capability and experience working directly with CSR clients, ensuring timely delivery, partner coordination, and outcome-oriented program execution.
