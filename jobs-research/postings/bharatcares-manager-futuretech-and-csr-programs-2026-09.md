						About BharatCares

BharatCares (SMEC Trust), the social impact arm of the CSRBOX Group stands tall amongst the Top 50 Social Impact Organizations in India. Specifically established and curated for program delivery, BharatCares' commitment revolves around the principles of innovation, technology, and scalability, aiming to address societal challenges comprehensively.

Functioning as a social solution bridge, BharatCares facilitates collaboration among communities, innovators, funders and government to implement the best impact solution for the last mile. Our flagship initiatives focus on improving education, employability, rural and healthcare infrastructure, entrepreneurship, environmental sustainability and road safety.

For more information, visit: www.bharatcares.org

Job Position

Manager – FutureTech and CSR Programs

Preferred Location: Ahmedabad, Delhi, Noida, Pune, Bangalore, Mumbai, Lucknow
 Salary: 10.80 LPA to 13.20 LPA (Depending on experience and skillset)

Position Overview

The Manager – FutureTech and CSR Programs will play a pivotal role in designing, implementing, and scaling technology-driven skilling programs. The role demands strong program management, facilitation skills, and relationship-building with academic institutions, industry mentors, and internal teams to create measurable impact.

Key Responsibilities

Program Design & Implementation

- Coordinate with the central team to implement AI,      Coding, Cybersecurity, and Full Stack programs across higher education      institutions.

- Support the design and rollout of faculty      development programs (FDPs), student bootcamps, and project-based learning      modules.

- Collaborate with colleges to integrate FutureTech      modules within academic schedules and credit systems.

Training Delivery & Facilitation

- Facilitate hybrid, classroom, and online sessions      using industry case studies, labs, and assignments.

- Organize student engagement activities including      hackathons, ideathons, and mentoring circles.

- Build capacity of institutional trainers and      mentors to continue program delivery post-deployment.

Academic & Industry Engagement

- Act as a single point of contact for academic      institutions on program planning, communication, and logistics.

- Engage with industry experts, guest faculty, and      practitioners to enrich curriculum and mentorship offerings.

- Represent BharatCares in academic events,      symposiums, and exhibitions to showcase program impact.

Project Coordination & Operations

- Coordinate with trainers, consultants, and internal      teams to ensure seamless multi-location program rollout.

- Maintain real-time trackers on participant      attendance, session completion, and program milestones.

- Oversee administrative aspects including      scheduling, documentation, reporting, and budget compliance.

Monitoring, Reporting & Quality Control

- Develop robust data collection and reporting      mechanisms to capture learning outcomes and program reach.

- Analyze stakeholder feedback to enhance delivery      quality and learner experience.

- Align program activities with frameworks like NSQF,      NEP 2020, and BharatCares’ impact standards.

Content Development & Resource Curation

- Co-develop contextualized content including decks,      toolkits, and digital resources.

- Curate industry-relevant projects and case studies      to ensure practical exposure.

- Keep pace with latest EdTech tools, learning      methodologies, and FutureTech trends.

On-Ground Support & Travel

- Travel to project sites for program facilitation,      quality monitoring, and partner engagement.

- Build institutional readiness and support      capacity-building workshops for long-term sustainability.

Key Requirements & Skills

- Bachelor’s or Master's degree in Engineering,      Computer Science, IT, or related fields; MBA or equivalent degree is a      plus.

- 4–6 years of experience in program management,      academic engagement, training, or CSR project implementation.

- Good technical understanding of AI/ML, Coding,      Cybersecurity, or Full Stack concepts.

- Strong facilitation and public speaking skills;      experience delivering sessions to college students preferred.

- Excellent stakeholder management, problem-solving,      and documentation skills.

- Comfort with data tracking, reporting tools, and      content development.

- Willingness to travel across cities as required.

What You’ll Gain

- Lead impactful, future-ready skilling programs      shaping thousands of learners’ careers.

- Collaborate with industry leaders, faculty, and      social sector partners.

- Grow professionally in project strategy,      facilitation, and stakeholder ecosystems.

- Be part of a mission-driven, collaborative team      dedicated to inclusive growth.
