						About BharatCares

BharatCares (SMEC Trust), the social impact arm of the CSRBOX Group stands tall amongst the Top 50 Social Impact Organizations in India. Specifically established and curated for program delivery, BharatCares' commitment revolves around the principles of innovation, technology, and scalability, aiming to address societal challenges comprehensively.

Functioning as a social solution bridge, BharatCares facilitates collaboration among communities, innovators, funders and government to implement the best impact solution for the last mile. Our flagship initiatives focus on improving education, employability, rural and healthcare infrastructure, entrepreneurship, environmental sustainability and road safety.

Job Position

Manager – Road Safety Programs

Location: Ahmedabad
 No. of Positions: 1
 CTC: INR 7.2 LPA to 8.4 LPA (Depending on the relevant experience)

Role Overview

BharatCares® is looking for a seasoned professional to join as Manager – CSR & Road Safety, based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. The role will lead the design, implementation, and scaling of CSR-funded road safety initiatives in collaboration with corporate partners, government authorities, and ecosystem stakeholders.

The Manager will play a critical role in translating road safety challenges into impact-driven, scalable CSR programs, aligned with national road safety priorities, ESG goals, and public systems strengthening.

Key Responsibilities

1. CSR Program Design & Management (Road Safety Focus)

- Lead end-to-end planning, execution, and monitoring      of CSR-funded road safety programs.

- Design interventions across Education, Engineering,      Enforcement, and Emergency Response (4Es).

- Ensure programs are aligned with corporate CSR      objectives, SDGs, and statutory compliance requirements.

- Manage timelines, budgets, partners, and on-ground      implementation teams.

2. Government Collaboration & Stakeholder Engagement

- Liaise with traffic police, urban local bodies,      transport departments, health departments, and district administrations.

- Support coordination with state and city-level road      safety committees and authorities.

- Represent BharatCares® in government meetings,      reviews, and joint planning sessions.

- Build long-term partnerships with government and      ecosystem stakeholders.

3. Technical Advisory & Solution Design

- Conduct situational assessments, road safety      diagnostics, and need-based analysis.

- Support road safety audits, black spot      identification frameworks, and low-cost engineering solutions.

- Provide advisory on enforcement strengthening,      technology-based interventions, and post-crash response systems.

- Benchmark initiatives against national and global      best practices (MoRTH, WHO, IRC, World Bank, etc.).

4. Monitoring, Evaluation & Reporting

- Define impact metrics and outcome indicators for      road safety programs.

- Track progress, analyze data, and flag      implementation risks or gaps.

- Prepare MIS reports, donor updates, government      submissions, and impact dashboards.

- Document learnings, case studies, and best      practices for scale-up.

5. CSR Proposals & Partner Engagement

- Lead development of concept notes, proposals, and      DPRs for corporate CSR partners.

- Support corporate engagements, presentations, and      reporting reviews.

- Act as a technical and programmatic point of      contact for CSR donors.

6. Team & Operations Support

- Guide and mentor project teams, consultants, and      field staff.

- Support capacity-building workshops, trainings, and      knowledge-sharing sessions.

- Take up strategic and special assignments as      required by leadership.

Required Qualifications & Experience

- Postgraduate degree in Transport Engineering, Civil      Engineering, Urban Planning, Public Policy, Social Work, or Development      Studies.

- 6–8 years of experience in road safety, CSR      programs, urban development, transport planning, or public systems      projects.

- Prior experience working with government      departments, CSR foundations, consulting firms, or multilateral agencies      is highly preferred.

Key Skills & Competencies

- Strong understanding of road safety systems and      public-sector programs.

- Experience in managing CSR-funded or donor-funded      projects.

- Excellent stakeholder management and coordination      skills.

- Strong analytical, documentation, and      proposal-writing abilities.

- Effective communication skills in English and Hindi      (Gujarati is a plus).

- Ability to work in a dynamic, multi-stakeholder      environment.

Reporting & Work Location

- The role will be based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

- Reporting to the designated senior leadership      within BharatCares®.

- Regular travel to project locations and government      offices is expected.
