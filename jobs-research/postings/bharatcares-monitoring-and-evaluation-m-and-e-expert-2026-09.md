Job Position

Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) Expert

Location: Hosur
 No. of Position: 1
 CTC: 6 – 7.8 LPA (Depending on the relevant experience)

About the Position

The Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) Expert will support the monitoring, reporting, and data management of skill development projects implemented by BharatCares at the ground level. The role involves tracking project performance, preparing MIS and donor reports, coordinating with stakeholders, ensuring quality assurance, and maintaining compliance for CSR-funded skilling programs, particularly in technical trades of CNC, Mechatronics, and Electric Vehicles (EV).

The candidate will work closely with implementation teams, clients, donors, and training partners to ensure accurate reporting, effective project monitoring, and timely submission of project updates and analytical reports.

Key Responsibilities

1. Monitoring & Evaluation

- Develop      and maintain monitoring frameworks, trackers, and reporting systems for      skilling projects.

- Monitor      project KPIs including enrolment, attendance, training completion,      certification, and placement outcomes.

- Conduct      regular data validation and quality checks to ensure data accuracy and      reliability.

- Support      impact assessment, beneficiary tracking, and outcome measurement      activities.

- Coordinate      with field teams for timely data collection and reporting.

2. MIS & Data Management

- Prepare      and manage MIS reports, dashboards, and analytical summaries for internal      and external stakeholders.

- Analyse      project data and generate insights for improving program performance.

- Maintain      centralized project databases and ensure timely updates from      implementation teams.

- Track      project progress against targets and highlight gaps or risks.

3. Donor & Client Reporting

- Prepare      donor reports, utilization reports, presentations, and progress updates.

- Develop      narrative and quantitative reports for CSR partners and clients.

- Coordinate      with clients and donors regarding reporting timelines, review meetings,      and data requirements.

- Ensure      timely submission of reports as per donor guidelines and reporting      formats.

4. Stakeholder Coordination

- Work      closely with project managers, trainers, mobilisers, and implementation      partners.

- Participate      in client meetings, monitoring reviews, and stakeholder discussions.

- Support      audits, inspections, and monitoring visits by CSR partners and external      stakeholders.

- Coordinate      with cross-functional teams for smooth project reporting and      documentation.

5. Documentation & Compliance

- Maintain      project documentation including reports, case studies, evidence records,      and trackers.

- Ensure      compliance with donor guidelines, SOPs, and reporting standards.

- Support      preparation of presentations, impact stories, and project documentation      for internal and external use.

6. Quality Assurance

- Ensure      quality standards are maintained across project implementation, reporting,      and documentation processes.

- Conduct      regular reviews of field data, MIS reports, and project deliverables to      identify gaps and inconsistencies.

- Support      implementation teams in improving data quality, reporting accuracy, and      compliance standards.

- Monitor      adherence to donor guidelines, SOPs, and project quality benchmarks.

- Coordinate      periodic audits and internal quality checks to ensure smooth project      execution and reporting efficiency.

Required Qualifications & Experience

- Graduate/Postgraduate      in Management, Statistics, Social Work, Economics, Data Analytics, or      related field.

- Has      experience of skill development projects.

- 3–4      years of experience in Monitoring & Evaluation, MIS, or Reporting      roles within the Skill Development / CSR.

- Strong      experience in donor reporting and client-facing coordination.

- Hands-on      experience in preparing MIS reports, dashboards, and analytical reports.

- Good      understanding of skilling program implementation and reporting structures.

- Experience      in handling large datasets and project tracking systems.

- Proficiency      in MS Excel, Google Sheets, PowerPoint, and reporting tools.

- Willingness      to travel locally as per project requirements.

Desired Skills

- Strong      analytical and problem-solving abilities.

- Excellent      communication and presentation skills.

- Strong      stakeholder management and coordination skills.

- Attention      to detail and data accuracy.

- Ability      to manage multiple reporting timelines simultaneously.

- High      ownership, accountability, and teamwork orientation.

- Commitment      towards social impact and community development.
