About BharatCares

BharatCares (SMEC Trust), the social impact arm of the CSRBOX Group, works on large-scale social development initiatives across education, healthcare, livelihoods, road safety, skilling, sustainability, and community development.

Position Overview

BharatCares is looking for a Project Associate – CSR & Road Safety Programs to support the implementation of road safety and community-focused CSR initiatives in Alwar, Rajasthan.

The role will involve field-level project implementation, community mobilisation, facilitation, stakeholder engagement, MIS management, and reporting. The ideal candidate should be comfortable working extensively in the field and interacting with diverse stakeholders and communities.

Key Responsibilities

1. Project Implementation

- Support the end-to-end implementation of CSR and      road safety activities as per project plans and timelines.

- Coordinate field activities, awareness programs,      workshops, training sessions, and community interventions.

- Ensure smooth execution of activities at the      grassroots level.

- Monitor project activities and flag implementation      challenges to the reporting manager.

2. Community Mobilisation & Facilitation

- Mobilise community members, students, youth,      drivers, institutions, and other target groups for road safety      initiatives.

- Facilitate awareness sessions, workshops, meetings,      and training programs.

- Engage with communities to understand local      requirements and encourage participation.

- Support the delivery of road safety awareness and      behavioural-change activities.

3. Stakeholder Engagement

- Coordinate with schools, colleges, government      departments, local authorities, community representatives, NGOs, and other      project stakeholders.

- Build and maintain positive relationships with      stakeholders at the field level.

- Support meetings, consultations, events, and      project-related coordination.

- Represent the organization professionally during      field engagements.

4. MIS, Data & Reporting

- Maintain accurate records of beneficiaries,      activities, attendance, events, and project outputs.

- Collect, verify, and update field-level data and      MIS.

- Prepare regular activity reports and project      updates.

- Document success stories, photographs, case      studies, and field learnings.

- Ensure timely submission of project reports and      required documentation.

5. Monitoring & Field Coordination

- Conduct regular field visits to monitor project      activities.

- Track progress against project targets and      timelines.

- Coordinate with internal teams and external      stakeholders for smooth execution.

- Identify gaps and support corrective actions.

Key Requirements

- Bachelor's/Master's degree in Social Work,      Development Studies, Rural Development, Management, Sociology, or a      related field.

- 1–3 years of experience in CSR, NGO, social impact,      community development, road safety, or development-sector projects.

- Freshers with strong communication and facilitation      skills may also be considered.

- Excellent communication and facilitation skills.

- Fluency in Hindi and working proficiency in English      is mandatory.

- Experience in community mobilisation and      stakeholder engagement.

- Basic understanding of MIS, data management, and      reporting.

- Good working knowledge of MS Excel, MS Word, Google      Workspace, or similar tools.

- Willingness to undertake frequent field visits and      work with communities.

- Candidates who have experience in road safety,      awareness campaigns, community mobilisation, or behavioural-change      programs will be preferred.
