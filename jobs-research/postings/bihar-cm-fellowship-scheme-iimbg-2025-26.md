Flagship GoB initiative with IIM Bodh Gaya / Bihar Prashasnik Sudhar Mission Society. Two-year embedding in governance system. Bihar domicile only; state reservation rules apply. Application portal closed; final results published through Mar–May 2026 updates on iimbg.ac.in/cmfs/.

Application cycles: Cycle 1 deadline 17 Dec 2025; Cycle 2 31 Dec 2025; Cycle 3 15 Jan 2026; Cycle 4 31 Jan 2026.

Eligibility:
- Domicile of Bihar
- Max age 45 years as of application deadline
- PG from reputed institution in listed fields with min 55%
- Valid CAT/GMAT/GRE/GATE/UGC-NET/CSIR-NET score — OR exemption if Master's from INI / Central University / NLU in listed fields

Experience by office:
- 3+ years: District, Divisional, Municipal Corporation
- 6+ years: Secretariat Department
- 8+ years: Chief Secretary and Development Commissioner Office
- 10+ years: Hon'ble CM and Dy. CM Office

Honorarium: ₹80,000 (district/divisional/municipal) to ₹1,50,000 (CM & Dy CM Secretariat); ₹1,00,000 Secretariat; ₹1,25,000 CS & DC Office.

Structure: 3-week induction at IIM Bodh Gaya + 2 years attachment; quarterly learning-cum-review; PG Certificate in Public Policy and Good Governance + GAD work experience letter. Early quit before 12 months: repay ₹1,50,000 training cost.

Selection: online application → shortlist → online interview → offers. Contact: cmf@iimbg.ac.in
