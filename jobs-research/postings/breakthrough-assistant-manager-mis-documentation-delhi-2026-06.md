Breakthrough is looking for an Assistant Manager – MIS & Documentation for its Programme Team at Delhi. The person will report to the Programme Lead, Delhi.

Key Responsibilities:
- Effective MIS reporting of state level-inputs and outputs for benefit of the Programme implementation.
- Ensure tracking of all planned activities at the state-level.
- Efficient inter-center coordination for delivering planned outputs.
- Conduct data validation, troubleshoot discrepancies and coordinate with field teams for data corrections.
- Support donor reporting by compiling, validating and analysing programme data.
- Manage data backups and ensure secure access to programme information.

Programme Documentation & Reporting
- Draft monthly, quarterly, and annual reports, donor updates, presentations and internal programme briefs.
- Develop high-quality documentation including success stories, case studies, process documentation, and field reports.
- Coordinate with field teams to collect qualitative and quantitative programme updates regularly.
- Document programme implementation processes, learnings, best practices, and challenges.
- Support development of content for newsletters, donor communication, and social media updates.

Specific Knowledge, Skill & Experience Required:
- Graduate in Computer Applications / Database management / Business Management or any other relevant field with at least 5-7 years of experience in MIS, reporting or documentation roles, preferably in the development sector.
- Experience in handling and managing datasets with high attention to accuracy and quality.
- Strong knowledge and hands-on experience in MS Excel (Advanced), MS Word, and other similar applications.
- Experience in report collation, documentation, and donor reporting.
- Excellent written communication skills with the ability to produce clear, concise, and high-quality reports and documentation.
- Ability to manage multiple deadlines and work collaboratively with cross-functional teams.

About Breakthrough: Breakthrough works on culture change by shifting social norms that limit women and girls from reaching their full potential.
