Call For Applications – Employment Opportunities (CCG-NLUD) 2026

The Centre for Communication Governance at National Law University Delhi (CCG) is inviting applications for multiple positions and levels across research, strategy, communication and administration. CCG is a continuously evolving organisation and the members of the Centre are expected to be active participants in building a collaborative, research-based institution and a lasting community of highly motivated researchers. Its work seeks to embed constitutional values and good governance within information technology law and policy and examine the evolution of existing rights frameworks to accommodate new media and emerging technology. The team's ongoing work is on subjects such as — privacy and data governance/protection across the digital realm, platform governance, regulation and the societal impact of emerging technologies like artificial intelligence (AI), internet governance, and digital access and inclusion. The Centre is an active participant in multistakeholder conversations in the country and in global processes as well.

Open Positions

CCG is hiring for roles in: (i) Research, (ii) Strategy and Communications, and (iii) Administration.

(i) Research

- Analyst (0-2 year's experience)
- Project Officer (2+ year's experience)
- Programme Officer (3+ year's experience) Project/Programme/Team Lead/Manager (4+ year's experience)

(ii) Strategy and Communications

- Community and Engagement Associate (2+ year's experience)
- Community and Engagement Officer (4+ year's experience)
- Strategic Development and Partnership Associate (2+ year's experience)
- Strategic Development and Partnership Officer (4+ year's experience)

(iii) Administration

- Administrative and Financial Assistant (2+ year's experience)

A Master's degree from a highly regarded programme or university can count towards work experience, but domain knowledge, experience, and expertise will be regarded as primary. As a young and inclusive, research-led organisation, CCG anticipates that all members of the Centre will have to manage substantive as well as institutional work in addition to research. Highly motivated candidates with a deep commitment to building policies that support and enable constitutional values and rule of law are most welcome. CCG is looking for people who see good academic research and policy designs as a way to build a better and more equitable world.

CCG is looking for individuals with work-style traits that include the ability to work both collaboratively and independently, while being empathetic towards colleagues. At the same time, candidates should also be self-motivated, proactive, creative as well as be capable of driving research when required.

About CCG

The Centre for Communication Governance is an academic research centre at the National Law University Delhi. The Centre aims to ensure that Indian legal education establishments engage more meaningfully with information technology law. It undertakes academic research, provides policy inputs at the domestic and international level, and builds the capacity of key stakeholders as part of its endeavour to embed good governance and constitutional principles in the information technology law and policy domain.
