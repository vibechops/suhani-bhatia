Call for Applications: Research Intern

Tue, 08/25/2026

Organisation: Centre for Civil Society (CCS), New Delhi

Internship Type: Unpaid, full-time internship

Duration: minimum 6 weeks (40 hours per week) or equivalent hours (if part-time)

Start Date: 7th September, 2026 (applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis)

Location: Delhi, CCS office/ Hybrid

We are seeking a motivated intern to support the "Brussels Effect" project. The intern will conduct a comprehensive mapping of existing literature on the Brussels Effect, assist in the conceptualisation of a co-creation lab, and contribute to developing new literature and knowledge in this space. The intern will also support a comparative study of the Brussels Effect in India and Europe, and monitor recent publications and media coverage related to the institutions, and emerging discussions in this sector.

Responsibilities include:

- Support the finalisation of the BEI (Brussels Effect on India) scope and concept note by conducting systematic online research and literature mapping to inform its conceptual framework.

Eligibility and Skills

- Currently enrolled in or a recent graduate of tech policy, social sciences, education, public policy, data science, communications, or related fields.
- Strong qualitative and quantitative research skills with attention to detail.
- Good understanding of the technology policy landscape in India
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.
- Comfortable working with data in spreadsheets (Google Sheets / Excel/Python/Web Scraping).
- Ability to work independently and manage time effectively.
- Comfortable working in a fast paced environment and is diligent

Application Process

Interested candidates should submit their CVs and cover letter. The applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis. Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.
