Research Analyst/Programme Associate — Industrial Sustainability (India)
CEEW
Location: New Delhi
Team: Industrial Sustainability
Reporting to: Programme Associate/Programme Lead

The Industrial Sustainability (IS) team aims to support India’s low-carbon growth aspirations by advancing the adoption of green hydrogen and developing initiatives to accelerate industrial decarbonisation. The IS team is working on several different initiatives that include energy transitions in the oil and gas sector, an assessment of alternative fuels like green hydrogen uptake across difficult-to-decarbonise sectors in industrial applications, developing optimal decarbonisation trajectory for the hard-to-abate sectors, such as, steel and cement, assessing the prospects on carbon capture utilisation and storage (CCUS) in India and characterising the industrial sector value chains to analyse various aspects such as carbon footprint, financial strength, resource requirements, and jobs supported.

The IS team seeks to hire a highly committed candidate to build and expand our multidimensional programmes across a range of technical and policy areas. This role demands high-quality research to provide direction, support, and insights to policy planners.
