Position Summary 

 The Program Associate will serve as the critical, on-ground position of our state-level technical support unit, embedded directly within the District Social Welfare Office (DSWO). The core mandate of this role is to pilot, refine, and ground-truth our solution stack across three priority pillars: administrative data quality, financial planning/utilization, and workforce capacity optimization. The Program Associate will translate state-level strategy into local execution while feeding ground realities back to the state team to iterate the model for scale. This is an action-oriented role requiring a balance of data fluency, program management, public finance understanding, and exceptional stakeholder management skills within government systems. 

 You will report to the Nutrition Project Lead based in Bhubaneswar, work closely with the Odisha team, and have active interactions with other team members across our states for effective project delivery.
﻿
﻿ Roles & Responsibilities 

 Data Integrity and Monitoring Systems 

 
 Data Ground-Truthing : Audit and cross-verify digital dashboard entries (e.g., Poshan Tracker data) with physical registers at the sector and Anganwadi Centre (AWC) levels to identify gaps in data quality.

 Analytical Support : Analyze monthly district progress reports to spot low-performing blocks and present actionable data to the DSWO to drive data-backed administrative reviews.

 Feedback Loops : Design and implement simplified, routine data validation protocols for field functionaries (Supervisors and Child Development Project Officers - CDPOs).

 
 Financial Planning & Budget Utilization 

 
 Expenditure Tracking : Work with the district finance/accounts team to map fund flow bottlenecks and trace delays in scheme utilization (e.g., supplementary nutrition, flexi-funds).

 Actionable Planning : Assist the DSWO office in preparing realistic, data-driven annual and quarterly District Action Plans based on historical spending capacity and actual local needs.

 Compliance and Reporting: Help streamline the documentation and tracking of Utilization Certificates (UCs) to ensure smoother financial cycles.

 
 Personnel Management and Capacity Building 

 
 Skill-to-Role Mapping: Conduct diagnostic assessments of district/block personnel (CDPOs, Supervisors) to map existing competencies against their operational mandates.

 Training Delivery: Co-design and facilitate hyper-localized training modules on data driven decision-making, financial tracking, and operational management.

 On-the-Job Mentorship: Provide hands-on troubleshooting support to block/field-level staff to ensure the solution stack tools are adopted in their daily workflows.
﻿
﻿ Liaison and Knowledge Management 
 
 
 State-District Conduit: Synthesize weekly insights, challenges, and operational bottlenecks from the field and relay them to the state team to inform iterative design.

 Operational Documentation: Document process maps, success stories, and structural hurdles discovered during the pilot phase to create a blueprint for statewide scaling.
﻿
﻿ Education and Experience 
 
 
 1 - 3 years of experience with a Masters degree or equivalent in economics, public policy, social science, computer science, or related fields from a reputed institution.

 Experience of working closely with government systems, public health, or nutrition programs (ICDS/WCD preferred) with demonstrated experience in grass-roots project coordination is highly desirable.

 Experience in field studies, data collection, cleaning and analysis of large and complex data sets. 

 Concrete experience in district level client servicing/government projects would be preferred
﻿
﻿ Required Competencies 
 
 a) Domain Competencies 

 
 Awareness/Understanding of India's nutrition/ECD ecosystem - ICDS/WCD architecture, Poshan Tracker, Anganwadi Centre (AWC) systems, and key indicators (Wasting, Stunting, Underweight, EBF)

 Familiarity with government administrative and public finance systems - budgeting cycles, fund flow mechanics, Utilization Certificates (UCs), and scheme-level expenditure (e.g., supplementary nutrition, flexi-funds)

 Working knowledge of state/district governance structures, including District Social Welfare Office (DSWO), CDPO, and Supervisor-level administrative hierarchies

 
 b) Functional Competencies 

 
 Research & analytical ability to interpret district progress reports and identify low-performing blocks

 Data literacy - cleaning, cross-verifying, and analyzing large/complex datasets (dashboard vs. physical register reconciliation)

 Proficiency in statistical software platforms—specifically Python, R, or STATA—to perform data manipulation, cleaning, and complex statistical analysis is highly preferred. Advanced analytics using Excel/Google Sheets are also welcome 

 Problem-solving/solution design - diagnosing fund-flow bottlenecks, designing simplified data validation protocols, and adapting solutions when standard processes stall

 Communication & documentation - synthesizing field insights into clear reports, documenting process maps and success stories for statewide scaling

 Project execution ability - driving District Action Plans, training modules, managing field stakeholders

 
 c) Behavioural Competencies 

 
 Ownership - acting as the "on-ground" anchor for CEGIS's solution stack with minimal direct supervision

 Learning agility - intellectual curiosity and willingness to stay current with data, measurement, and governance practices

 Collaboration - building trust-based working relationships with DSWO officials, CDPOs, Supervisors, and the state-level team

 Stakeholder sensitivity - navigating government systems with tact, actively listening to and valuing officials' perspectives

 Ability to work in ambiguity - a "builder" mindset comfortable finding alternate pathways when formal administrative processes stall (critical given the pilot/reform nature of the role)
