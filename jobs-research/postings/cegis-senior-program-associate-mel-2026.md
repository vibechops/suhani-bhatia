 3. About the Team / Vertical 

 The Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning (MEL) vertical at CEGIS focuses on strengthening how CEGIS led programmes and solutions are monitored, evaluated, and continuously improved across CEGIS' engagement with multiple departments and states. The team works closely with project and government stakeholders to design and operationalise robust MEL systems, including developing logframes, theories of change, and measurement frameworks that align with programme objectives. The team enables evidence-based decision-making, fosters continuous learning, and supports adaptive improvements in programme design and implementation. 

 Key Workstreams: 

 Design of logframes and results frameworks

 Development of MEL systems and measurement protocols

 Implementation of monitoring and evaluation studies

 Knowledge generation and dissemination

 
 Through this work, the MEL team contributes to improved accountability within the teams, better programme design and delivery across sectors.

 4. Position Summary 

 The Senior Program Associate will support project teams in developing monitoring, evaluation, and learning plans for their respective projects in alignment with the project logframes. As the projects will be involved with departments engaged in frontline service delivery (Nutrition, health, education, ECD, etc.), the work will include field research and engagement. The Senior Program Associate should have demonstrable experience in conducting monitoring and evaluation studies on the ground and have an in-depth understanding of the logistics and methodologies of impact and process assessments of projects. The Senior Program Associate will collaborate closely with the project teams across CEGIS states. 

 5. Roles & Responsibilities 

 A. MEL Design & Framework Development

 Support the design and finalisation of project logframes in collaboration with project teams

 Develop comprehensive and context-appropriate MEL plans integrating quantitative and qualitative approaches

 Design measurement frameworks, indicators, and data collection strategies aligned with project objectives

 Develop MEL protocols, toolkits, and standardised templates for use across projects

 
 B. Research, Data & Analysis

 Support the design and execution of monitoring and evaluation studies, including process and impact assessments

 Develop data collection tools (surveys, interview guides, etc.) and analysis plans

 Analyse primary, secondary, and administrative data using statistical tools (e.g., STATA, R, or equivalent)

 Generate actionable insights and translate data into clear, decision-oriented outputs

 
 C. Project & Field Implementation Support

 Coordinate with field teams and local partners to ensure high-quality data collection and study execution

 Identify and manage partnerships with research agencies or local organisations for MEL activities

 Monitor progress of MEL activities and ensure adherence to timelines, budgets, and quality standards

 
 D. Data Systems & Management

 Manage and maintain project data, ensuring adherence to data quality, security, and governance protocols

 Support integration and use of administrative data systems for ongoing monitoring

 Ensure proper documentation and version control of datasets, tools, and outputs

 
 E. Knowledge, Documentation & Stakeholder Engagement

 Develop high-quality reports, briefs, and presentations for internal and external stakeholders

 Support dissemination of findings to government counterparts and other stakeholders

 Build a strong understanding of government systems and contextual challenges to inform MEL design

 Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to integrate MEL insights into project design and delivery
