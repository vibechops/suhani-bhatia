We Are Hiring!!

Looking for candidates for the position of Deputy Director - Indian Digital Gaming Society (IDGS) to be based out of Delhi & Pune.

Key Responsibility Areas:

1. Member Engagement & Relationship Management
- Build and strengthen relationships with IDGS members and key stakeholders.
- Drive member engagement, retention, and participation across IDGS initiatives.

2. Government & Policy Coordination
- Coordinate with Central and State Governments, industry bodies, and regulatory stakeholders.
- Prepare policy papers, consultation documents, recommendations, and advocacy materials.
- Support policy discussions across the gaming, esports, AVGC-XR, and emerging technology ecosystem.

3. Project & Programme Management
- Lead the execution of strategic projects, workshops, conferences, and industry initiatives.
- Coordinate with industry, academia, startups, and international partners.

4. Business Development & Revenue Support
- Support membership acquisition and sponsorship generation.
- Develop proposals and identify new business and partnership opportunities.

5. Event Management
- Plan and execute conferences, industry events, webinars, networking sessions, and delegations.
- Coordinate with internal and external stakeholders to ensure seamless event execution.

6. Communication & Secretariat Support
- Prepare meeting agendas, minutes, presentations, reports, and official correspondence.
- Provide secretariat support to the Executive Committee and senior leadership.

Qualifications & Experience:
- MBA/PGDM or Master's degree in Management, Public Policy, International Business, Media, Communications, Technology, or a related discipline.
- 8-12 years of relevant experience in industry associations, public policy, government relations, programme management, or the gaming/media/technology sector.

Key Competencies Required
- Strong proposal writing, report preparation, and presentation development skills.
- Understanding of the video gaming, esports, AVGC-XR, digital media, and technology ecosystem will be highly preferred.
- Willingness to travel across India and internationally, as required.

Interested candidates may please share their resume along with current CTC, expected CTC, and notice period to aditi.sharma@cii.in latest by 25th July 2026.
