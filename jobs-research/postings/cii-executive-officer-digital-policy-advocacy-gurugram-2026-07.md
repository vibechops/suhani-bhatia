We Are Hiring!!

Looking for candidates for the position of Executive Officer in our Digital (Policy & Advocacy) vertical to be based out of Gurugram.

Key Responsibility Areas:

1. Policy Research & Advocacy Support
- Support policy and advocacy initiatives through research, policy analysis, and preparation of background notes, concept papers, industry recommendations, and consultation responses.
- Consolidate inputs and recommendations from industry members for policy submissions and build consensus through stakeholder consultations.
- Track policy and regulatory developments from TRAI, DoT, TEC, MeitY, and other relevant ministries and share timely updates with stakeholders.
- Coordinate policy consultations, industry-government meetings, and roundtables to facilitate effective advocacy.

2. Industry & Stakeholder Engagement
- Organize conferences, consultations, and policy dissemination events involving industry leaders, government officials, and other key stakeholders.
- Coordinate with industry members, speakers, delegates, partners, sponsors, and government representatives to ensure smooth execution of initiatives.

3. Internal Coordination & Financial Processes
- Coordinate with finance, accounts, and internal teams for invoice generation, payment follow-ups, sponsorship collections, documentation, and financial closure.

4. Documentation & Reporting
- Prepare presentations, concept notes, meeting minutes (MOMs), policy reports, stakeholder databases, and project trackers.
- Maintain accurate documentation and reporting for all ongoing policy initiatives and events.

5. Team Collaboration & Operational Support
- Support the department in day-to-day coordination and execution of multiple policy and advocacy projects.

Qualifications & Experience
- Postgraduate degree in Public Policy, Technology, or related disciplines.
- 4-7 years of relevant experience in policy research, government engagement, consulting, programme coordination, or related domains.
- Exposure to policy analysis, regulatory tracking, and government-industry engagement (preferably in the telecom or digital sector).

Key Competencies Required
- Strong policy research and analytical skills with the ability to interpret regulatory and policy documents.
- Excellent stakeholder management and government engagement capabilities.
- Strong written communication skills for drafting concept notes, policy submissions, and reports.
- Proficiency in MS Excel, PowerPoint, and MS Office applications for analysis, reporting, and presentations.
- Ability to manage structured databases, documentation, and project trackers with high accuracy.

Interested candidates may please share their resume along with current CTC, expected CTC, and notice period to aditi.sharma@cii.in latest by 06th July 2026.

Please mention "Application for Executive Officer - Digital (Policy & Advocacy)" in the subject line.
