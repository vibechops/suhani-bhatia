We Are Hiring!!

Looking for suitable candidates for the position of Executive/Executive Officer in our Financial Sector vertical to be based out of Delhi.

Key Responsibility Areas:

1. Policy Research & Advocacy
- Prepare talking points, presentations, policy papers, reference notes, and briefing materials for senior leadership and industry members.
- Conduct research on financial sector developments and provide policy inputs on key issues.
- Track regulatory developments and announcements from RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, and the Ministry of Finance.

2. Committee & Stakeholder Management
- Independently manage 1-2 Financial Sector Committees under the guidance of the Director/Desk Head.
- Coordinate with offices of CEOs, senior industry leaders, government officials, and regulators.
- Prepare meeting minutes (MOMs), key takeaways, and ensure timely follow-up on action points.

3. Policy Formulation & Industry Engagement
- Support the development of policy recommendations by collecting industry feedback and assisting in policy advocacy initiatives.
- Engage with stakeholders across Banking, NBFCs, HFCs, Insurance, Financial Markets, Fintech, Private Equity & Venture Capital, Financial Inclusion, GCCs, and Investor Bridge.

4. Events & Sponsorship Coordination
- Plan and execute conferences, committee meetings, roundtables, and other financial sector events.
- Support sponsorship generation and develop event-related marketing collateral.

5. Communication & Documentation
- Prepare presentations, policy summaries, reports, committee documentation, and reference materials.

6. Administrative & Operational Support
- Manage day-to-day committee operations, stakeholder communication, and administrative activities.

Qualifications & Experience:
- Postgraduate degree in Economics, Public Policy, Commerce, or related disciplines.
- 3-5 years of relevant experience in the Financial Sector.
- Prior experience in policy research, stakeholder engagement, committee management, financial services, consulting, or industry associations will be an advantage.

Key Competencies Required
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills with strong presentation abilities.
- Strong analytical and research skills with sound knowledge of financial sector developments.
- Good understanding of the regulatory ecosystem, particularly RBI, SEBI, or IRDAI.
- Ability to interact with senior industry leaders, CEOs' offices, and government stakeholders.
- Quick learner with the ability to connect the dots and work effectively under tight deadlines.

Interested candidates may please share their resume along with current CTC, expected CTC, and notice period to deepti.tandon@cii.in latest by 5th September 2026.

Please mention "Application for Executive/Executive Officer - Financial Sector" in the subject line.
