Senior Engagement Manager / Engagement Manager
cKinetics Consulting Services Private Limited
Practice: Sustainability Advisory
Listed: 12 Aug 2026 (Green Jobs India)
Work Location: Okhla, New Delhi; Mumbai; Field travel for client projects

cKinetics is looking for a seasoned Sr/Engagement Manager who is passionate about Sustainability and seeking to mainstream the same in Industrial settings.

The candidate would be part of the Sustainability Industries practice of cKinetics, with a special focus on driving sustainability in manufacturing value chains. The Sustainable Industries practice provides strategic and operational advisory in the areas of resource efficiency, climate goals related action in supply chains, decarbonization, water neutrality, and material and resource circularity to various Indian and global Corporates. The work entails developing insights into environmental sustainability-related issues and framing recommendations on pathways for developing sustainable products, rolling out global best practices of manufacturing, enhancing resource efficiency in manufacturing processes, clean energy transition, circular resource use mechanisms, as also ESG reporting and disclosures.

Requirements (extract):
- Must have relevant 6-10 years of experience in Consulting or Industry related to Sustainability, resource efficiency, circular economy, sustainability target setting, carbon accounting, ESMS frameworks, ESG due diligence and/or ESG governance
- Knowledge of sustainability standards or frameworks such as the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI), Integrated Reporting, etc.
