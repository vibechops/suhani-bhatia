Sr Associate- Global Carbon Markets

Talent Acquisition Specialist

Role: Global Commercial Lead- Events and Sponsorship

cKinetics is hiring a Commercial Lead, Events and Sponsorship (Global) to help build and scale a growing global convenings platform alongside its cCarbon.info market intelligence business. This is a business development, partnerships, and community building role for someone who wants to grow something meaningful at the intersection of climate, markets, policy, finance, and the energy transition.

Through its convenings, cKinetics creates data and modelling driven conversations around the economics, policies, and markets shaping the climate transition. These forums are designed to cut through the noise, bring senior stakeholders together, and support practical, evidence led dialogue on where climate and carbon markets are headed.

Over the last few years, cKinetics has built a strong convenings platform across Canada, the UK, the USA, and India, bringing together senior stakeholders, investors, corporates, policymakers, project developers, and market participants across carbon markets, clean fuels, carbon removals, climate finance, and broader climate transition markets. Flagship convenings such as the Canada Clean Fuels & Climate Markets Summit (CFCM), the Carbon Removal Investment Summit (CRIS) and the India State Climate Investment Forum (ISCIF) have grown significantly, creating trusted spaces for serious dialogue, high value market connections, and forward looking perspectives on the markets supporting the climate transition.

The opportunity now is to take this proven model and scale it into a larger, repeatable, commercially successful global platform. The goal is not only to drive revenue, but also to build a high quality community around some of the most important climate markets.

This is a front facing commercial role. It carries responsibility for commercial partnerships, sponsorships, delegate revenue, strategic relationships, and community engagement across multiple geographies, and for converting high quality convenings into a scalable revenue engine.

 Commercial growth and revenue

Own and grow the revenue line across sponsorship, partnerships, and delegate sales, and build a repeatable commercial motion that scales across events and geographies.

Manage the pipeline from prospecting through signature, shape the sponsorship prospectus and pricing tiers with leadership, and hold pricing discipline in negotiation.

Deliver committed sponsor entitlements with the marketing and delivery teams, secure renewals, and pass qualified leads into cCarbon research subscriptions and advisory.

Partnerships and ecosystem development

Develop and manage a portfolio of partnerships across industry associations, trade bodies, exchanges, registries, media platforms, investors, and adjacent convenings.

Structure agreements with measurable value on both sides, covering audience reach, co-marketing, content exchange, and delegate or client acquisition.

Own the relationship between convening cycles, maintain a view of the ecosystem across Canada, the USA, the UK, and India, and advise on where the platform expands next.
