CLPR engages in strategic impact litigation and legal aid for constitutional rights of persons with disabilities, women, gender and sexual minorities, Dalit and Adivasi persons, and other vulnerable groups. The Litigation Associate will handle and supervise litigation work of CLPR’s Constitution Litigation Unit (CLU).

Full-time, Bangalore. Reports to Executive Director and Litigation Director.

Scope of Work: Meet with clients; draft affidavits, writ petitions, appeals, applications and other pleadings; brief and assist senior advocates; appear in High Courts and Supreme Court as required; conduct legal research for ongoing and prospective cases.

Required: Fluency in Kannada (speaking and reading); undergraduate Law degree and enrollment with a State Bar Council; Master’s in Law desirable; excellent legal research and writing; minimum 2 years’ litigation experience, preferably in High Courts or Supreme Court.

How to Apply: careers.clpr@clpr.org.in with subject “Your Name_Application for Litigation Associate | CLPR 2026”. Attach Cover Letter, Resume, writing sample, at least 1 professional reference.
