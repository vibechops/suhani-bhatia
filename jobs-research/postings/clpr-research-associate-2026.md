The Centre for Law and Policy Research (CLPR) is a not-for-profit trust dedicated to making the Constitution work for everyone through law and policy research, social and governance interventions, and strategic impact litigation. The organisation primarily focuses on addressing discrimination at the intersections of caste, gender, disability, and other minority groups.

Position: Research Associate
Nature of Role: Full-time
Location: Bengaluru or New Delhi
Remuneration: Commensurate with qualifications and experience

The Research Associate will report to the Executive Director and Senior Research Associate(s) and will be responsible for legal research, litigation support, research, advocacy work, and contributing to CLPR’s public outreach initiatives. The role also includes assisting in administrative functions related to research and project execution.

Education & Experience:
- Bachelor’s degree in Law with strong academic credentials
- Postgraduate degree in Law, Public Policy, or related fields
- 0–3 years of experience in legal research, litigation, or policy advocacy
- Prior work on issues relating to Dalit and Adivasi rights, gender justice, disability rights, or minority rights will be an advantage
- Demonstrated commitment to rights-based research and social justice
