The Senior Research Associate will report to the Executive Director. Responsible for legal research, supporting litigation, research, and advocacy work, and contributing to CLPR’s public outreach initiatives, plus allied administrative functions. Full-time; can be based in Bengaluru or New Delhi.

Scope of Work includes compiling and analyzing case law/legislation/policy; doctrinal, comparative, and empirical research on constitutional rights and social justice; drafting reports, academic papers, policy briefs, and blog posts; supporting workshops and consultations; project management; dissemination; stakeholder coordination.

Education & Experience:
- Essential: Bachelor’s degree in Law with strong academic credentials.
- Desirable: Postgraduate degree in Law, Public Policy, or related fields; 4+ years of experience in legal research, litigation, or policy advocacy; prior work on Dalit and Adivasi rights, gender justice, disability rights, or minority rights.

How to Apply: careers.clpr@clpr.org.in with subject “Your Name_Application for Senior Research Associate 2026”. Attach Cover Letter (500–800 words), Resume, One Writing Sample, Two References.
