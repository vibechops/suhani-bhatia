The Chief Minister's Good Governance Associates (CMGGA 2.0) is a flagship fellowship programme of Government of Haryana. Each year, Good Governance Associates are selected through a rigorous national process and placed across all 23 districts of Haryana. They work directly with the district administration and in coordination with the Chief Minister’s Office.

Key Eligibility (2026):

- Age: 28 years or below as on 30 June 2026
- Graduate with 60%+ and at least 1 year of full-time work experience, OR a postgraduate degree/PG diploma from a reputed institution
- Proficiency in Hindi & English
- Full-time commitment required

Duration: 1 year
Stipend: ₹90,000/month + travel expenses (as reported in public announcements)
Applications open 8–23 August 2026. Apply via cmgga.in
