Centre for Migration and Inclusive Development (CMID) is a non-profit organisation registered in India with its core office in Perumbavoor, Kerala. CMID focuses on social inclusion of migrant workers and advocates for and promotes evidence informed policies and programmes that foster migrant inclusive development. Its work ranges from grassroots interventions to advocacy for policy change. International Labour Organisation (ILO), United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), National Health Mission, Aajeevika Bureau, Azim Premji Philanthropic Initiatives, Wipro Foundation, Dasra, Gram Vikas, ESAF, Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals, Cochin Shipyard Limited, Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited, etc. are some of CMID’s recent collaborators. CMID implements various projects in Ernakulam district, Kerala which is the commercial capital of Kerala with the largest concentration of interstate migrant workers in Kerala.

The Partnership for Decent Work in Plantations (PDWP) project seeks to strengthen labour standards, promote responsible business practices, and improve the working and living conditions of plantation workers in the tea estates of Kerala and Tamil Nadu. Through collaboration with tea estate management, workers' representatives, government agencies, and other key stakeholders, the project aims to support sustainable remediation measures, enhance workers' welfare, and foster compliance with national labour laws and internationally recognized standards. The project places a strong emphasis on worker participation, capacity building, and the development of systems that contribute to long-term improvements in labour practices and social sustainability.
