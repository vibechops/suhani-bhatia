Job Opportunity at Crisil Research - Analyst- ESG project
Position: Financial Analyst / Analyst - ESG project
Job type: Full Time, Permanent
Experience: 1 - 5 years

The role involves supporting S&P analysts, based out of North America, EMEA and Asia Pacific regions, on ESG research engagements, including:
- Assess core ESG risks faced by the industries and evaluate performance along the key benchmarks for companies globally
- Perform holistic analysis to support development, testing, and implementation of analytical framework.
- Assess ESG scores for entities basis the analytical framework and support on drafting publishable reports detailing key driving ESG factors
- Track and analyse ESG trends, issues and their implications on various sectors/ industries
- Search, procure and process information about global companies using multiple methods, techniques and sources
- Support on ESG thought leadership pieces

Requirements:
- Prior knowledge of ESG research mandatory
- Prior exposure to the conceptualizing projects involving ESG-based screening, ESG controversies for companies and drafting reports
- Excellent communication skills and client relationship skills
- Attention to detail, ability to stretch and learn, high analytical rigor
- Good problem solving skills
- Prior experience in guiding and mentoring junior team members will be advantageous
- VBA preferred; MS Office
- MBA(Finance), CA or CFA (US) preferred but not essential
