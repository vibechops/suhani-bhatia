ESG Research Support
CRISIL
Location: Mumbai
Experience: 1-3 Years
Last Updated (Foundit): 28-04-2026

Job Description
- Contribute to research report writing and summarizing peer research on thematic topics related to investment strategy for ESG investors.
- Develop and maintain Excel models to evaluate fund ownership for global companies, sectors, and regions.
- Create, update, and maintain comprehensive Excel databases on ESG data.
- Perform stringent data quality checks on third-party ESG data using financial databases and company filings. Ensure robustness of data used for modeling purposes.
- Help the team in running back-tests of ESG-related strategies and apply quantitative techniques to analyze historical and future trends.
- Assist in analyzing macro-level ESG trends to write thematic reports.
- Assist the onshore team on a day-to-day basis and handle ad-hoc requests.

Key Skills:
- Proactive candidate with advanced-level MS Excel skills and report writing experience.
- Experience with VBA macros/Python is an advantage.
- Preferably experienced with Datastream Eikon, Bloomberg Finance LP, or similar financial databases.
- Knowledge of basic finance, including valuations, financial ratios, and extracting data from company filings.
