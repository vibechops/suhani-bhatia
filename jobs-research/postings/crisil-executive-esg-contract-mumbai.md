Executive- ESG (On Contract)
Organization: CRISIL
Location: Mumbai
Experience: 0-1 Years
Last Updated (aggregator): 28-04-2026

Job Description:
- Research, write, and manage production of sustainability disclosures including the CRISIL Sustainability Report, BRSR Report, GRI, SDG and other frameworks
- Monitor sustainability trends and assess their implications.
- Work with Central Administration and location teams to strengthen climate change policy and coordinate efforts to measure, reduce and mitigate carbon emissions
- Organize and manage Sustainability and ESG materiality assessments and stakeholder engagement reviews
- Research in evolving ESG reporting and measurement standards for adoption by CRISIL and subsidiaries
- Support in devising, validating, and monitoring ESG strategy and initiatives for emission reduction, resources, DEI and governance
- Managing the ESG assurance process
- Communication, training and awareness on ESG within the organisation
- Participation in developing sustainable Supply Chain Framework
- Formulation of policies, procedures, and charters on ESG for the business

Education Requirements:
- PG Diploma in Environmental & Sustainability Management

Required Skillsets:
- Experience of upto 1 year or training/ internship in Sustainability teams and involvement in reporting, analysis, ESG program design in a large corporate environment
- Knowledge of initiatives such as the SBTi, CDP, GRI, BRSR, Sustainable Development Goals and the Value Reporting Foundation
- Knowledge of ESG ratings and indices
- High proficiency in Word and PowerPoint
- Demonstrated project management experience
- Able to work in a fast-paced environment
- Significant understanding of ESG thematics
