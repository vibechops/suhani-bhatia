The Centre for Social and Behaviour Change is hiring a full-time Strategic Communications intern!

The role sits at the intersection of behavioural science and communication: taking research on how people make decisions about health, finances, and family, and translating it into material that audiences read, watch, and act on. We are looking for a candidate with copywriting, visual design, and video editing skills. The intern will contribute to writing, design, and the broader work of bringing behavioural research to the people it is intended to reach.

The position is structured with growth in mind, and hence is well-suited to a recent graduate seeking opportunities.
