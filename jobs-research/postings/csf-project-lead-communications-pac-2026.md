About Central Square Foundation (CSF) 

Established in 2012, 
Central Square Foundation (CSF)
 is a non-profit organisation dedicated to enhancing the learning outcomes of all schoolgoing children across India through system-led reforms. As a catalytic enabler, our initiatives span innovation and policy to practice and focus on learning impact at scale across 16 states.

At the heart of our mission is our strategic focus on 
Foundational Literacy and Numeracy (FLN)
, which drives our collaborative efforts with nine state governments to design and execute statewide FLN missions. We are deeply committed to 
Early Childhood Education (ECE)
 reforms, recognising the critical role it plays in laying a strong groundwork for children’s learning journey, thereby augmenting FLN outcomes. Additionally, we prioritise 
EdTech
 integration, aiming to deploy digital solutions for in-class and at-home learning and establish evidence-based practices while also tapping into the potential of 
Artificial Intelligence (AI)
 to augment learning outcomes. We also work in the domain of 
School Governance
, extending our system-level approach to children in both private and government schools, to strengthen the country’s education landscape and 
High Potential Students
 to support India’s bright young minds from underprivileged backgrounds in becoming future innovators, researchers and leaders.

Read more about our work here: 
https://www.centralsquarefoundation.org/

About Policy and Communications at CSF

The 
Policy and Communications (PAC)
 team at CSF comprises two closely connected sub-verticals, Policy and Communications, that work together to strengthen CSF’s influence, visibility and positioning in the education ecosystem. The 
Policy team
 drives salience around CSF’s areas of work, with a particular focus on foundational learning and the NIPUN Bharat Mission, by engaging and informing key stakeholders including bureaucrats, political influencers and state and central government decision-makers on the policy landscape and priorities for quality education for every child. The 
Communications team
 builds thought leadership across CSF’s FIVE focus areas of Foundational Literacy & Numeracy; Early Childhood Education; EdTech & AI; School Governance & High Potential Students through campaigns, strategic partnerships, content collaborations, research and knowledge assets, digital activations across social media and the website and strategic storytelling. The team also leads events, brand building and CSF’s overall positioning, with the aim of establishing CSF as an intellectually rigorous, evidence-backed organisation that contributes meaningfully to strengthening India’s education system.
