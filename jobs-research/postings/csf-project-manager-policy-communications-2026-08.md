Project Manager (2-5 years)
Team: Policy and Communications
Location: Delhi

About Central Square Foundation (CSF)
Established in 2012, Central Square Foundation (CSF) is a non-profit organisation dedicated to enhancing the learning outcomes of all school-going children across India through system-led reforms. As a catalytic enabler, our initiatives span innovation and policy to practice and focus on learning impact at scale across 14 states.
At the heart of our mission is our strategic focus on Foundational Literacy and Numeracy (FLN), which drives our collaborative efforts with 11 state governments to design and execute statewide FLN missions. We are deeply committed to Early Childhood Education (ECE) reforms. Additionally, we prioritise EdTech integration and High Potential Students to support India’s bright young minds from underprivileged backgrounds.

About the Policy and Communications Team (PAC)
At CSF, PAC’s role is to build salience around CSF’s key thematic areas and strengthen the organisation’s position as a thought-leader in India’s school education sector. The PAC team operates horizontally, closely collaborating with various program teams at CSF. The core workstreams of PAC encompass Salience and Communications.

The ‘Project Manager - Policy and Communications’ will primarily work in the Salience workstream. They will work across three pillars including Engagement and Outreach and Strategic Salience and Surround Sound: Identifying opportunities to build salience for CSF’s thematic areas, building strategic campaigns to build surround sound, and drafting thought pieces and articles in English and Hindi.
They will report to, and work closely with the ‘Senior Project-Lead - Policy and Communications’.

Key Responsibilities of the Project Manager - Policy and Communications
Strategic Communications and Thought Leadership (as listed in posting): support salience campaigns, stakeholder engagement, and drafting thought pieces and articles in English and Hindi.

Required Qualifications, Skills and Abilities
- 2-5 years of work experience, preferably in the policy research/ governance sector, with at least 3 years working directly on public policy issues
- Exceptional written and verbal communication skills in both English and Hindi, with the ability to translate complex ideas

Start date: As soon as possible
