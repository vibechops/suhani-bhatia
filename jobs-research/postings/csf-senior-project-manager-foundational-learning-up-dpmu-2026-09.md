About Central Square Foundation (CSF)
Founded in 2012, Central Square Foundation is a non-profit philanthropic foundation working with the vision of ensuring quality school education for all children in India. CSF has a mission focus on ensuring high-quality foundational learning for all children in India. To achieve this, we partner with individuals and social impact organizations to bring innovative solutions in education to build the capacity of the government to drive systemic impact. To know more about us and our work, visit www.centralsquarefoundation.org.
About the District Project Management Unit (DPMU)
The Foundational Learning UP DPMU team strengthens NIPUN Mission Implementation at the district level, by providing governance and academic support to the key stakeholders at district and block level. The DPMU unit of 4-5 Project Managers led by a District Lead works with administrative and academic leaders in the district to build mission salience, set short term and mid term goals, build capacity, and undertake micro-innovations that assist student learning.
The District Lead will lead the implementation of the project at the district level and will be responsible for managing a team. The key responsibilities are as follows:
Key responsibilities
:
Lead district strategy and planning by developing annual, quarterly and monthly implementation plans aligned with organisational priorities and government goals, while identifying district-specific challenges and designing contextually relevant solutions.
Build and maintain strong partnerships with key district and block-level government stakeholders, including the District Magistrate, Chief Development Officer, Basic Shiksha Adhikari, DIET Principal, Block Education Officers and other relevant officials, positioning CSF as a trusted strategic partner.
Drive high-quality project implementation by providing academic, governance and monitoring oversight across all district interventions, ensuring timely delivery of project milestones and achievement of intended outcomes.
Provide strategic leadership by proactively identifying implementation bottlenecks, designing corrective actions, advocating with relevant stakeholders and ensuring effective execution of priority initiatives.
Strengthen district education systems by providing technical guidance and support to institutionalise effective review mechanisms, governance forums and monitoring processes that improve decision-making and accountability.
Lead and develop a high-performing team by setting clear expectations, providing regular coaching and feedback, fostering collaboration, and building the capabilities of team members to deliver high-quality work.
Use data to drive decisions by reviewing programme data, analysing trends, monitoring key performance indicators and translating insights into actionable recommendations for government stakeholders and internal teams.
Ensure quality and operational excellence across all district activities, including field implementation, stakeholder engagements, reporting, documentation, presentations and review processes.
Represent CSF in district forums by facilitating meetings, presenting evidence-based recommendations, supporting government reviews and influencing decisions that strengthen foundational learning outcomes.
Capture and scale good practices by documenting innovations, synthesising field learnings, sharing evidence with internal and external stakeholders, and contributing to continuous improvement of programme design and implementation.
Collaborate closely with state and cross-functional teams to ensure alignment with organisational priorities, facilitate knowledge sharing and support the successful implementation of state-wide initiatives.
