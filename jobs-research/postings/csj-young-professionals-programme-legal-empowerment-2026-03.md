Young Professionals Programme 2026 at Centre for Social Justice | Stipend ₹45,000/Month

About the Programme

The Young Professionals Programme for Legal Empowerment (YPPLE) 2026 is a 2-year immersive programme designed for young professionals interested in social justice, legal empowerment, and grassroots legal work.

Participants will work closely with paralegals, lawyers, and communities, and will gain real-world experience in legal empowerment and social justice initiatives.

What the Programme Offers

The programme includes:

- Hands-on grassroots engagement and field travel
- Mentorship from social justice lawyers and leaders
- Opportunities for research, collaboration, and capacity building
- Exposure to legal empowerment projects and community justice work
- Multidisciplinary learning environment

Eligibility

Applicants may include:

- Graduates or Postgraduates from Law, Management, Communications, Design, Art, Journalism, or other disciplines
- Individuals interested in social justice and grassroots legal work
- Candidates willing to travel extensively and work in field conditions

Duration: 2 Years

Stipend: ₹45,000 per month

Last Date to Apply: 31 March 2026
