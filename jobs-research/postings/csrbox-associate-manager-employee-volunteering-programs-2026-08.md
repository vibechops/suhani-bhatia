CSRBOX is India’s leading CSR knowledge and impact intelligence-driven media cum social impact advisory platform. It works as an enabler to create corporate-non-profit, corporate-corporate, and corporate-government collaborations.

About the Role

CSRBOX is seeking a dynamic and experienced professional to join our team as Associate Manager – Employee Volunteering Program & Impact Programs. The candidate will be responsible for designing, managing, and scaling employee volunteering initiatives, as well as leading CSR projects across thematic areas.

This role demands strong project management, stakeholder engagement, and strategic thinking skills to deliver impactful programs in partnership with corporates, NGOs, and government bodies.

Key Responsibilities

1. CSR Strategy & Implementation

- Co-develop      and execute CSR strategies aligned with corporate partners’ goals and CSR      mandates.

- Design      and manage impactful projects across thematic areas such as education,      employability, healthcare, environment, and technology.

- Monitor,      evaluate, and report on CSR project impact and outcomes.

2. Employee Volunteering Programs (EVP)

- Design      innovative EVP frameworks (annual/multi-year programs, skill-based      volunteering, hybrid/virtual formats).

- Organize      high-impact volunteering events and activities with corporates and      community partners.

- Track      and report volunteer participation, hours, and measurable social impact.

- Enhance      program effectiveness through regular feedback and adoption of best      practices.

3. Strategic Growth & Partnerships

- Build      and sustain partnerships with corporates, foundations, NGOs, academic      institutions, and government bodies.

- Identify      and secure new business opportunities, funding sources, and      collaborations.

- Represent      CSRBOX at industry forums, events, and conferences to expand networks and      visibility.

- Develop      compelling proposals, pitches, and presentations for CSR and EVP      initiatives.

4. Project Management & Coordination

- Lead      cross-functional teams to ensure smooth delivery of projects.

- Manage      project timelines, budgets, and resources to meet client commitments.

- Foster      collaboration across teams to align execution with CSRBOX’s growth      strategies.

5. Communication & Reporting

- Prepare      impact reports, case studies, and presentations for clients and internal      stakeholders.

- Develop      communication materials to showcase CSR and EVP initiatives.

- Work      with communication teams to ensure external visibility and impactful      storytelling.

6. Budget & Resource Management

- Support      in preparing and managing project budgets.

- Ensure      optimal utilization of resources across multiple CSR and EVP initiatives.

Qualifications & Skills

- Bachelor’s/Master’s      degree in Social Work, Development Studies, Management, or related field.

- 3–6      years of experience in Employee Volunteering Programs (EVP), CSR project      management, or corporate partnerships.

- Strong      expertise in project management, stakeholder engagement, and CSR program      design.

- Proven      experience in client-facing roles with ability to build and sustain      partnerships.

- Excellent      communication, presentation, and proposal-writing skills.

- Ability      to innovate, multitask, and manage projects in fast-paced environments.

- Proficiency      with MS Office, Google Suite, and project management tools.

What We Offer

- Opportunity      to work on diverse, high-impact CSR and EVP programs with leading      corporates and non-profits.

- Exposure      to innovative models of employee engagement and CSR strategy.
