About CSRBOX

CSRBOX is India’s leading CSR knowledge and impact intelligence-driven media cum social impact advisory platform. It works as an enabler to create corporate-non-profit, corporate-corporate and corporate-government collaborations.

CSRBOX’s research and consulting team maps over 4,000 CSR projects and 1,500 companies’ CSR portfolios every year. Its CSR implementation vertical, BharatCares, works with companies and CSR foundations on projects for underprivileged communities across livelihoods, skill development, education, health and environment.

Corporate clients include ICICI Bank, SKF Ltd., Bosch India, IBM India, Diageo, DP World, Future Generali, Airbus, Arvind, LG Electronics, Hero MotoCorp, Sandvik, Vastu Housing Finance, DCM Shriram and L&T, among others.

Position Overview

CSRBOX is looking for a Manager – Social Impact Solutions with strong consulting experience and a proven track record in social impact, CSR, program strategy and stakeholder management.

The role involves working with corporate and institutional clients to understand their social impact priorities, develop customized solutions, manage consulting engagements, and support the design and execution of impactful CSR and development programs.

The ideal candidate should be able to translate complex social challenges into practical, scalable and measurable solutions while managing multiple stakeholders and delivering high-quality outputs.

Roles and Responsibilities

1. Social Impact Consulting & Strategy

- Lead      consulting assignments related to CSR, ESG, sustainability and social      impact.

- Understand      client requirements and translate them into customized social impact      solutions.

- Conduct      sector research, needs assessments and landscape analysis.

- Develop      CSR strategies, program frameworks, implementation plans and impact      roadmaps.

- Provide      strategic recommendations based on research, data and stakeholder      insights.

2. Client & Stakeholder Management

- Manage      relationships with corporate clients, foundations, government departments,      NGOs and implementation partners.

- Lead      client meetings, presentations, workshops and review discussions.

- Understand      client expectations and ensure timely delivery of consulting assignments.

- Coordinate      with internal and external stakeholders to ensure smooth project      execution.

- Build      long-term partnerships and identify opportunities for further engagement.

3. Proposal Development & Business Support

- Develop      concept notes, proposals, technical presentations, project plans and      consulting reports.

- Support      business development through research, solution design and client      presentations.

- Identify      emerging opportunities in CSR, ESG, sustainability and social development.

- Contribute      to the preparation of budgets, timelines and implementation frameworks.

- Support      the development of case studies, knowledge products and thought leadership      content.

4. Program Design & Implementation Support

- Design      and review social impact programs across sectors such as education,      livelihoods, healthcare, environment, skilling and community development.

- Develop      monitoring frameworks, KPIs and impact measurement approaches.

- Support      implementation partners in program planning and delivery.

- Review      project progress, identify challenges and recommend corrective actions.

- Ensure      projects are aligned with client objectives and organizational standards.

5. Research, Monitoring & Reporting

- Conduct      primary and secondary research on social impact themes and development      priorities.

- Analyze      program data and prepare insights for decision-making.

- Develop      impact reports, dashboards, presentations and client updates.

- Track      project milestones, deliverables and outcomes.

- Support      impact assessments, evaluations and documentation of best practices.

6. Team Management & Knowledge Sharing

- Mentor      and guide team members working on consulting and social impact      assignments.

- Allocate      tasks, monitor deliverables and ensure quality and timely completion.

- Coordinate      with cross-functional teams including research, program implementation,      communications and operations.

- Build      internal knowledge resources and share sector insights with the team.
