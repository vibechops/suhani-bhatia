Job Position

Sr. Associate – Public Infrastructure

Location: Ahmedabad, Guwahati
 Salary: INR 3.50 LPA to 5.40 LPA (Depending on Experience)

Role Overview

The Senior Associate – Public Infrastructure will be responsible for supporting and independently coordinating the planning, execution, monitoring, and quality management of CSR-supported public infrastructure and civil works projects across multiple locations.

The role will involve working on projects such as:

- Renovation      and upgradation of government schools and healthcare facilities

- Construction      and improvement of WASH infrastructure

- Check      dams and water conservation structures

- Community      infrastructure and public utility projects

- Climate-resilient      and sustainability-linked civil infrastructure

The ideal candidate should have strong civil engineering and project execution knowledge, hands-on site experience, and the ability to coordinate effectively with contractors, vendors, government stakeholders, implementation partners, and CSR teams.

Key Responsibilities

1. Public Infrastructure Project Management

- Lead      and coordinate execution of civil infrastructure projects from planning      through completion.

- Support      assessment of project sites and identify technical and infrastructure      requirements.

- Review      and support preparation of drawings, BOQs, cost estimates, technical      specifications, and project plans.

- Develop      and monitor project execution schedules, milestones, and deliverables.

- Ensure      projects are delivered within approved scope, timelines, quality      standards, and budgets.

2. Site Execution & Quality Management

- Conduct      regular site visits to monitor project progress and execution quality.

- Ensure      construction activities adhere to approved drawings, BOQs, specifications,      and applicable standards.

- Monitor      workmanship, material quality, measurements, and construction practices.

- Identify      technical issues and coordinate timely corrective actions.

- Ensure      appropriate health, safety, and environmental practices at project sites.

- Review      contractor bills, measurement records, work completion reports, and      supporting documentation.

3. Contractor & Stakeholder Coordination

Coordinate closely with:

- Civil      contractors and vendors

- Field      engineers and supervisors

- Implementation      partners

- Government      departments and local authorities

- CSR      teams and corporate stakeholders

- Community      representatives

Evaluate contractor performance and support resolution of execution-related issues.

Facilitate coordination between technical teams, project teams, and stakeholders to ensure smooth implementation.

4. WASH, Water & Sustainable Infrastructure

- Support      implementation of WASH infrastructure in schools, healthcare facilities,      and communities.

- Coordinate      execution of water conservation structures such as check dams, recharge      structures, and related interventions.

- Promote      practical and sustainable approaches to water management and      infrastructure development.

- Support      integration of climate-resilient, resource-efficient, and environmentally      sustainable practices into projects.

5. Project Monitoring & Reporting

- Track      project progress against approved timelines, budgets, and milestones.

- Prepare      periodic progress reports, site visit reports, MIS updates, and project      status reports.

- Maintain      project documentation including drawings, BOQs, measurements, photographs,      approvals, and completion records.

- Develop      project dashboards and highlight risks, delays, quality concerns, and      required interventions to management.

- Support      project audits, technical assessments, and closure processes.

6. Program Design & Proposal Support

- Contribute      technical inputs for the development of CSR infrastructure proposals and      project concepts.

- Conduct      preliminary site assessments and feasibility reviews.

- Support      preparation of project budgets, technical scopes, implementation plans,      and timelines.

- Provide      technical recommendations based on community needs and site conditions.

- Contribute      to developing scalable and sustainable public infrastructure models.

Required Qualifications

- Bachelor’s      degree in Civil Engineering.

- Additional      qualifications in Project Management, Construction Management, Water      Management, or related fields will be an advantage.

Experience Requirements

- 1–3      years of relevant experience in civil infrastructure, construction, public      infrastructure, or development-sector projects.

- Strong      hands-on experience in site execution and project coordination.

- Experience      in school, hospital, community, WASH, water conservation, or other public      infrastructure projects is preferred.

- Experience      managing contractors, vendors, field engineers, and implementation      partners.

- Exposure      to CSR, NGO, government, or development-sector projects will be an      advantage.

Key Competencies

- Civil      project planning and execution

- BOQ,      estimation, and technical documentation

- Site      supervision and quality control

- Contractor      and vendor management

- Project      monitoring and reporting

- Stakeholder      coordination

- Problem-solving      and decision-making

- Strong      written and verbal communication

- Willingness      to travel extensively to project sites

- Ability      to manage multiple projects and locations
