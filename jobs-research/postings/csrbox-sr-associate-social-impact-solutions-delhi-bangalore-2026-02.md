Position Overview:

We are seeking an analytical and detail-oriented Associate - Social Impact Solutions to join our team in Ahmedabad. The successful candidate will assist in data analysis, impact assessment, and the development of innovative solutions to enhance our social impact initiatives. This role requires strong analytical skills, cross-functional coordination, and effective communication.

Roles and Responsibilities:

Data Analysis and Interpretation:

Assist in collecting, organizing, and analyzing data from various sources.

Identify trends, patterns, and key performance indicators.

Utilize data analysis tools and techniques to support decision-making processes.

2. Impact Assessment Support:

Collaborate with team members to evaluate the effectiveness of social impact initiatives.

Contribute to the preparation of recommendations based on data-driven insights.

3. Cross-Functional Coordination:

Work closely with departments such as marketing, operations, and finance to gather relevant data.

Participate in meetings and discussions, providing analytical input to support strategies.

4. Solution Development Assistance:

Assist in the design and development of solutions to address social impact challenges.

Contribute to the optimization of processes and customer experiences using data insights.

5. Reporting and Documentation:

Contribute to the creation of reports and dashboards to track performance metrics.

Maintain documentation related to data analysis, insights, and recommendations.

6. Continuous Learning and Improvement:

Stay informed about industry trends and advancements in data analysis techniques.

Suggest improvements to data collection, analysis methodologies, and reporting processes.

7. Communication:

Collaborate with team members to convey analytical findings in a clear and comprehensible manner.

Present insights, progress updates, and findings during meetings and presentations.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Data Science, Economics, Statistics, Social Sciences, Business Administration, or a related field.

1-3 years of experience in data analysis, social impact assessment, or a similar role.

Proficiency in data analysis tools and software (e.g., Excel, SQL, Python, R).

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.

Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

Attention to detail and a commitment to accuracy.

Knowledge of CSR and social impact metrics is a plus.

Can communicate in English, Kannada (for Bangalore location), Marathi (for Mumbai location)

Open for extensive travel
