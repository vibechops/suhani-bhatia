1. Woman Field Coordinator – Snehidhi Network (Puducherry)
Location: Puducherry (Field-based)
Coverage: Puducherry, Villupuram and Tiruvannamalai Districts
Salary: Rs.30,000 per month

Educational Qualification:
- MSW (Community Development) or Postgraduate degree in Women's Studies, Human Rights, Development Management or related social science discipline.
- Fluency in Tamil and good communication skills in English.
- Willingness to travel extensively within the assigned districts.

Roles and Responsibilities:
- Establish and strengthen the Snehidhi Adolescent Girls' SRHR Network in Puducherry, Villupuram and Tiruvannamalai.
- Identify, mobilise and collaborate with NGOs, community-based organisations, educational institutions and government departments.
- Organise adolescent girls' groups and develop young women leaders.
- Conduct awareness programmes on Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights (SRHR), menstrual health, gender equality, child rights, child marriage prevention and gender-based violence.
- Build partnerships with schools, colleges, ICDS, Health Department, Police, Panchayats and other stakeholders.
- Coordinate district-level campaigns, leadership programmes, consultations and advocacy events.
- Train community volunteers, peer educators and Snehidhi leaders.
- Prepare monthly work plans, activity reports, success stories and documentation.
- Collect programme data using digital tools and mobile applications.
- Represent CWDR and Snehidhi Network at district meetings and public events.

Desired Competencies:
- Passion for women's rights and adolescent girls' empowerment.
- Strong networking and community mobilisation skills.
