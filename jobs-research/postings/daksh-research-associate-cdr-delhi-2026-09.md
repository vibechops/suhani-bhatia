DAKSH is a Bengaluru-based civil society organisation working on judicial reforms. It operates at the intersection of data science, public policy, and operations research, with a focus on addressing case pendency in the Indian legal system through quantitative research and empirical legal methods.

DAKSH is inviting applications for the position of Research Associate – Commercial Dispute Resolution in Delhi.

The role involves legal and empirical research on commercial courts, arbitral institutions and sector-specific tribunals, along with research writing, data analysis, stakeholder engagement and project management.

Eligibility Criteria — Applicants must:
- Have 3–4 years of relevant work experience in litigation, arbitration, policy research or a related field.
- Possess an undergraduate law degree (LL.B.) or postgraduate degree (LL.M.).
- Have working knowledge of the functioning of tribunals and specialised adjudicatory bodies in India, including their procedure, jurisdiction and institutional design.
- Have a solid grounding in corporate law and financial regulation, including company law, insolvency and bankruptcy, securities law, competition law and related areas.
- Be able to independently manage research projects, timelines and multiple workstreams.
- Possess strong legal research and evidence-based policy writing skills.
- Have excellent written and verbal communication skills and the ability to engage confidently with judicial, government and industry stakeholders.
- Be comfortable working with data, datasets, court/tribunal statistics and empirical indicators.

Roles and Responsibilities — The Research Associate will:
- Conduct legal and empirical research on commercial courts, arbitral institutions and sector-specific tribunals in India.
- Research corporate and financial laws relevant to commercial dispute resolution.
- Draft policy briefs, research papers, reports and other knowledge products.
- Support project planning, execution, timelines and deliverables.
- Analyse quantitative and qualitative data, including case pendency, disposal rates and tribunal performance metrics.
- Coordinate and represent DAKSH in meetings, consultations and workshops with judicial officers, government officials, regulators and industry stakeholders.
- Contribute to articles, blogs, presentations and other public-facing communication.
