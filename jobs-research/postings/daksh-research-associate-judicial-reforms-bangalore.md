Role Title: Research Associate
Location: Bengaluru
Reporting to: Research Manager
Nature: Full time

DAKSH is a Bengaluru-based civil society organisation doing transformational work to address deep structural challenges in India’s governance and the justice system. Our research, data tools, and policy insights help uncover systemic challenges and shape real-world improvements in how justice is delivered across the country.

Broad objective: To carry out research and advocacy on public institutions in line with DAKSH’s vision.

Responsibilities include institutional engagement with courts, tribunals and governments (about 50%): interviews, field visits, stakeholder consultations, project coordination, cultivating networks with judges, lawyers, government officials, researchers and technology experts.

Other DAKSH work (about 50%): research and writing on access to justice, the workings of the justice system, technology, data, and the impact of procedural and substantive law on court administration; publishing articles, blog posts, and op-eds.

Education: An undergraduate degree in law is a must.

Core experience: At least two years of demonstrable work experience. Minimum one year of litigation experience and good knowledge of the court system.

Competencies: Strong project management; strong research and writing; good interpersonal skills; ability to thrive in dynamic environments and fieldwork; deep passion for judicial reforms. Knowledge of Kannada preferable.
