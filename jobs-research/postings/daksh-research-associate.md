DAKSH is a Bengaluru-based civil society organisation doing transformational work to address deep structural challenges in India’s governance and the justice system. Our research, data tools, and policy insights help uncover systemic challenges and shape real-world improvements in how justice is delivered across the country.

Purpose of the job
To carry out research and advocacy on public institutions in line with DAKSH’s vision.

Responsibilities include:
Conduct interviews, field visits and stakeholder consultations.
Manage tasks and project deliverables.
Cultivate relationships and build networks with judges, lawyers, government officials, researchers and technology experts.
Write op-eds and research outputs.

Education and Experience Guidelines
- An undergraduate degree in law is a must.
- At least two years of demonstrable work experience.
- Minimum one year of litigation experience and good knowledge of the court system.
- Strong project management skills, with the ability to effectively coordinate tasks, manage timelines, and ensure project timelines are met
- Strong research and writing skills
- Good interpersonal skills and ability to work collaboratively
- Capability to thrive in dynamic work environments and to adeptly navigate fieldwork requirements with ease
- Deep passion to make an impact in the field of judicial reforms

How to apply: Interested candidates may apply through the form on dakshindia.org. CV or resume containing educational qualifications and work experience required.
