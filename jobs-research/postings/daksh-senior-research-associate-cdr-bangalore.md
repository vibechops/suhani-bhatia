Organisation: DAKSH
Project: Commercial Dispute Resolution (Commercial Courts & Tribunals)
Location: Bengaluru
Fixed Term: 3 Years
Reporting To: Director - Commercial Disputes

DAKSH works to strengthen public institutions and improve the functioning of courts and tribunals through data-driven research, institutional analysis, and strategic engagement.

DAKSH’s Commercial Dispute Resolution initiative examines how commercial courts and related judicial mechanisms function in practice. It seeks to improve case management, procedural compliance, and institutional capacity to support economic productivity, ease of doing business, and public trust in the justice system.

The Senior Research Associate will lead and support research projects through analysis and stakeholder engagement for the Commercial Dispute Resolution initiative. The role involves identifying systemic challenges in commercial adjudication, developing evidence-based reform proposals, and contributing to institutional capacity-building and policy influence.

Research & Analysis (40%): Lead research on case management hearings, summary judgments, mediation, document admission protocols, and scheduling practices; design methodologies; conduct field research; analyse case timelines and disposal patterns; synthesize actionable insights.

Stakeholder Engagement (30%): Build relationships with judges, court administrators, lawyers, businesses, and industry associations; coordinate workshops and consultations; facilitate dialogue on procedural reform.

Policy Writing & Knowledge Dissemination (20%): Lead policy papers, reports, reform toolkits; draft blogs and opinion pieces; present findings.

Project Coordination & Team Support (10%): Align research with project goals; mentor junior staff; track milestones.

Educational Qualifications: LLB or LLM required. Master’s in Public Policy, Public Administration, Economics, or related field is an advantage.

Core experience: At least 3 to 5 years of demonstrable work experience; strong understanding of India’s commercial litigation and court administration landscape; quantitative and qualitative data skills; excellent communication; experience managing research projects; ability to engage with judges, lawyers, court staff, government officials, and industry representatives.
