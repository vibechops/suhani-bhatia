Job Title: Assistant Manager | Environment | Mumbai | Sustainability
Entity: Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu India LLP
Job requisition ID: 106254

As an Assistant Manager in Deloitte’s SRT Sustainability team, you will support and lead components of social impact assessments, due diligence, and lender advisory assignments across sectors. The role will focus on baseline data collection, stakeholder engagement, social risk assessment, and compliance assessment against international standards including IFC Performance Standards, ADB Safeguards, and EHS Guidelines as well as national regulations.

Responsibilities:
- Lead and support social baseline studies, including primary surveys, stakeholder consultations, and field data collection.
- Contribute to and manage sections of Environmental & Social Impact Assessments (ESIA) focusing on social risks and impacts.
- Execute Environmental & Social Due Diligence (ESDD) assignments for lender and investor clients.
- Conduct gap assessments against IFC Performance Standards (esp. PS1, PS5), ADB Safeguards, and World Bank Group EHS Guidelines.
- Assist in impact investing evaluations, ESG screening, and social risk profiling.

Key skills required:
- Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Social Sciences, Sociology, Development Studies, Anthropology, or related field.
- 2–4 years of experience in social impact, ESIA/ESDD, or sustainability consulting.
- Experience working on lender-funded projects, DFIs, or impact investing assignments preferred.
- Familiarity with ESG frameworks and sustainability reporting.
