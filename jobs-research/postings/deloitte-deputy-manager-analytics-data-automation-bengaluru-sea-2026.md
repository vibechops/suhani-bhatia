Your work profile 
Build the data foundation that powers AI, automation, audit analytics, reporting, risk insights, and evidence-driven decision making.
 
Looking for a hands-on data and analytics consultant with strong expertise in AI Agentic, Automation, SQL, Python, Databricks, Snowflake, data manipulation, and analytics engineering. The ideal candidate can source and onboard data, build scalable datasets to be used in Databricks Genie or Snowflake Cortex, develop AI Agentic/Analytical solutions, and generate actionable automation for auditors and leadership teams. Prior experience in Technology, AI/ML, Audit, Risk,, or regulated environments is preferred. The candidate should combine strong technical skills with a consulting mindset, attention to detail, problem-solving ability, and the confidence to engage directly with business stakeholders
 
Ideal Background: 
·    7+ years in AI/ML, Automation, Data Strategy, Analytics, Risk Analytics, Data Governance, or Business Intelligence
·    Experience working with large enterprise datasets
·    Familiar with audit and control environments
·    Strong understanding of data lineage, governance, and authority-at-source principles
 
 
Key skills required:  
·      Expert-level proficiency in SQL.
·      Expert-level proficiency in Python.
·      Hands-on experience with Databricks.
·      Hands-on experience with Snowflake.
·      Strong knowledge of data modeling.
·      Strong knowledge of data quality.
·      Experience in metadata management.
·      Experience in analytics engineering.
