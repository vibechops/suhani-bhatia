## About the role

Deloitte is hiring a Deputy Manager for its Regulatory practice, covering corporate laws, sectoral laws, People laws, M&A, compliance and public policy.

In the firm's own words, it is looking for a Regulatory and Compliance Specialist with strong experience in corporate laws, regulatory compliance and transactional support. The role involves providing advisory and compliance support under the Companies Act, FEMA regulations and the Labour Codes, while ensuring adherence to evolving regulatory requirements. The selected candidate will also support transaction related activities, including due diligence and drafting of legal documentation.

## Key responsibilities

- Provide end to end regulatory advisory and compliance support under the Companies Act, FEMA regulations and Labour Codes.
- Ensure timely compliance with applicable corporate and regulatory requirements.
- Conduct and manage legal and regulatory due diligence for transactions, investments and restructuring activities.
- Draft, review and negotiate transaction documents, agreements and other commercial contracts.
- Support clients and stakeholders on regulatory matters and risk mitigation strategies.
- Monitor regulatory developments and advise on their impact on business operations.
- Liaise with government authorities and regulatory bodies for approvals, filings, registrations and clarifications (preferred).

## Eligibility and key skills required

- Qualified Company Secretary (CS) is mandatory.
- An LLB qualification will be an added advantage.
- Relevant experience in corporate law, FEMA compliances, regulatory advisory and transactional support.
- Strong drafting, analytical and stakeholder management skills.
- Ability to manage multiple assignments and work in a dynamic environment.

The official posting does not specify a minimum number of years of experience.
