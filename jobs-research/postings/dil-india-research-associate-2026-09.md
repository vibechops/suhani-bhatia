 About the Trust 

 The University of Chicago Trust (the Trust) is a charitable trust in India, established in 2008 by an alumnus of The University of Chicago. The Trust’s objectives are to provide opportunities for grants to support educational programs, research programs and activities for the betterment of India.  

 The Development Innovation Lab – India (DIL-India) 

 DIL-India, based at the Trust in India, is dedicated to using the tools of economics to benefit millions of people. We work across sectors like education, health, agriculture, climate change, road safety, and others. We do so by partnering with government agencies, firms, and non-profits in India to identify, test, and scale evidence-based solutions. DIL-India has a fast-growing portfolio of policy relevant research. For example, DIL-India is working closely with the Ministry of Jal Shakti and other partners to identify and test safe water solutions that could be deployed at scale. Our estimates suggest that safe water solutions could save the lives of up to 136,000 children annually in India. DIL-India is also working with the Government of Andhra Pradesh to evaluate the impact of personalized adaptive learning on student outcomes in secondary schools across the state.  

 Position description 

 DIL-India is seeking full-time Research Associates to collaborate on new and ongoing research in development economics. Successful applicants will collaborate with Principal Investigators (PIs) at the Development Innovation Lab (DIL) at the University of Chicago, including Professor Michael Kremer, Nobel laureate, and DIL Faculty Director. The ideal candidate possesses a mix of economics, analytical, interpersonal and communications skills, and is passionate about conducting research in development economics.  

 Responsibilities 

 Supports the design and implementation of experiments under the direction of the PI. 
 Design and refine data collection instruments as well as data collection and monitoring protocols aligned with the research design and desired outcome measurements. 
 Leads field research activities including training and overseeing the work of research support personnel. 
 Codes high-frequency checks and analyzes data from field activities. 
 Manage relationships with collaborating organizations 
 Conducts literature reviews. 
 Support in writing policy memos, technical reports, and academic manuscripts summarizing research findings. 
 Presents research findings at staff meetings, seminars, and scientific conferences. 
 Assists with building databases. 
 Maintains technical and organizational support for a research project. 
 Perform other related duties as assigned by the PI or senior management.

 Education A master’s degree in Economics, Public Policy, Environment Policy, Engineering, Science, or another related field.  
 Will consider a bachelor’s degree in a related field with at least one year of work experience.  
 Experience 
 At least one year of experience, which can be a combination of graduate education and work experience.   
 
 Competencies Excellent management and organizational skills along with strong quantitative skills. 
 Flexible, self-motivating, able to manage multiple tasks efficiently, and a team player. 
 Strong written, oral, and interpersonal communication, and presentation skills. 
 Excellent data management, cleaning, and analysis skills.  
 Familiarity with Stata/R is required and experience with Survey CTO is preferred. 
 Strong demonstrated interest in the development sector. 
 Fluency in both English and Hindi. Fluency (at least spoken) in other Indian languages like Telugu and Odia is preferred for the roles based in Bhubhaneshwar and Vijayawada. 
 Knowledge of impact evaluations, particularly randomized controlled trials is preferred.

 Demonstrated ability to independently manage components of field research projects, including research design, data management, and analysis is preferred. 
 
 Working conditions Positions are based in Delhi, Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Bhubaneswar or other project sites.
