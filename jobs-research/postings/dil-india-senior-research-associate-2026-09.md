 About the Trust 

 The University of Chicago Trust (the Trust) is a charitable trust in India, established in 2008 by an alumnus of The University of Chicago. The Trust’s objectives are to provide opportunities for grants to support educational programs, research programs and activities for the betterment of India.  

 The Development Innovation Lab - India (DIL-India) 

 DIL-India, based at the Trust in India, is dedicated to using the tools of economics to benefit millions of people. We work across sectors like education, health, agriculture, climate change, road safety, and others. We do so by collaborating with government agencies, firms, and non-profits in India to identify, test, and scale evidence-based solutions.  

 DIL-India has a fast-growing portfolio of policy relevant research. For example, DIL-India is collaborating with the Ministry of Jal Shakti and other collaborators to identify and test safe water solutions that could be deployed at scale. Our estimates suggest that safe water solutions could save the lives of up to 136,000 children annually in India. DIL-India is also working with the Government of Andhra Pradesh to test the impact of personalized adaptive learning on student outcomes in secondary schools across the state.  

 Position description 

 The Trust and DIL-India is seeking a full-time Senior Research Associate to collaborate on new and ongoing research in development economics. The Senior Research Associate would lead scoping and research implementation efforts for DIL India’s projects in education, weather forecasting/digital agriculture, safe water and other areas. Successful applicants will work closely with Principal Investigators (PIs) and collaborate with Professor Michael Kremer, Nobel laureate and DIL Faculty Director. The ideal candidate possesses a mix of economics, analytical, interpersonal and communications skills, and is passionate about conducting research in development economics. 

 Responsibilities 

 Support the design and implementation of experiments under the direction of the PI. 
 Design and refine data collection instruments as well as data collection and monitoring protocols aligned with the research design and desired outcome measurements. 
 Lead data quality assurance efforts for studies 
 Collect, record, and analyze data from field activities. 
 Oversee data management systems, transcribe and code data, conduct consistency and accuracy checks, and ensure data integrity across all fields and administrative datasets. 
 Maintain relationships with partner organizations, government departments, and implementation partners to ensure smooth project execution. 
 Conduct literature reviews. 
 Support in writing policy memos, technical reports, and academic manuscripts summarizing research findings. 
 Ensure compliance with institutional, state, and federal regulatory policies, procedures, directives, and mandates. 
 Oversight provided to research support personnel, field staff, and interns; mentor and provide feedback and technical guidance as needed. 
 Present research findings at staff meetings, seminars, and scientific conferences. 
 Recruit and schedule research participants in compliance with ethical standards and study protocols. 
 Provide technical and administrative support to ongoing and new research projects. 
 Provide guidance to new research associates and research support staff and support their onboarding and capacity building. 
 Perform additional related duties assigned by the PI or senior management.
