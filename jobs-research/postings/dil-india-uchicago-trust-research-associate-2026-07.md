# Research Associate — Development Innovation Lab - India

About the Trust

The University of Chicago Trust (the Trust) is a charitable trust in India, established in 2008 by an alumnus of The University of Chicago. The Trust's objectives are to provide opportunities for grants to support educational programs, research programs and activities for the betterment of India.

The Development Innovation Lab – India (DIL-India)

DIL-India, based at the Trust in India, is dedicated to using the tools of economics to benefit millions of people. We work across sectors like education, health, agriculture, climate change, road safety, and others. We do so by partnering with government agencies, firms, and non-profits in India to identify, test, and scale evidence-based solutions.

DIL-India has a fast-growing portfolio of policy-relevant research. For example, DIL-India is working closely with the Ministry of Jal Shakti and other partners to identify and test safe water solutions that could be deployed at scale. Our estimates suggest that safe water solutions could save the lives of up to 136,000 children annually in India. DIL-India is also working with the Government of Andhra Pradesh to evaluate the impact of personalized adaptive learning on student outcomes in secondary schools across the state.

Additionally, DIL-India works closely with the Ministry of Earth Sciences to support the dissemination of improved AI-based monsoon onset forecasts to 52.8 million Indian farmers.

Position Description

DIL-India is seeking full-time Research Associates to collaborate on new and ongoing research in development economics. Successful applicants will collaborate with Principal Investigators (PIs) at the Development Innovation Lab (DIL) at the University of Chicago, including Professor Michael Kremer, Nobel laureate, and DIL Faculty Director. The Research Associate would lead scoping and research implementation efforts for DIL India's projects in education, weather forecasting/digital agriculture, safe water and other areas. In the near term, the role will involve supporting field implementation and research in Telangana related to weather forecasting, heat-risk forecasts, farmer decision making, as well as qualitative research on numeracy and probability training of farmers. The ideal candidate possesses a mix of economics, analytical, interpersonal and communications skills, and is passionate about conducting research in development economics.
