Analyst II, ESG (R-16753)
Dun & Bradstreet
Mumbai, Maharashtra (also Navi Mumbai/Delhi/Hyderabad/Bengaluru/Chennai)
Hybrid

Designation - ESG Analyst II
Location - Navi Mumbai/Delhi/Hyderabad/Bengaluru/Chennai

Key Responsibilities:
- Involved in ESG, sustainability, climate change consulting, corporate sustainability, or impact investing.
- Use analytics, research, and stakeholder engagement skills for sustainability data analysis and ESG reporting frameworks.
- Work on global sustainability standards: GRI, SASB, TCFD, CDP, UN SDGs, PRI, ISSB.
- Understand carbon markets (India & global) and carry out ESG risk assessments, and regulatory compliance.
- Drafting ESG and sustainability reports (BRSR, GRI, SASB, CSRD) and responding to assessments (CDP, CSA, DJSI, Ecovadis).
- Work on ESG assessments as per IFC PS, ADB ESS, CSDDD, and conducting GHG Accounting (Scope 1, 2, and 3), DMA, LCA, PCF, and EPD.
- Will be working on carbon accounting methodologies (GHG Protocol, ISO 14064) and net-zero strategies.
- Will be handling multilateral and bilateral agency projects (UNDP, IFC, World Bank, etc.).

Key Requirements:
- Bachelor’s or Master’s Degree in Environmental Science, Sustainability, Climate Change, Engineering, Economics, Public Policy, Business Administration, or a related field.
- Preferred postgraduate degrees include MBA, MS in Physics, Chemistry, or Mathematics, or any sustainability/climate change-related course.
- Proficiency in MS Office (Excel, PowerPoint, Word) and ESG analytics tools (SBTi, CRREM, etc.).
- Professional certifications such as SASB FSA, CFA ESG Investing, GRI Certification, GHG Accounting, GARP SCR, PRI Academy ESG Certification, or Certified Climate Change Professional are an advantage.
