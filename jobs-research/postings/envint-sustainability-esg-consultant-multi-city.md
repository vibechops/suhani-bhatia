Sustainability & ESG Consultant
Envint
Experience: 1 - 10 Years
Location: Multiple - Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Pune, Kolkata. Hyderabad & Chennai will also be considered.

Envint (www.envintglobal.com) is a sustainability & ESG services firm, founded with the purpose to shape a more liveable planet for the coming generations. Our mission is to drive sustainability into mainstream thought and action, with the belief that Green makes sense beyond conscience.

Envint helps businesses progress on their sustainability goals with offerings across multiple service lines, sectors and themes. We currently work with diverse clients in India and overseas, on sustainability integration, promoting responsible investment and facilitating climate action.

We are looking for ESG Consultants across multiple levels (from 1-2, 2-5 and 5-10 years of experience) to join us in leadership and delivery roles. The consultant would work closely with Clients (Corporates, Investors and Portfolio companies) and lead/deliver engagements on Sustainability Integration, Responsible Investment and Climate Action across multiple sectors and themes deploying proprietary/third party automation & tools.

Responsibilities:
1. Leading & supporting corporate engagements on sustainability strategy/roadmaps and its implementation, ESG integration, sustainability targets, assessments & foot-printing, ESIAs, decarbonization & climate action, sustainable supply chain, capacity building and ESG disclosures/reporting
2. Leading & supporting investor engagements on ESG due diligences (ESG DD), ESMS roll outs, impact assessments, ESG integration for portfolio companies, capacity building & ESG/impact disclosures/reporting
