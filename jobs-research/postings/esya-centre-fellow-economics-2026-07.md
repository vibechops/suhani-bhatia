General Applications

We are always on the look-out for bright professionals who want to work at the forefront of multi-disciplinary research. The individuals we work with are dynamic and have an intrinsic curiosity for learning as well as a passion for delivering quality outputs. We hire on an on-going basis, as well as through campus placements with select colleges and universities.

To apply to work with us, please submit your CV and statement of intent to contact@esyacentre.org

We prefer candidates with:

- Graduate, postgraduate and advanced degrees in economics, law and information technology.
- Interest in emergent technology trends.
- Demonstrable research and writing skills.
- Ability to work with multidisciplinary teams and meet deadlines.
- Fluency in English as well as local languages.
