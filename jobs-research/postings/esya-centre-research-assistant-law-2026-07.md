(LawFoyer summary, July 21, 2026:)

About the Organization: Esya Centre is a New Delhi-based public policy think tank dedicated to generating empirical research and thought leadership that shapes future policy frameworks. The Centre focuses on the intersection of people, innovation, and value to strengthen public policy discourse in India. Its key research areas include digital antitrust, AI and emerging technologies, creative and green economies, and internet and data governance.

Type / Nature of Opportunity: Full-Time Research Assistant (Law)

Eligibility Criteria: Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) is the minimum qualification (Master's degree preferred). Candidates with a background in technology, digital markets, or related fields will be preferred. Demonstrated ability to produce high-quality legal or policy research will be an added advantage.

Areas of Work / Key Responsibilities: Conduct legal and policy research at the intersection of technology, law, and public policy. Contribute to policy analysis and evidence-based policy recommendations. Prepare research outputs such as memos, issue briefs, and related documents.

General Applications

We are always on the look-out for bright professionals who want to work at the forefront of multi-disciplinary research. To apply to work with us, please submit your CV and statement of intent to contact@esyacentre.org

We prefer candidates with:

- Graduate, postgraduate and advanced degrees in economics, law and information technology.
- Interest in emergent technology trends.
- Demonstrable research and writing skills.
- Ability to work with multidisciplinary teams and meet deadlines.
- Fluency in English as well as local languages.
