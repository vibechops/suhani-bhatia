Job Title: Volunteer / Intern – Law and Governance Program
Organization: Feminist Alliance Trust (FEMAT)

Feminist Alliance Trust (FEMAT) is looking for final year law students, fresh law graduates and interested legal practitioners to support our Law and Governance program. Under this program, FEMAT provides free legal counselling to women and vulnerable groups, pro-bono lawyers for appropriate litigation, and promotes gender-mainstreaming of governance and institutional reforms via RTI, PIL and other legal mechanisms. The ideal candidate is someone with demonstrated interests in social justice issues particularly women's rights.

Key Responsibilities
- Conduct preliminary legal research and literature reviews on judicial precedents, legislation, policy documents, Law Commission reports, international laws, covenants, good practices, media reports, and academic studies.
- Assist in drafting pleadings, writ petitions, RTI applications, and materials for public awareness campaigns.
- Provide preliminary assistance to vulnerable persons seeking legal counselling and pro-bono legal representation through FEMAT.
- Support field-based campaigns, legal awareness initiatives, and public outreach programmes, as and when required and feasible.

Qualifications
- Strong understanding of civil, criminal, constitutional laws along with international covenants and good practices in social justice field
- Strong research and writing skills
- Self-motivated and enthusiastic for social justice and women's rights
- Ability to create social media content and campaigns will be added advantage.

Number of positions: 5 positions are currently open.

Location: The position is hybrid and majority of the work may be carried out remotely. Occasional field visits maybe required. Candidates from Delhi NCR, particularly West Delhi, maybe preferred.

Remuneration: All positions presently announced by FEMAT are voluntary and offered on a pro-bono basis. Interns completing one-month of internship will be provided with a completion certificate. Travel and miscellaneous expenses related to field based activities reimbursed on as-is basis.

How to apply: email CV, cover letter, and writing sample to feministalliancetrust@gmail.com or volunteer@feministallianceindia.org with subject "Application – Volunteer".

About Us: FEMAT works towards ending gender-based violence and discrimination via feminist transformation, spatial justice for women, free legal counselling/pro-bono support and short-stay residence for women leaving violence, and gender mainstreaming of governance.
