 The required qualifications for a Project Manager at a Garima Greh (shelter home for transgender persons under the SMILE scheme) include an MA in Social Work (MSW) along with a minimum of 5 years of experience in the social sector.

Key Criteria & Preferences
Educational Qualification: Master of Arts (MA) in Social Work.Work Experience: At least 5 years of working experience in the social welfare or development sector.

Desirable Preference: Preference/desirability is given to a transgender person who possesses a minimum of 2 years of experience in running or managing a shelter home or residential facility.

Core Responsibilities
Overseeing the day-to-day management and effective functioning of the shelter home.
Ensuring compliance with prescribed care, protection, and rehabilitation standards.
Assisting the Project Director with individual care plans and reporting progress.
