Do you have a strong desire to address pre-birth and post-birth discrimination against girls and want to create meaningful change? At Girls Count, we believe in the power of individuals to drive social change. Volunteering or interning with us allows you to contribute to our mission of enhancing the value of girls in the country.

Flexible Opportunities
Whether you can contribute a few hours a week or are looking for a full-time internship, we offer flexible roles that suit your availability and interests.

Who can apply
We welcome applications from students, recent graduates, professionals, and anyone who shares our commitment to gender equality. Prior experience in social work, gender studies, communications, or related fields is a plus, but not mandatory. What matters most is your dedication and willingness to learn.

How to apply
To apply, please send your resume and a brief cover letter explaining your interest in volunteering or interning with Girls Count to info.girlscount@gmail.com. Be sure to mention any specific skills or areas of interest you have.
