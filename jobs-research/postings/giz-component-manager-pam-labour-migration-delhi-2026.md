Component Manager
Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ) GmbH
Location: Delhi

This vacancy announcement is for a professional role that encompasses two significant projects: the Global Programme “Partnerships for Development-oriented Vocational Training and Labour Migration (PAM)” in India and the develoPPP initiative “German-Indian Apprenticeship Partnership: Technical Vocational Education for SMEs”.

PAM - Partnerships for development-oriented vocational training and labour migration & Skilled Labour Fund / IGGSP Indo – German Green Skills Programme

PAM India develops fair, development oriented mobility pathways between India and Germany. In partnership with IGGSP, it strengthens Indian TVET institutions to meet German/international standards, supports recognition of qualifications, and pilots fair apprenticeship mobility models. Measures include transparent selection, German language and integration preparation, soft skills/interview training, employer matching, visa and recognition support, and capacity building—with a focus on green occupations and opportunities for women.

Responsibilities (excerpt):
- Provide technical leadership and coordination for national support staff and serve as the primary interface with PAM Germany, Skilled Labour Fund/develoPPP teams, IGGSP, and private sector partners.
- Establish and manage strategic India–Germany partnerships in TVET, skills development and fair labour migration across government, private sector, civil society, diaspora organisations and academia.
- Support the coordination and engagement of political and institutional stakeholders to strengthen the institutional anchoring of the project approach within the TVET system in Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Telangana and Karnataka and facilitate future scaling.
- Advise project partners and applicants on vocational training pathways, apprenticeship opportunities in Germany, fair labour migration, visa procedures and career prospects.
