B. Project Background
India’s freight decarbonization is constrained by the limited adoption of Zero Emission Trucks (ZETs), particularly among small and medium fleet operators and logistics MSMEs. These operators face high upfront vehicle costs, limited access to credit, uncertain residual values and technological and operational risks. At the same time, financial institutions perceive ZET financing as high-risk due to the lack of performance data, standardized risk assessment tools, and proven mitigation mechanisms. This results in low credit uptake, slowing the transition to low-carbon freight transport.
The project aims to overcome the financing barrier faced by small fleet operators in India’s freight sector, which limits the adoption of Zero Emission Trucks. It will achieve this by mobilizing small carriers into viable ZET projects, supported by a de-risking framework and a Risk Sharing Facility (RSF) that enhances project bankability and enables lenders to offer better loan terms. Once the targeted fund is secured, the fund manager will operate the RSF by providing partial credit guarantees to financial institutions, ensuring responsible fund use and catalysing lending at scale. Small fleet operators and logistics providers will gain access to clean technology and affordable finance, improving ESG standards and reducing emissions across supply chains.
