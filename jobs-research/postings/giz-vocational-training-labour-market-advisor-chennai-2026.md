Vocational Training and Labour Market Advisor
Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ) GmbH
Location: Chennai, Tamil Nadu

This vacancy announcement is for a professional within the develoPPP initiative “German-Indian TVET Partnership” / related PAM India component.

Responsibilities (excerpt):
- Lead the implementation and management of the PAM India component and support the implementation of project activities in Tamil Nadu by coordinating with public and private partners and ensuring timely, high-quality delivery.
- Develop and maintain partnerships with government institutions, TVET providers, private sector organisations and other stakeholders to promote fair labour migration and skills development.
- Provide technical advice and operational support to project partners and applicants on vocational training, apprenticeship opportunities in Germany, labour migration pathways and related processes.
- Manage project operations, including contracts, grants, monitoring, reporting, event organisation and project documentation in compliance with GIZ procedures.
- Support communication, visibility and knowledge management, including stakeholder engagement, PR materials and dissemination of project results and lessons learned.
- Support the development and delivery of training programmes, including Training of Trainers (ToT), Training of Technical Trainers (TTT), intercultural preparation and pre-vocational skills development.
- Assist in adapting curricula, training materials and bridging modules to local needs and project requirements.

Sound expertise sought in at least one of: labour migration; international recognition of professional qualifications; vocational education and training; cooperation with the private sector; public-private partnerships.
