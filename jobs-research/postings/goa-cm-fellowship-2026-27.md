No. DPSE/III/CMFP/180/2020-21/580 Date: 01/06/2026

Applications invited for 20 Fellows under Goa Chief Minister's Fellowship Programme 2026-27.

Essential:
- Graduate/Post Graduate any discipline from recognised University/Institution with First Class in Graduation/Post Graduation
- Experience in the relevant fields
- 15 years Resident Certificate
- Passed Std. X and XII public examinations from the State of Goa
- Knowledge of Konkani
- Fluent in all forms of communication in English
Desirable: Knowledge of Marathi
Age: below 45 years

Thematic areas (at least one): agriculture & allied, industries, renewable energy, education, health, Ayurveda, Pharmacy, skill development, women empowerment, finance, welfare, planning, revenue, AI, Robotics, Computer, new innovative technology, urban reforms.

Duration: 12 months including one month training.
Stipend: ₹10,000 during training month; then ₹40,000/month for remaining 11 months.

Selection: shortlist on qualifications/experience; oral interview; shortlist on www.goadpse.gov.in

Apply by 15 June 2026: plain paper application to Director, DPSE, Pt. Deendayal Upadhyay Bhavan, Porvorim Goa 403521 with photo, bio-data, mobile, email, self-attested degrees/experience, 15-year residence certificate, Aadhaar/EPIC — OR email planning-dpse.goa@nic.in. Queries: dir-dpse.goa@gov.in

No right to extension/confirmation on regular basis. High Powered Committee decisions binding.
