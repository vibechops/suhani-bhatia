About Godrej Industries Group (GIG)

 

At the Godrej Industries Group, we are privileged to serve over 1.1 billion consumers globally through our businesses with market leadership positions in the consumer products, real estate, agriculture, financial services and chemicals industries. We strive to continuously improve how we do business, and how we live in the world; shaping a more sustainable and responsible future by placing our planet and people alongside profit.

 

https://www.godrejindustries.com/

 

About the function

 

The Good & Green (G&G) team drives GIG’s commitment of placing planet and people alongside profit. The team is responsible for designing, driving and achieving the Godrej Industries Group’s sustainability and social impact vision, covering Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG), Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) and Workers’ Wellbeing. The function works closely with leadership across all our Group companies and cross-functional teams such as manufacturing, supply chain, R&D, HR, Investor Relations, DEI, Legal, Finance, Corporate Affairs, Audit and Corporate Communications, to create and deliver impactful environmental and social programmes. 

 

https://www.godrejindustries.com/sustainability

 

About the role

 

As a Good and Green Manager, you will lead CSR programs across our thematic areas of water, public health, environment, waste management and community impact for our Godrej Consumer Products business. Your responsibilities will include strategy, design, implementation, monitoring and impact assessment of all ongoing and new programs.

 

 

Key Responsibilities:

 

1. Strategy & Governance 

a. Support the development and implementation of GCPL Strategy aligned with Good & Green priorities and business ESG roadmaps

b. Design business-specific CSR roadmap that address diverse needs such as environment, livelihoods, gender equity, health, and education, among others

c. Ensure alignment with Schedule VII of the Companies Act, UN SDGs, Godrej’s sustainability framework and other requirements

d. Strengthen and support CSR governance structures — policy implementation, board reporting, and compliance oversight

e. Support integration of CSR insights into GCPL and Group-wide sustainability and brand narratives. 

 

2. Program management:

a. Program Design: Strategize, build and roll out new programmes including flagship programs that deliver social impact while aligning with core business capabilities; embed innovation and technology in program design to drive efficiency and transparency; build strong monitoring and evaluation frameworks for the program and develop key performance indicators 

b. Program Monitoring and Reporting: Ensure yearly outcome and impact targets are met within pre-defined budgets; conduct needs assessments, and impact assessments as required; ensure all CSR programs are reported on the MIS platform and update relevant dashboards; review and oversee all program activities in various locations, measure their performance on weekly, monthly and quarterly basis and document the findings; travel regularly to various locations to oversee project implementation, develop professional relationships with the external stakeholders, and support them in showcasing program outcomes to the public.

c. NGO Relationship Management: Build new relationships and manage relationships with existing partners; support in forging strategic partnerships with select NGOs

d. External Stakeholder Relationship Management: Build new relationships and manage existing partnerships across stakeholder groups (government, social enterprises, like-minded corporates); support in forging strategic partnerships with select foundations, academia, and government institutions

e. Internal Stakeholder Relationship Management: Consult with and influence key internal stakeholders to gain input and buy-in; work closely with colleagues across functions to communicate impact and progress

f.  Continuously learn from and adapt to changes in the external environment

 

3. Compliance, Reporting & Communication 

a. Ensure compliance with statutory CSR regulations and utilization of mandated budgets 

b. Enable the preparation of the Annual CSR Report, statutory disclosures (like BRSR, CSR form 2 and other ESG-related disclosures), and updates for the CSR Committee and any other updates and reports as needed by leadership 

c. Collaborate with Corporate Communications to amplify storytelling and impact communication through digital, print, and media channels. 

d. Showcase milestones through internal campaigns, leadership dialogues, and Good & Green Week celebrations. 

 

4. Innovation & Thought Leadership 

a. Enable positioning Godrej Industries Group and GCPL as a thought leader in inclusive and sustainable development

b. Support in publishing case studies, research papers, and impact stories to highlight CSR innovation and outcomes

c. Enable building collaborations with think tanks, academia, and global foundations to influence broader CSR and ESG discourse 

d. Build the capacity of internal teams; support relevant internal stakeholders with learning opportunities
