Background
Founded in April 1994, The Humsafar Trust (HST) is India's first community-based organization (CBO) of self-identified LGBTQ+ persons. HST's advocacy & inclusion vertical engages with communities and stakeholders to promote the meaningful inclusion of LGBTQ+ persons. Through outreach with corporates and other workspaces, it has been helping create inclusive environments, supporting incidents of discrimination, designing policy change, and setting up partnerships with over 75 organizations across India. Additionally, HST organizes awareness and sensitization workshops for healthcare providers, law enforcement agencies, judiciary, legislators, politicians and political parties, government bodies, media, and student communities to sensitize them on LGBTQ+ issues.

Key responsibilities
1. Programme coordination & implementation
- Support the setup and operation of the digital community centre in Mumbai.
- Coordinate training sessions, virtual employability workshops, and entrepreneurship capacity-building programmes.
- Maintain direct communication with beneficiaries, trainers, and mentors to ensure smooth delivery of project components.
- Assist in the execution of the Gulabi Mela – queer enterprise marketplace, including coordination with entrepreneurs, vendors, and volunteers.
2. Monitoring & reporting
- Track activity timelines, budgets, and outputs as per project plans.
- Collect data, attendance, and documentation for monitoring and evaluation.
- Prepare monthly and quarterly progress reports and assist in final project reporting.
- Support the team in preparing utilization and narrative reports for the donor.
3. Outreach & communication
- Assist in dissemination activities through social media, community networks, and digital campaigns.
- Document success stories, testimonials, and visual media for impact reporting.
4. Stakeholder & volunteer engagement
- Coordinate employee volunteering engagements with donor representatives.

Qualifications & Competencies
- A clear commitment toward advocating for the inclusion of LGBTQ+ communities.
- Must be street smart, detail-oriented, and solution-driven.
- Passion for LGBTQ+ inclusion and engagement.
- Proficiency in English, Hindi and Marathi is preferred.
- Experience working on upskilling and education, gender or queer inclusion will be preferred.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
- Preferred skills and experience in social media handling for advocacy and outreach.
