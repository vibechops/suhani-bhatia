Background: The Humsafar Trust (HST) is India's first community-based organization (CBO) of and for LGBTQ+ individuals that started in April 1994.

Project description: Research interviewers under Project CARET: Consortium for Advancing LGBTQ+ Health Research, Policy Engagement & Training. Under this project, HST is conducting a large-scale, national-level hybrid survey, 'The Rainbow Health Survey', on LGBTQ+ health status, health care access and social determinants.

Objectives of this survey:
- To determine the prevalence of physical and mental health conditions among diverse LGBTQ+ subpopulations in India
- To examine the associations between social determinants and health outcomes among LGBTQ+ individuals
- To assess healthcare access, utilization patterns, and unmet health needs of LGBTQ+ people
- To identify proactive factors and resilience resources that may mitigate negative health outcome in LGBTQ+ populations

Main job responsibilities:
- Understand the study objectives, questionnaire content, and interview protocols to identify eligible participants
- Obtain informed consent ethically – written, verbal, as applicable
- Conduct face to face, telephonic or online interviews in a professional, non-judgemental, and empathetic manner
- Ensure privacy and comfort of the study participant
- Record responses accurately using electronic mode of data collection device (tablet/laptop)
- Ensure data completeness and accuracy; Adhere strictly to data privacy and other ethical consideration
- Report daily progress to the field supervisor

Minimum Qualifications and Skills required:
- A minimum qualification of 12th pass with prior experiences of working with the LGBTQ+ community
- Experience in conducting interviews with the community
- Strong understanding of research ethics, including informed consent, confidentiality, and voluntary participation
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
- Knowledge of at least two languages is preferred (English and Hindi/Marathi/Assamese)
- Experience in using digital data collection tools (tablets)
- Willingness to travel to field sites and work flexible hours as required by the project

Duration: The recruitment is purely on contract basis for the duration of field survey
Application: research@humsafar.org with subject "Application for the post of Research Interviewer". Transmasculine person, lesbian and bisexual women are encouraged to apply.
