VA26P046V01 – Project Officer – Partnerships, Outreach and Communications – New Delhi
Organisation: International Centre for Migration Policy Development
Deadline: 11/04/2026
Status: Expired

Within the Project “European Legal Gateway Office Pilot in India” and directly reporting to the Project Manager in New Delhi, India, the Project Officer – Partnerships, Outreach and Communications contributes to the implementation and coordination of stakeholder engagement, partnership development, outreach and communication activities under the project. This includes cooperation with Indian ministries, state-level authorities, skilling bodies, universities, research institutions and private sector actors, as well as engagement with EU Member State embassies in India, their partner agencies and relevant EU institutions.

In coordination with the Project Manager and the EU Support Office in Brussels, the incumbent contributes to the implementation of partnership and outreach activities and supports structured information exchange between project partners in India and the EU. The role also contributes to project communication and digital outreach activities to enhance the visibility of the EU–India Legal Gateway initiative.

Mandatory languages: English and Hindi.
