# Associate/Senior Associate, AI Transformation

IDinsight is hiring an AI Transformation Associate/ Senior Associate to deepen our internal AI transformation and support our work with external partners. The Associate/Senior Associate will support AI transformation focused on generative AI, from driving adoption to ensuring safe deployment, optimizing workflows, and building custom solutions.

This role sits at the intersection of change management, consulting, and product building. It is perfect for someone who wants to master the practicalities of AI implementation, navigating the messy reality of rapidly advancing technology, data privacy, and organizational behavior.

The Senior Associate will be a part of our Impact & Transformation team, which drives organizational transformation by bringing data to bear on organizational decisions and leads initiatives, especially our internal generative AI transformation, that increase our impact and cost-effectiveness.

This role will report to Anmol Rawat, Manager, Impact & Transformation.

About IDinsight

IDinsight helps leaders combat poverty worldwide by designing, deploying and promoting evidence-generating tools. We tailor the best methodologies to partner needs and constraints to fuse evidence with action. We serve governments, NGOs, foundations and social businesses across Africa and Asia in all major program areas, including health, education, agriculture, livelihoods, finance, energy and governance.

Our approach rests on four pillars:
- Rigorous: We develop and use a wide range of cutting-edge data and evidence tools, including experimental evaluations, monitoring systems, data analytics and visualization, process evaluations, machine learning and more.
- Cost-effective: Every dollar spent is justified by expected impact. If funds could be better used in another way, we say so.
- Timely: Actionable information is delivered in time for client decision-making deadlines.
- Demand-driven: We deploy solutions tailored to partner contexts and needs with no competing agendas.

Our diverse, growing team of roughly 200 outstanding colleagues operates in nearly two dozen countries worldwide. Learn more about our mission and values at www.IDinsight.org.

What You'll Do

You will support AI transformations within IDinsight and for external clients. Your work could be 100% internal or 100% external at any given time. Your work will include:

Project & engagement delivery
- Own workstreams: Manage distinct components of advisory engagements (e.g., design of a no-code agent, or creation of a training module), ensuring high-quality execution and timely delivery.
- Produce high-quality deliverables: Draft clear, compelling presentations, memos, and technical documentation that translate complex AI concepts into actionable insights for non-technical audiences.
- Build and manage relationships: Support managers in building and managing trusted advisor relationships with internal and external clients.

Discovery & workflow diagnosis
- Diagnose pain points: Conduct user research, interviews, and shadowing sessions to uncover bottlenecks where AI can generate the highest return on investment.
- Map user journeys: Translate fuzzy organizational problems into clear, logical process maps to identify specific intervention points for automation or augmentation.
- Prioritize opportunities: Analyze potential use cases based on feasibility, risk, and impact, helping leadership decide what to build and what to skip.

Build & deploy AI solutions
- Configure agents: Build and refine tailored AI agents (e.g., CustomGPTs, Claude Projects, Gems) that solve specific user problems.
- Build automations: Design and implement low-code workflows (using tools like Zapier, Make, or n8n) to connect AI models with everyday tools (email, Slack, CRMs), moving beyond simple chat interfaces to real workflow integration.
- Curate knowledge: Structure and organize institutional data to ensure AI tools have high-quality context to draw from, ensuring outputs are accurate and relevant.

Drive adoption & change
- Facilitate workshops: Lead interactive sessions that guide teams through AI skills like context building, use-case identification, and workflow optimization.
- Design learning materials: Create accessible guides, videos, and cheat sheets accelerate value creation.
- Support champions: Equip champions across teams with the resources and information they need to drive peer-to-peer learning.

Measure Impact & Ensure Governance
- Track success: Deploy learning and impact-focused measurement strategies to support continuous iteration and achieve impact goals. Run surveys, analyze data, and conduct interviews to determine if our tools are actually saving time and improving quality.
- Safeguard quality: Conduct testing and red-teaming of AI tools to identify risks, hallucinations, or biases before widespread deployment.
- Operationalize ethics: Help implement AI governance frameworks, ensuring that every deployment adheres to data privacy and ethical standards.

This role will be dynamic. Responsibilities are likely to change over time as we learn and our opportunities evolve.

Qualifications

We're looking for a combination of consulting expertise, technical capability, product thinking, and entrepreneurial drive. We value demonstrated ability over traditional credentials.

- Commitment to IDinsight's values and mission.
- 2-6 years in consulting, product operations, or digital transformation: Track record of supporting complex projects and delivering high-quality results.
- AI Power User: You are "AI native" in your work. You use LLMs daily to accelerate your output. You have a strong grasp of prompting strategies, context building, and the limitations (hallucinations, bias) of current models.
- Experience with hands-on AI implementation: Experience building and integrating no-code AI agents (e.g., CustomGPTs, Projects, Gems, etc.) and low-code automations (using tools like Zapier, Make, or n8n) that augment or automate workflows.
- Change Management: Experience supporting organizational change or technology adoption. Strong intuition that tools don't create impact unless people use them. Experience facilitating training, managing attitudes, or helping teams navigate new ways of working.
- Product Thinking: Experience conducting user research, translating fuzzy problems into clear solutions, defining problem statements, and validating assumptions through interviews and data.
- Communication & facilitation: Strong writing and presentation-building skills. Ability to distill technical concepts for non-technical audiences. Experience facilitating workshops or training sessions.
- Analytical Rigor: Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to measure impact through data. Comfortable with advanced Excel/Google Sheets or SQL/R/Python.

Bonus:
- Experience living and working in low-income country settings.
- Experience working within social sector organizations, especially nonprofits, research organizations, consulting organizations, or foundations.
- Ability to collaborate with technical teams building AI solutions and agents.
- Knowledge of agile/lean methodologies and rapid experimentation frameworks.

Not required: Data science or data engineering background. This role will likely not require heavy coding.

IDinsight's commitment to reducing power asymmetries

IDinsight is committed to reducing power asymmetries in the social sector. Our commitment to diversity, equity, and inclusion reflects our understanding of the need for the sector to abandon unhealthy practices of the past. We wish to be part of a new generation of international NGOs who are honest about this history and transparent about our role in the present. Our commitment is also aligned with the impact of our work.

We seek a workforce that is inclusive of a variety of perspectives that will help us refine and improve our methods and relationships and strengthen the services we provide our clients and their communities or constituencies. The following commitments represent our vision for the IDinsight team:

- IDinsight will have greater representation from the populations with whom we work and the clients we serve.
- IDinsight will have greater representation from the countries in which we work.
- Across all countries in which we recruit, we will seek greater representation from historically excluded communities.
- IDinsight will foster an inclusive work culture that empowers a diverse team to do their best work.
