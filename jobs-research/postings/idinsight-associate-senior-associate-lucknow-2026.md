Associate/Senior Associate, India (Lucknow) 

 Location: Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India
 

 

 About IDinsight 
 

 We amplify social impact by partnering with leaders to develop tailored solutions. Our mission is to use data and evidence to help leaders combat poverty worldwide. 

 IDinsight is a mission-driven global advisory, data analytics, and research organisation that helps global development leaders maximise their social impact. We tailor a wide range of data and evidence tools to help decision-makers design effective programs and rigorously test what works to support communities. We work with governments, multilateral agencies, foundations, and innovative non-profit organizations in Asia and Africa. Our work spans various sectors, including agriculture, education, health, governance, sanitation, financial inclusion, and others. 
 We are a team of economists, statisticians, data scientists, data engineers, and data collection experts. Our goal as an organisation is to make sure that data and evidence are used for making decisions -- this is why we invest a lot of time and energy in understanding the institutional constraints within which our partners operate and what decisions they need to take, so that we can focus on generating only that data which is genuinely required to help inform decisions. 

 IDinsight is committed to non-discrimination in our recruitment, employment practices, and organisational culture, regardless of people’s age, disability, gender, gender identity, national origin, race, religion or belief, or sexual orientation, or any other status protected by applicable law. 

 Our diverse, growing team of roughly 200 outstanding colleagues operates in nearly two dozen countries worldwide.  Learn more about our mission and values at www.IDinsight.org .   

 

 About our governance portfolio in India 

 In India, we have worked with several union and state government organisations over the past 13 years. We are currently the M&E partner for Mission Karmayogi, working closely with both the Capacity Building Commission and the Karmayogi Bharat, and have previously worked with NITI Aayog on the Aspirational Districts Program and the National Data Analytics Platform. We also have active engagements with the Planning Departments in different states. In Telangana, we conducted the annual KPI survey for the government, as part of which we have built a representative panel for ~50,000 households and used that panel to collect data on health, nutrition, and learning outcomes. In Uttar Pradesh, we are supporting the Planning Department to generate high-frequency estimates of economic activity as well as supporting capacity building efforts in the department. We have supported state governments in similar ways in Punjab, Bihar, Odisha, Delhi and Andhra Pradesh.  

 
 

 About the  Associate/ Senior Associate Position 
 

 Associates / Senior Associates at IDinsight are high-performing early-career professionals passionate about experiencing the front-line realities of evidence-informed decision-making. These roles provide a rich, varied experience with responsibilities ranging from in-depth research and analysis, data collection management, client interactions, and internal team building.  We seek intellectually curious, resilient, and team-oriented individuals who, in addition to developing professional and technical skills, are highly motivated to grapple with the difficulties and unpredictability of trying to improve lives at scale. 

 

 We are looking for an experienced Associate / Senior Associate with a proven track record of working with Indian governments. The ideal candidate will bring expertise in public policy, governance reforms, and stakeholder relationship management.This role requires the candidate to be based in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh
