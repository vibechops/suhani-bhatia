ABOUT IDR
IDR (India Development Review) is Asia’s largest knowledge platform for the development community.
Founded as a nonprofit in 2017, we publish ideas and insights on philanthropy and social impact in India.

THE ROLE
We are currently looking for an analyst to assist with the implementation of our editorial strategy. In this role, you will support the editorial team with sourcing stories, transcribing interviews, writing and editing article drafts, as well as in newsletter production, publishing of articles, and editorial operations.

- Transcribe interviews; write and edit article drafts
- Conduct research for IDR features and properties
- Publish articles and support newsletter production
- Support the editorial team on workshops and field work

Requirements:

- A keen interest in social change issues.
- Excellent writing and communication skills and an orientation for multimedia content.
- Strong command over the English language; a strong command over Hindi or any other Indian language is a bonus.
- Attention to detail.
- The ability to think critically and work well in a team.
- Internet fluency, including comfort with social media and search engines and familiarity with digital publishing.
- A college degree, and 1–3 years of work experience, either in the development sector or in writing/editing.

HOW TO APPLY
Send your CV and a writing sample with the subject line ‘Analyst — English Editorial’ to hiring@idronline.org. The writing sample should not have been edited by a third party or AI.

Compensation: INR 3.9–4.8 lakh per annum based on experience.
Location: anywhere in India. Delhi-NCR twice a week; Mumbai once a week; elsewhere remote.
