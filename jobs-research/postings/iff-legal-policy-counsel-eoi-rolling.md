IFF accepts expressions of interest across staffed roles, internships and volunteering on a rolling basis and responds as vacancies arise.

Application process — email join@internetfreedom.in:
1. Covering letter showing personality, skills and suitability; state the month you want to start.
2. Attach resume.
3. Attach a work sample. For counsel (Legal and Policy): ordinarily a writing sample or project. For literacy roles (Ops, Social Media, Fundraising): usually a script, carousel or video.

IFF is a small team and hires deliberately; there may not be immediate openings, but they try to respond to every applicant passionate about defending digital rights in India.
