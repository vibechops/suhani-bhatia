Specialist-Gender Integration, India Health Action Trust (IHAT), Bhopal

DevNetJobsIndia status at scrape: This Job is no longer active.

Role summary (from public listing while active):
The primary responsibility of this position would be to provide technical support for integrating gender-responsive and gender-transformative approaches across programmes of MPIH and across all levels (State, Division, District, facility and communities level). They will lead documentation and support secondary and primary research, along with co-ordination for gender integration across other programmes. The Specialist will lead evidence generation to inform programme and policy decisions, and strengthen the capacity of programme teams and government counterparts to institutionalise gender-responsive practices across programmes and interventions.

Selected responsibilities from public listing:
- Support the development of tools, study designs, processes and approaches that continuously improve technical excellence and impact in the area of transformation to increase women's access to quality care.
- Support gender and power analyses, effectiveness and efficacy studies, evaluation and needs assessments as required by the program to inform program strategies.
- Lead the design and implementation of gender integration strategies across programme verticals, ensuring alignment with organisational priorities and government health programme.
- Provide technical guidance for integrating gender and intersectionality into programme design, implementation, monitoring, learning, and evaluation.

Experience (from public listing):
- Relevant experience required 6-8 years
- Essential – 3-4 years of experience in gender related work
- Strong technical understanding of gender equality, gender integration, intersectionality, and social norms programming.
- Qualitative and quantitative research skills.
- Excellent written and interpersonal communication skills.
