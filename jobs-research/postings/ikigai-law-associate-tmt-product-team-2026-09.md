Associate, TMT Product Team (Full Time)

About our product team

Our product team helps tech businesses navigate regulatory environments while they launch and scale. We work on products where the law is unclear or absent. And help our clients develop business models which were unknown, if not unimaginable, a few years ago. We have been fortunate to be part of shaping several first-to-market products. Products which break the mould and create disruptive technologies.

We see ourselves as an extension of a company's in-house product team. Where we develop the product with our clients. We come in at the strategic and ideation stage - often working backwards from a single line value proposition. At the same time, we also zoom-in, combing through UI/UX of a product to stress-test it against the law.

Although we work with many tech-driven sectors, fintech is one of our core practice areas. We are deeply entrenched in the digital payments, digital lending, neo-banking, and payment intermediation space. In our monthly newsletter 'FinTales' we probe legal and policy issues that impact the fintech-verse.

Who are we looking for?

We are looking for someone who loves technology and how it interacts with the law. And has 3 to 6 years of post-qualification experience with tech-legal issues. Two years of work experience with a law firm is a must. Experience with an in-house legal team at a technology company is a bonus.

Our product team demands a knack for understanding tech products. And what brings them to life. We are looking for someone who is receptive to evolving regulatory environments. And much like our entrepreneurial counterparts can 'thrive in chaos'.

If you are that someone, we encourage you to apply. Our work is chaotic, energetic, and fast-paced. And most of all, fun.

You can apply at careers@ikigailaw.com with a brief introduction and your CV. We will be in touch.
