Introduction
India is among the leading countries of origin for migrant care workers, particularly in the health sector, across the world. Through the Migrant Care Project (April 2025 – May 2026), the ILO promotes decent work for migrant care workers from India with strengthened policy frameworks and institutional mechanisms, increased access to information and quality migration services.

The Project Administrative and Finance Assistant will work as a team member to support administrative and financial management of the project to enable delivery of project activities and outputs within the stipulated timeline.

Reporting: National Project Coordinator (NPC), under overall administrative supervision of the Country Director of India.

Description of Duties include: coordination of project administrative support; office management; briefing materials; project delivery data; financial overview and budget data; databases/registers; correspondence; liaison with constituents and donors; meetings; process project payments; informal interpretation/translation.

Required qualifications
Education: Completion of secondary school education.
Experience: At least five years of relevant work experience.
Languages: Excellent knowledge of English and good working knowledge of language of duty station.
