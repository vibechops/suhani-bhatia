Introduction
ILO Country Office in India (CO-New Delhi) is implementing a development cooperation project entitled ‘Promoting Decent Work for Migrant Care Workers (MigrantCare Project)’ with support from Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ) under its Global Programme Setting Development-Oriented Migration (MEG).

The ultimate objective of this project will be to ensure labour migration stakeholders in India promote decent work for migrant care workers with strengthened policy frameworks and institutional mechanisms, increased access to information, and quality migration services.

Key results expected:
1. Improved technical capabilities of migration officials and relevant stakeholders on strengthening decent work opportunities for migrant care workers at both national and state level through evidence-based research and capacity strengthening sessions.
2. Increased institutional capacity and understanding on migration pathways, social protection, skills and qualifications, and access to justice, among key stakeholders

Description of Duties (selected):
1. Responsible for overall planning, implementation, monitoring and reporting of MigrantCare project
2. Develop concept notes, terms of reference and other relevant documents
3. Lead assessment, evaluation and contracting of project partners, consultants and service providers
4. Conduct research and integrate technical inputs for reports and publications
5–13. Stakeholder follow-up, collaboration with other ILO labour migration projects and GIZ, promote ILO standards on migrant workers and care economy, budget monitoring, represent ILO in forums, supervise consultants and admin/finance staff

Required qualifications
Education: First level university degree in economics, public policies, development studies or a relevant field. Advanced degree on labour economics an advantage.
Experience: At least one year of professional experience at the national level in project management, implementation, monitoring and evaluation. Proven experience with government bodies and development partners an asset.
Technical requirements: Good knowledge of labour migration, as well as care economy, gender, and social policies. Knowledge of the policy making process.
Languages: Excellent knowledge of the working language of the duty station and a good knowledge of English.
