Executive
Organization: Indus Action
Apply By: 17 Mar 2026
Location: Bengaluru (Karnataka)

Indus Action is seeking a young professional who can contribute to critical aspects of the e-Governance project in Karnataka and support the project lead in driving towards crucial outputs and outcomes. The project involves working with the state government department to strengthen their welfare delivery architecture of assisted tech (i.e. common service centres) and/or direct delivery (integrated portals) to ensure entitlements are seamlessly accessible to citizens. The role may also involve supporting operations in other states as per project requirements.

Operations Management: Support the day-to-day operations of projects within designated states, ensuring strict adherence to project terms and scope of work. This includes reviewing and re-designing workflows, auditing govt platforms, data analytics to generate insights, ideating on easing welfare accessing to citizens via assisted-tech.
Cross-functional collaboration: Coordinate with various teams within Indus Action to drive key project outputs and outcomes.

Skills & expertise:
- Solving Abilities: Strong analytical skills to interpret complex data sets and derive actionable insights.
- Core Technical Skills: Medium proficiency in Python/JavaScript/ R Programming, full-stack web apps, scalable system design and data analytics (SQL/Advanced Excel/Tableau etc.)
- Design Skills: UI/UX design proficiency (Figma/Adobe XD/user research) for creating intuitive citizen service interfaces
- Communication Skills / Team Collaboration
- Language proficiency: Ability to read and speak Kannada is essential. Working knowledge of Hindi or another South Indian Language will be an added advantage.

Education & experience:
- Bachelor’s Degree (preference will be given to candidates with background of computer science, engineering or data analytics, Master’s degree is a good to have)
- Minimum of 2 years of work experience in using programming languages, data analytics, developing web applications etc.
- Additional experience of working in government agencies or non-profit organisations will be preferred.
