Location: New Delhi

Indus Action aims to enable over 800 million vulnerable citizens in India to move irreversibly out of extreme poverty by facilitating access to critical government support to which they are entitled. We partner with communities and governments to simplify access to education, livelihoods, healthcare and welfare, using tech and better design to navigate bureaucratic hurdles.

Public job-board mirrors describe the Lead/Sr. Lead– Fundraising role as co-developing and co-leading the organisation's fundraising strategy and funder portfolio, reporting into the Chief of Staff, responsible for securing and stewarding strategic funding partnerships, building a diversified sustainable funding base, and strengthening Indus Action's positioning within the philanthropy ecosystem. Combines strategic fundraising, external engagement and networking, storytelling, and team leadership. Responsibilities include developing annual and multi-year fundraising strategy, owning organisation-wide fundraising targets, and growing domestic fundraising (CSR, foundations, HNIs).
