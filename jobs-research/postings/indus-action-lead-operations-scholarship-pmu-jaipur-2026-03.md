Lead, Operations
Organization: Indus Action
Apply By: 17 Mar 2026
Location: Jaipur (Rajasthan)

About the Organization
Indus Action aims to enable over 800 million vulnerable citizens in India to move irreversibly out of extreme poverty by facilitating access to critical government support to which they are entitled. We partner with communities and governments to simplify access to education, livelihoods, healthcare and welfare, using tech and better design to navigate bureaucratic hurdles.

Job Description
The Lead, Unified Scholarship Interface PMU will anchor the design, implementation, and government partnership efforts for a breakthrough digital scholarship platform in Rajasthan. This individual will drive the conceptualisation, stakeholder engagement, and program management functions required to unify existing scholarship schemes into one streamlined portal under the Department of Social Justice, Government of Rajasthan. The Lead will interface directly with senior government officials, technical vendors, and social protection experts to ensure a user-centric and scalable solution.

Skills & Qualifications:
- A master’s degree in public policy, information systems, management, social sciences, or a related field is preferred.
- At least 8 years’ experience in program management, e-governance, digital platform development, or government relations, with a preference for experience in social welfare or scholarship programs.
- Proven success in leading large-scale tech-enabled projects with government entities, particularly in Rajasthan or similar state contexts.
- Fluency in English and Hindi is required; proficiency in Rajasthani or other regional dialects is highly desirable.
