Multimedia Executive
Organization: Indus Action
Apply By: 27 Sep 2026
Location: New Delhi (Delhi)

About the Organization
Indus Action aims to enable over 800 million vulnerable citizens in India to move irreversibly out of extreme poverty by facilitating access to critical government support to which they are entitled. We partner with communities and governments to simplify access to education, livelihoods, healthcare and welfare, using tech and better design to navigate bureaucratic hurdles.

By 2030, we aim to impact 30 million people with adequate social protection. Since 2013, our program innovations have impacted 2 million+ citizens in accessing entitlements across the above workstreams.

We are looking for a creative and detail-oriented Multimedia Executive to support Indus Action's communications and outreach efforts through compelling visual storytelling. The ideal candidate will have strong graphic design skills, experience creating digital content for social media and reports, and the ability to produce light video edits for campaigns, events, and organisational communications.

Education Qualification/Required Skills & Experience:
- Bachelor's degree in Graphic Design, Visual Communication, Multimedia, Mass Communication, or a related field.
- 2-3 years of professional experience in graphic design and multimedia production.
- Experience working with social impact organizations, nonprofits, development sector organizations, or agencies is preferred.
- Familiarity with social media content formats and platform requirements.
