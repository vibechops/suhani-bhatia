State Operations Manager
Industree Foundation
Locations: Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Jharkhand, Nagaland
Apply by: 22 Aug 2026

Industree Foundation, established in 2000, is based out of Bengaluru, Karnataka, and has been working to create an organised farm and off-farm enterprise ecosystem for women's economic empowerment in India and Ethiopia for over two decades. Industree, through an ecosystem approach, holistically tackles the root causes of poverty by enabling rural women from marginalised communities to set up collectively owned enterprises linked to the market. This has led to significant economic and social gains for women, substantially increasing incomes, social security, improved resilience to crisis, and positive climate action.

State Operations Manager will be responsible for leading, coordinating, and monitoring the implementation of the program in partnership with the State Rural Livelihood Mission (SRLM) at the state level. This role will ensure effective collaboration with state, district, and block-level teams, build the capacities of stakeholders, and drive alignment with program objectives. The position will serve as the key link between the organization, SRLM, and other stakeholders, ensuring timely execution, quality outcomes, and comprehensive documentation for reporting and knowledge management.

Responsibilities (excerpt from posting):
- Foster and maintain strong partnerships with the State Rural Livelihood Mission (SRLM) and other key stakeholders.
- Lead and oversee program implementation across the state, ensuring timely execution, quality delivery, and effective monitoring of activities.
- Provide capacity-building support to SRLM teams at state, district, and block levels.
- Proven experience in program implementation, monitoring, and reporting
- Ability to coordinate with government stakeholders, community institutions
- Strong understanding of rural development and livelihood promotion
- Strong leadership and team management abilities
- Effective stakeholder engagement and partnership-building skills
