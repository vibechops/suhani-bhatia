 and the CV is to be sent via email to  natcom.india@undp.org . 
   
 The detailed vacancy circular containing eligibility, conditions and application form can be accessed at the website of the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, New Delhi on the Recruitment Page at  https://moef.gov.in/
