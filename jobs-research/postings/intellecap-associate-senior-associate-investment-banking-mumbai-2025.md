Associate/ Senior Associate - Investment Banking
- Mumbai
- 2-5+ years
- September 30 2025

Whether it is a consulting expert, a research analyst, a finance prodigy, or an investment banker, we seek to hire the best across the globe and provide them with a unique global ecosystem of learning, experience, and expertise. Our teams are diverse, entrepreneurial, and fun, comprising millennials and seasoned experts, who are passionate about changing the landscape of social change. The ability to serve the next 3 billion is an opportunity of immense promise, and we look at those who are entrepreneurial in their mindset, sharp in their thinking and bring in deep sector expertise to deliver compelling work globally.

Responsibilities:
- Transaction Execution: Support end-to-end deal execution for equity and debt mandates — support in drafting investment memos, pitch decks, teasers, financial models, and term sheets.
- Client Engagement: Support in liaising with clients (entrepreneurs, NBFCs, MSMEs, impact enterprises) to gather data, understand capital requirements, and help structure fundraising strategies
- Investor Outreach: Assist in identifying and connecting with relevant investors/lenders; prepare tailored materials for investor discussions.
- Financial Analysis & Modeling: Build and review financial models, perform historical analysis, projections, valuation (DCF, comparables), and debt structuring scenarios
- Sector & Market Research: Conduct in-depth research on focus sectors (Financial Services, Healthcare, Climate, & manufacturing); create industry landscapes and benchmarking analysis.
- Due Diligence & Compliance: Support due diligence processes (business, financial, ESG); ensure documentation and regulatory requirements are met.

Requirements:
- MBA (Finance) / CFA (or progress towards CFA)
- 2-5+ years of relevant experience in investment banking, financial advisory, credit rating, equity research, or corporate finance
- Strong grasp of financial statements, valuation techniques, and investment structuring (both debt and equity)
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel (including modeling), PowerPoint, and Word
- Excellent communication skills—both written and verbal—with the ability to present ideas clearly and persuasively
- Prior experience in impact sectors, development finance, or NBFCs is a strong plus

Interview Process:
- Round 1 – Business Interview (Functional & technical discussion)
- Round 2 – Business Interview (Case study assessment to evaluate problem-solving skills)
- Round 3 – HR Interview (Final assessment of role fitment & cultural alignment)

Closing date: September 30 2025
