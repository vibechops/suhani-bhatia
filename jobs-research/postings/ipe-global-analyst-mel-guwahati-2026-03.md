Analyst - Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning
Organization: IPE Global
Apply By: 17 Mar 2026
Location: Guwahati (Assam)

Job Description
IPE Global invites applications from eligible candidates for the position of Analyst - Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEL).

As the Analyst - MEL, s/he will primarily be responsible for overseeing and executing the monitoring, evaluation and learning framework on ground for the project related to maternal and child health. S/he will be required to develop necessary tools, oversee field execution of survey and data collection efforts, database management, data cleaning, data analysis, and prepare technical reports. Based on insights from the monitoring data, s/he will also provide feedback and guidance to the program team both on data collection and program improvements.

- Help designing state specific MEL framework and appropriate mechanisms to ensure that project M&E activities, and key indicators are captured in a timely manner

Education Qualification/Required Skills & Experience
Essential
- Advanced university degree in Economics, Statistics, Demography, Development Studies or other relevant disciplines.
- 2-4 years of experience in an M&E role including large-scale quantitative data processing and management; and contributing to evaluation and research studies.
- Experience in large scale data collection, management and analysis in donor funded projects, preferably in health/nutrition/gender-related programmes.
- In-depth knowledge and proven expertise in developing M&E systems, methods & tools, knowledge products using high quality data analytics. Operational experience in health/nutrition sector highly preferred;
- An advanced level skill in SPSS or STATA is non-negotiable. Data mining, data cleaning, merging datasets, running complex query and data analysis in SPSS or STATA is necessary (the candidate must be able to take a test over Skype).
- Experience of working on programs related to maternal child health and nutrition.
