iProbono India is looking to hire a Program Officer in Delhi

Candidates with PQE of 3 years of litigation experience in child rights and/or POCSO matters are encouraged to apply.

Published on: 12 Aug 2026

iProbono India is looking to hire a Program Officer (Child Protection) in Delhi.

Term: Full-time

Qualification: Bachelor's degree in law

Location: New Delhi

Job Description

- We're looking for a dynamic, mid-career legal professional with expertise and litigation experience in child protection laws, particularly the Prevention of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act.
- Ideal candidates should have strong legal skills, strategic thinking, leadership abilities, and a long-term commitment to our work.
- Strong communication and organisational skills, and results-driven teamwork are essential.
- This role will directly report to the Program Manager, Child Protection and involves providing direct legal representation to children, working on strategic litigation, advocacy initiatives and capacity enhancement efforts, and overseeing the work of other colleagues in this vertical.
- This is an opportunity to drive impactful initiatives while growing within iProbono India.

Responsibilities

- Assist Delhi trial and appellate court casework for child survivors of sexual abuse, bonded labour, trafficking, etc., which includes:

1. Coordination with panel advocates, social workers, and partner organisations; and ensuring effective court representation;
2. Attending court hearings, drafting pleadings, briefing panel advocates, and liaising with police, CWCs and government authorities, when required;
3. Conducting legal research and allocating responsibilities within the team, as required.

- Identify areas for legal interventions, lead advocacy and fact finding initiatives, and engage with government stakeholders. Identified areas may also be addressed through social impact litigation, for which the team member will have to work on legal strategy, research and working closely with the panel lawyers involved.
- Internal documentation (updating the iProbono in-house database, updating litigation management notes and dates of hearings).
- Collect and analyse data from various sources to identify trends, gaps, and areas for intervention related to iProbono's work verticals.
- Analyse and assess relevant legislation, new and emerging policy papers, and their implications for iProbono's advocacy efforts.
- Build relationships with non-profit organisations and state agencies to expand our network.
- Conduct training and develop legal resources on child rights laws for partner organisations, their beneficiaries, and the legal community.
- Collaborate with the communications team to develop advocacy materials and storytelling campaigns.

Skills and Experience

- Only candidates with a law degree and relevant litigation experience will be considered.
- At least 3 years of litigation experience in child rights and/or POCSO matters, in an NGO and/or litigation chamber.
- Sound knowledge of the Indian legal system, particularly laws related to child rights.
- Excellent written and oral communication skills in English and Hindi.

Remuneration: Competitive

Deadline: Friday, August 28, 2026

Application

Please email india.recruitment@i-probono.com with all of the following:

- Your CV
- A self authored writing sample (research paper, short/long form article, or other such piece of work)
- A short cover letter stating why you are suitable for this role
- Email subject line: Program Officer 2
