Location: Hauz Khas, New Delhi | In-person, full-time | Experience: 1-3 years preferable | Compensation: 6 to 8 LPA

The Centre for Universal Health Assurance (CUHA) at ISPP works to strengthen India’s journey toward Universal Health Coverage through research, policy engagement, implementation support, and institutional partnerships (health financing, primary healthcare, digital health, governance, healthcare quality, insurance/assurance models, evidence-informed policymaking).

Key Responsibilities:
- Literature reviews, policy analysis, quantitative and qualitative research on health systems, UHC, health financing, governance.
- Support flagship reports, policy papers, journal manuscripts, blogs.
- Collect, manage, analyse and synthesise primary and secondary data.
- Coordinate expert consultations, stakeholder interviews, workshops, roundtables and policy dialogues.
- Prepare briefing notes, presentations, concept notes, meeting summaries.
- Project management support: timelines, documentation, partner coordination, reporting.
- Assist faculty/senior researchers in drafting policy recommendations.
- Monitor health policy developments and knowledge management.

Qualifications: Master’s in relevant fields; 1-3 years preferable; strong analytical and academic writing; literature review and evidence synthesis; familiarity with quantitative/qualitative methods; MS Office; travel willingness. Stats software (R, Stata, SPSS, Python, NVivo) an advantage.

Apply: CV (2 pages max) and Cover Letter (max one page) to careers@ispp.org.in.
