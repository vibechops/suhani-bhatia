ISPP Centre for Urban Transitions (ICUT), Hauz Khas. Reports to Director, ICUT. Salary ₹8–10 Lakh p.a. One-year contract extendable. Daily on-campus presence expected.

ICUT is a hub for research, capacity building, and policy advisory on integrated urban management and urban development; building repository of online learning resources in urban planning and management.

Role contributes to research, programme design and delivery, stakeholder engagement, and knowledge dissemination. 5–10 years in research, consulting, capacity building, or project implementation in urban sector; understanding of urban governance, infrastructure, mobility, land-use, or public finance preferred; prior work with government, think tanks, or multilaterals desirable.
