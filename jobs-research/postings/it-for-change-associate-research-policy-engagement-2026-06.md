We are hiring!

At IT for Change, we believe digital justice is one of the defining developmental challenges of our time. Our work sits at the intersection of critical technology research, policy advocacy, and Global South thought leadership.

We are seeking two researchers and policy professionals to join our team in Bengaluru:

Lead – Research & Policy Engagement — a senior researcher and policy advocate with 6+ years of experience, ready to shape and lead our work on data, AI, and development. We are looking for someone with a strong track record in rigorous research, policy engagement, discourse-shaping, and public-facing thought leadership — from a Global South standpoint.

Associate – Research & Policy Engagement — an early-career researcher with 2+ years of experience, curious and driven, with a keen interest in using research to influence policy discourse on emerging technologies.

We welcome applications from candidates with backgrounds in public policy, political economy, law, development studies, and allied disciplines. Both roles require a deep commitment to social justice, equity, and building just digital futures.

Essential Qualifications
● A master's degree in public policy, law, economics, sociology, development studies, international relations, political science, or related fields
● A minimum of two years of relevant professional experience
● Excellent writing and analytical skills, with the ability to translate complex findings into actionable insights for policymakers, civil society, academia, and other stakeholders
● Demonstrated interest in technology policy, digital governance, AI, and development, with the capacity to translate findings across multiple thematic areas
● Familiarity with political economy, development justice frameworks, and Global South thought leadership
● Ability to work collaboratively across networks and interdisciplinary teams
● Fluency in English and at least one additional Indian language (Kannada, Tamil, Hindi preferred)

The thematic focus of this role includes a wide range of topics at the intersection of digitalization and society, such as:
● Data governance, platform regulation, and corporate accountability frameworks in national, regional, and international governance regimes
● Digital public infrastructure, data justice in health systems, digitalization and the right to food, climate justice, and tech policy
● Digital futures of work, workers' rights in the platform economy, working conditions in digital value chains, and sustainable development

About IT for Change: We are a Bengaluru based non-profit working at the intersection of technology and society. IT for Change aims for a society in which digital technologies contribute to human rights, social justice and equity. Our work in the areas of education, gender, governance, community informatics and internet/digital policies push the boundaries of existing vocabulary and practice, exploring new development and social change frameworks. Some of our work can also be found on our online digital magazine Bot Populi (link: https://botpopuli.net/)
