About Karta Initiative India Foundation (KIIF)

Karta Initiative India Foundation is committed to enabling talented young people from under-served backgrounds to access transformative higher education opportunities and build pathways to leadership.

Through partnerships, mentorship and systemic engagement, KIIF aims to unlock potential and create equitable access to education and career opportunities.

About the Role

KIIF is looking for a dynamic, mission-driven and entrepreneurial Fundraising Lead who can help build a strong, diversified and sustainable resource mobilisation ecosystem for the organisation and contribute to its long-term institutional growth.

The Fundraising Lead will play a key role in developing and strengthening KIIF’s fundraising and resource mobilisation efforts. The role will be responsible for identifying and cultivating institutional donors, CSR partners, foundations and philanthropic organizations; developing high-quality proposals and concept notes; managing the fundraising pipeline; strengthening donor relationships; and supporting grant acquisition, management and reporting.

Key Responsibility Areas

1. Fundraising Strategy & Planning

- Develop      and implement KIIF’s annual fundraising and resource mobilisation strategy      aligned with the organisation’s education and youth-development      priorities.

- Set      fundraising targets, timelines and priorities; maintain a diversified and      sustainable fundraising pipeline across institutional donors, CSR      partners, foundations and philanthropic organisations.

- Track      fundraising progress, identify gaps and recommend new approaches to      strengthen KIIF’s long-term financial sustainability.

2. Institutional, Grant & CSR Fundraising

- Identify,      research and cultivate potential institutional donors, foundations, CSR      partners and philanthropic organisations; assess their priorities and      align them with KIIF’s programmes.

- Lead      the preparation and submission of high-quality proposals, concept notes,      budgets, grant applications and expressions of interest, ensuring strong      programme and financial narratives.

- Support      grant acquisition, negotiations, renewals and budget discussions, while      ensuring alignment between donor requirements and KIIF’s programme      objectives.

3. Donor & Funder Relationship Management

- Build      and maintain strong, professional and long-term relationships with donors,      CSR partners and funding organisations, including regular communication      and engagement.

- Coordinate      donor meetings, presentations, updates and visits, and ensure timely      sharing of progress reports, impact stories, utilisation updates and other      donor requirements.

- Maintain      accurate records of donor commitments, communication, reporting deadlines      and renewal opportunities through appropriate CRM/MIS systems.

4. Cross-functional Collaboration

- Work      closely with the Program, Finance, Leadership and Communications teams to      develop strong funding proposals, budgets, impact narratives and donor      reports, ensuring that fundraising commitments are aligned with KIIF’s      programme priorities and organisational capacity.

- Coordinate      internally to gather programme data, financial information, impact      evidence and success stories required for proposals, donor reporting and      fundraising communications.

- Support      the organisation in strengthening its fundraising systems, documentation,      impact communication and institutional readiness for engaging with diverse      funding partners.

5. Additional Responsibilities

- Undertake      any other duties and responsibilities as assigned by the supervisor or      senior management team to support the mission and goals of the      organisation.

- Be      flexible to travel to programme sites or events as required, contribute to      the on-ground impact of Karta’s programmes and represent KIIF at relevant      external forums, donor meetings and partnership engagements.
