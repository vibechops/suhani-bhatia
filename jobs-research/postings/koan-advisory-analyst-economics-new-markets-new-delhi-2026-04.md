## How to apply (Koan careers page, koanadvisory.com/careers)

To apply to work with us, please submit your CV and statement of intent to contactus@koanadvisory.com

We prefer candidates with:
- Graduate, postgraduate and advanced degrees in economics or law
- Interest in the Indian political economy
- Interest in emergent industries and technology trends
- Demonstrable research skills
- Ability to work with multidisciplinary teams and meet deadlines
- Fluency in English as well as local languages

Internship Programme: We provide rolling internships for economics and law students. Interns may be asked to participate in activities including research, writing and field visits that support the goals of projects assigned to them. Candidates can apply for internships at contactus@koanadvisory.com

## About Koan Advisory Group

Koan Advisory is a research-driven advisory firm that combines legal, economic, and investment expertise to deliver on client mandates in India. Lean, quality-driven, and results-oriented; counts some of the world's largest companies as clients. 40-50 employees, founded 2014, headquartered New Delhi. Practice areas span digital economy and platform regulation, competition economics, trade and geopolitics, electronics supply chains and sustainability, for corporate clients.
