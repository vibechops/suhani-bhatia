## How to apply (Koan careers page, koanadvisory.com/careers)

We are always on the look-out for bright professionals who want to work at the forefront of multi-disciplinary policy and business research. We work with creative and dynamic individuals who have an intrinsic curiosity for learning and a passion for quality outputs. We hire on an on-going basis, as well as through campus placements with select colleges and universities.

To apply to work with us, please submit your CV and statement of intent to contactus@koanadvisory.com

We prefer candidates with:
- Graduate, postgraduate and advanced degrees in economics or law
- Interest in the Indian political economy
- Interest in emergent industries and technology trends
- Demonstrable research skills
- Ability to work with multidisciplinary teams and meet deadlines
- Fluency in English as well as local languages

## About Koan Advisory Group

Koan Advisory is a research-driven advisory firm that combines legal, economic, and investment expertise to deliver on client mandates in India. Lean, quality-driven, and results-oriented; counts some of the world's largest companies as clients. 40-50 employees (+30% YoY), founded 2014, headquartered New Delhi with US presence. Works on technology policy, digital economy, electronics and semiconductor supply chains, trade and geopolitics; co-hosted the July 2026 "India's Digital Economy: Potential & Prospects" conclave with Chintan Research Foundation and Ikigai Law.
