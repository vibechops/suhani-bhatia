## About the Role

As an Analyst in the Compliance Review - Legal function at KPMG, you will be responsible for supporting the review and assessment of regulatory and legal compliance frameworks for clients across various industries. Your role involves analyzing applicable laws, regulations, and statutory requirements to ensure client operations align with legal mandates.

You will assist in conducting compliance audits, reviewing legal documentation, identifying areas of non-compliance, and preparing detailed reports with findings and actionable recommendations. Working closely with senior team members and client stakeholders, you will help assess the adequacy of existing compliance programs and suggest enhancements.

The position requires staying up to date with evolving legal and regulatory landscapes, contributing to research on compliance-related matters, and supporting the development of compliance policies and procedures. You will also help in tracking regulatory changes and assessing their impact on client businesses.

This is an excellent opportunity for professionals with a legal or compliance background looking to build expertise in regulatory advisory within a leading professional services environment.
