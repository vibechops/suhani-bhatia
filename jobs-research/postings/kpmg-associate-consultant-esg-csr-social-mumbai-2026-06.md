Job Title: Consulting Specialist – ESG, CSR & Social

Location: Mumbai

>>Overview:

KPMG in India can help turn your ESG aspirations into action. Our ESG solutions are both holistic and practical. With capabilities that span the enterprise, we can help instill sustainable innovations across your business and help you gain a competitive edge. Our deep multidisciplinary expertise across critical issues—including green strategies, climate resilience, energy transition, reporting, sustainable finance, and social impact can help create the right blueprint for integrating ESG into all verticals of your organization. We go beyond strategy, working with you at each step of your ESG transformation to unlock new value as you build a sustainable future.

>>Job Description:

KPMG Assurance & Consulting Services LLP is seeking a highly motivated and experienced Associate Consultant to join our Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Service line. The successful candidate will work on both CSR and Social Impact engagements, providing expert guidance and support to our clients.

>>Key Responsibilities:

- Strategic Consulting for CSR & Social Impact Initiatives: Support clients across sectors to design, manage, and implement transformative social impact programs aligned with their CSR & Social impact goals and values.
- Stakeholder Engagement: Foster strong relationships with clients ranging from non-profits and social enterprises to corporates, ensuring successful project outcomes.
- Program Management: Manage multiple client engagements and oversee the CSR life cycle from end to end.
- Impact Assessment & Reporting: Develop metrics and frameworks, undertake field visits to urban and rural areas, and measure the effectiveness of social programs.
- Innovation & Sustainability: Promote innovative solutions to address social challenges, integrating sustainable practices during client engagements.
- Financial Analysis for Impact Projects: Guide clients on budgeting, funding opportunities, and financial structuring for social impact investments.
- Knowledge Sharing & Training: Conduct workshops, training sessions, and knowledge-sharing activities to empower stakeholders and ensure long-term impact.
- Collaboration & Team Management: Work closely with multidisciplinary teams within KPMG to ensure seamless delivery of consulting projects.
- Business Development: Support practice leadership with identifying key opportunities and responding to RFPs.

>>Qualifications:

- Master’s degree in Social Work, Rural Management, Social Entrepreneurship, Development Studies, Economics, Public Policy, Finance, MBA, or any allied qualification from Tier 1 institutions.
- 1-4 years of experience in the development sector, CSR program management, blended finance, program management, concurrent monitoring, and impact measurement through various methodologies including SROI and OECD.
- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
- Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
- Additional certifications in CSR, ESG, SROI, NISM Social Impact Assessment, Blended Finance, CFA, or CA (if any).
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, PowerPoint, Word).

>>Preferred Skills:

- Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
- Strong project management skills with the ability to manage multiple engagements simultaneously.
- Sound knowledge of research methodology, sampling techniques, and data collection tools, along with the ability to work with qualitative and quantitative data.
- Acumen for business development.
- Event management.

>>Why Join Us:

- Opportunity to work with a leading global professional services firm.
- Exposure to a diverse range of clients and industries.
- Professional development and career growth opportunities.
- Collaborative and inclusive work environment.
