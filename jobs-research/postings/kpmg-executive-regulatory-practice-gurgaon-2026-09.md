## Role Overview

The Executive will be part of KPMG India's growing Regulatory practice, supporting clients in navigating India's complex regulatory environment across Central Ministries, State Governments, regulators and statutory authorities. The role involves regulatory research, execution support, documentation, and coordination with government authorities, while working closely with senior team members on high-impact regulatory engagements.

This is a client-facing, execution-focused role requiring strong drafting, research and stakeholder coordination skills.

## Key Responsibilities

- Support regulatory advisory engagements across industries involving:
  - Central Government Ministries and Departments
  - State Governments and State-level authorities
  - Sectoral regulators and statutory bodies (e.g., SEBI, MCA, DPIIT, MeitY, RBI, State Industrial Development Corporations, Pollution Control Boards)
- Conduct detailed regulatory research and interpretation of:
  - Acts, Rules, Notifications, Circulars and Policy documents
  - Central and State-specific regulatory frameworks
- Prepare and maintain:
  - Regulatory matrices, compliance trackers and issue logs
  - Draft representations, applications, clarifications and responses to authorities
  - Internal notes and client deliverables
- Support licensing, approvals, registrations, inspections and regulatory filings
- Coordinate with government officials, consultants, lawyers and internal KPMG teams under supervision
- Track regulatory and policy developments and flag potential implications for clients
- Ensure high standards of documentation, independence, ethics and compliance with KPMG policies
- Work closely with global and domestic teams on multi-state or multi-jurisdictional engagements

## Qualifications and Experience

- Bachelor's degree in Law, Economics, or Political Science from a recognized Indian university
- 1-3 years of relevant experience in regulatory, compliance, legal advisory, consulting, or government-facing roles
- Exposure to Central or State government departments, regulators or public authorities preferred
