LEAD at Krea University seeks a Senior Research Associate to strengthen research on small businesses and employment through rigorous analysis, supporting high-quality, policy-relevant insights and evidence.
 Reports to : Senior Research Fellow 
 Salary : Commensurate with qualifications and experience 
 About LEAD at Krea University: 
 LEAD is an action-oriented research centre of IFMR Society (a not-for-profit society registered under the Societies Act), leverages the power of research, innovation and co-creation to solve complex and pressing challenges in development. LEAD specialises in developing in-depth granular understanding of socio-economic contexts and harnessing actionable insights that have a wide range of applications in industry, policy, and academia. LEAD is a collaborative hub that brings experts from diverse disciplines and sectors together to develop innovative solutions by harnessing innovation, technology and analytics.
 About the Role 
 The Senior Research Associate will play a critical role in strengthening the analytical and research capabilities of the Small, Growing Businesses and Employment (SGBE) vertical, working on rigorous studies related to small businesses, entrepreneurship, employment, and policy impacts. The role will involve working closely with senior leadership, multidisciplinary teams, and external stakeholders to support high-quality research design, data analysis, and insight generation.
 What We Are Looking for: 
 A candidate who can independently drive high-quality quantitative analysis, contribute to research design, and strengthen the analytical depth of ongoing and upcoming projects within the SGBE portfolio.
 Key Responsibilities: 
 Strong academic background in Economics, Econometrics, Public Policy, Statistics, or related quantitative disciplines;

 Advanced knowledge of econometrics and applied microeconomic analysis;

 Proficiency in causal inference methods (RCTs, quasi-experimental designs, DiD, PSM, IV, regression techniques);

 Strong hands-on experience with STATA, R, or Python for large-scale data analysis;

 Ability to manage and analyse primary and secondary datasets, including cleaning, structuring, and advanced modelling;

 Experience in impact evaluation, labour economics, MSME/enterprise studies, or development research is preferred;

 Strong analytical thinking, problem-solving ability, and attention to detail;

 Ability to translate complex analysis into clear, policy-relevant insights, reports, and presentations;

 Exposure to field-based research and large datasets is an added advantage.

 Essential Qualifications: 
 Postgraduate in Economics, Statistics, Development Studies, Public Policy, Data Science, or related fields;

 3-4+ years of experience in research, impact evaluation, or data analytics, preferably in livelihoods, entrepreneurship, MSMEs, women’s economic empowerment, or financial inclusion;

 Strong proficiency in Stata/R/Python and quantitative modelling;

 Experience working with primary data and field-based research operations;

 Strong writing, communication, and stakeholder-handling skills;

 Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously and independently.

 Application Instructions: 
 Deadline: Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis until the position is filled. To apply, please collate your resume, a cover letter and writing sample in a single PDF and apply in the form here . 
 Note: Due to the large volume of applications we receive, we are unable to contact each candidate individually regarding the status of their application. Only shortlisted applicants will be contacted for interview purposes.
