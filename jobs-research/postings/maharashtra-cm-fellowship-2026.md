Chief Minister Fellowship Programme Maharashtra 2026: 12-month fellowship; 60 fellows; coordinated by Commissionerate of Economics and Statistics under Planning Department. Academic training component with IIT Bombay.

Important dates: Applications 8 May – 27 May 2026 (fee payment also by 27 May 2026). Application fee ₹500.

Eligibility:
1. Age 21–26 as on 8 May 2026 (born 08/05/2000 to 08/05/2005 inclusive)
2. Graduate any discipline with ≥60% from recognised university
3. Minimum 1 year full-time work experience (employment, internship, apprenticeship, articleship or entrepreneurship)
4. Read, write and speak Marathi; working knowledge of Hindi and English
5. Basic computer and internet knowledge
6. Permanent resident of Maharashtra
7. Prior CM Fellowship completers not eligible
8. One-third of 60 seats reserved for women
9. Placement decided solely by Commissionerate (no candidate preference)

Selection (2 stages):
- Stage 1: Online objective test + essay (same day at centre); top 210 on objective test go to Stage 2; essay ≤500 words, English or Marathi
- Stage 2: Essay assessment + personal interview; final 60 on merit
- Weightage: Objective 30% / Essay 20% / Interview 50%
- Exam centres: Mumbai, Pune, Nashik, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Amravati, Nagpur (choose 3 preferred)

Benefits: Position equivalent to Grade-A officer; stipend ₹56,100 + travel allowance ₹5,400 = ₹61,500/month; 8 days leave; accidental insurance; temporary ID and official email; IIT Bombay academic certificate; GoM fellowship completion certificate.

Apply (cycle closed): https://mahades.maharashtra.gov.in/FELLOWSHIP/
