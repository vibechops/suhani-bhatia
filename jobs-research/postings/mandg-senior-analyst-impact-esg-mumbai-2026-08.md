Job Title: Senior Analyst – ESG : Impact & Private Equity
Location: Mumbai

This role offers an opportunity to support the Impact Management and ESG agenda within the Impact & Private Equity team, by contributing to impact assessments, ESG due diligence, sustainability data collection, validation, and management, regulatory disclosures, and ongoing monitoring of investments. The role will help ensure the availability, quality, and consistency of ESG and impact data used for investment decision-making, internal oversight and external reporting.

The ideal candidate will have up to 2-3 years of experience related field. Exposure to ESG analytics and understanding of sustainability and impact measurement.

Requirements (extract):
- Up to 2-3 years of relevant experience, preferably within financial services, asset management, consulting, sustainability, ESG research or data-related roles
- Exposure to ESG data, sustainability metrics or impact measurement, ideally within private markets or a broader financial services context
- Familiarity with regulatory or disclosure frameworks such as SFDR, EU Taxonomy, UK SDR and/or ESG data standards would be highly beneficial
- Strong analytical skills with the ability to work with quantitative and qualitative ESG/sustainability data
- Strong communication skills (both verbal and written)
- Graduation/MBA (Finance) OR progress towards or completion of CFA Level 1 or above OR an equivalent degree from a reputed institute
- Any ESG, sustainability, or impact investing-related certification would be an added advantage
