About MAP
The Margins to Access Project (MAP), An Initiative of The Ara Trust formerly known as Migration & Asylum Project, was founded in 2013 to democratize access to justice for migrants and forcibly displaced persons in India who face critical legal protection gaps. MAP builds community awareness, provides legal first response, and strengthens institutional accountability to address the impacts of migration, displacement, and gender-based violence (GBV). Since its inception, MAP has assisted more than 25,000 marginalized individuals, including survivors of GBV, torture, and labour exploitation. MAP is a women-led organization, and over 75% of its clients are women.

The Position:
The Project Coordinator – Law and Gender will play a key role in leading and supporting MAP's legal and gender justice programming under the Displacement, Movement and Inclusion (DMI) vertical.

The position will provide legal and technical leadership for programme implementation, capacity building, legal research, strategic interventions, partnerships, advocacy, monitoring, evaluation, learning, and documentation.

Project Management & Oversight
- Provide legal and technical oversight for the implementation of project activities, ensuring interventions are grounded in relevant national and international legal and policy frameworks.
- Represent the organisation at relevant meetings, consultations, conferences and other forums.
- Travel as required for official purposes.

Outreach & Stakeholder Engagement
- Conduct legal capacity-building and training for grassroots justice actors, including paralegal volunteers, lawyers, litigators, community-based justice actors, with a focus on women's rights, access to justice and available legal remedies.
- Design and conduct legal workshops, trainings and awareness sessions.
- Oversee community and stakeholder outreach and engagement.

Partnerships & Networks
- Develop and maintain networks and partnerships with government departments, legal professionals, justice-sector actors, CSOs and CBOs.
- Engage with accountability and redress mechanisms, including relevant UN mechanisms and domestic judicial, quasi-judicial and administrative bodies.

Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning
- Contribute to MEL by developing and tracking relevant legal and programme indicators.
- Maintain high-quality documentation and reporting systems.

Qualifications & Experience
- Graduate degree in Law, with postgraduation in relevant discipline preferred;
- A minimum of 6 to 10 years of work experience; familiarity with Refugee laws pertaining to sexual and gender-based violence and institutional strengthening;
- Experience engaging with marginalised communities and working with grassroots support structures;
- Excellent research, coordination, and documentation skills;
- Understanding of gender-based violence responses and community justice mechanisms;
- High-quality written and verbal communication skills (Hindi and English).
