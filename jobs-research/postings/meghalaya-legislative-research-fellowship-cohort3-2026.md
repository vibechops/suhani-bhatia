MLRF strengthens legislative functioning by providing research and data support to Members of the Meghalaya State Legislative Assembly. Implemented by Meghalaya Institute of Governance with Bharti Institute of Public Policy, ISB.

Research Fellow (Meghalaya Domicile Required): Postgraduate/PhD in relevant fields; strong policy research and analytical abilities; excellent communication; understanding of governance and legislative processes; Meghalaya socio-cultural context preferred. Experience in public policy, research, governance, or social sector; prior policy reports/publications/field projects helpful.

Responsibilities: policy research, briefs, issue notes, legislative documents; support MLAs; Constituency Development Plans; fact sheets/reports; actionable recommendations.

Compensation: ₹50,000 monthly fellowship + HRA; mobile allowance as applicable. Must be domicile of Meghalaya (mandatory).
