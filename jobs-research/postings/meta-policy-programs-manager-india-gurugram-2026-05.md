Policy Programs Manager, India

Meta is looking for a Policy Programs Manager to join our India Policy Programs and Campaigns Team. As part of Meta's Global Public Policy organization, the Programs team is responsible for demonstrating the value of our platform and the investments that we are making to keep people safe across the region. This team focuses on a range of areas including safety and integrity, economic policy, and innovation. You will develop and implement a thoughtful plan to champion the responsible use of technology across India. Through programmatic partnerships with governments, academics, non-profits and other key stakeholders, you will support parents, educators, and community leaders to equip their children and constituents with the skills to navigate technology safely and responsibly. You will engage with policymakers, non-profits and other stakeholders in key dialogues and conversations on emerging areas for collaboration and research. You will also work closely with the policy, communications, marketing and research teams at the company. We are looking for a candidate who can come up with strategic, creative ways to address challenging issues, has great interpersonal and collaboration skills and possess actual organizational skills to manage multiple fast-moving projects simultaneously. This is an extraordinary opportunity to join our growing global public policy team and work on dynamic programs in an exciting, fast-paced environment. This position reports into the Director of Policy, India.

Policy Programs Manager, India Responsibilities:

- Work across the India Campaigns and Programs team to define and build core programs and demonstrate key initiatives with key stakeholders
- Lead programmatic efforts on safety & literacy programs/ campaigns: program development and execution, research projects, partnership management, case study and collateral material development, general program management
- Lead the creation of communications, educational and marketing materials
- Drive ideation, creation and production of events, program elements and collateral materials across a range of subject areas (economic impact programs, integrity programs) for different countries in the region

Minimum Qualifications:

- Bachelors Degree related to Public Policy, Public Affairs or Communications
- 7+ years of relevant work experience, in the field of public policy or public affairs
- Experience deploying policy projects in India
- Experience drafting internal and external material communicating policy issues
- Experience with project management in the policy space and attention to detail
- Demonstrated interpersonal skills to build successful relationships based on collaboration
- Experience prioritizing high-priority projects simultaneously
- Experience working with all levels of seniority, both internally and externally
- Knowledge of tech policy
- Strategic thinker and creative

Preferred Qualifications:

- Experience working on trust and safety programs
- Experience with policy programs or public affairs
- Experience leading on multi-market research projects
- Demonstrated ability to integrate AI tools to optimize/redesign workflows and drive measurable impact (e.g., efficiency gains, quality improvements)
- Experience adhering to and implementing responsible, ethical AI practices (e.g., risk assessment, bias mitigation, quality and accuracy reviews)
- Demonstrated ongoing AI skill development (e.g., prompt/context engineering, agent orchestration) and staying current with emerging AI technologies
