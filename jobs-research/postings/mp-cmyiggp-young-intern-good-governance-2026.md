CM Young Intern for Good Governance Program 2026 (relaunched from earlier Jan Seva Mitra framing). Cabinet-approved scheme to appoint local youths for monitoring government schemes.

Scale: 4,695 interns (15 youths per block across 313 blocks in 55 districts). Duration: 1 year. Honorarium: ₹10,000/month + ₹1,000 travel + ₹200 internet/data (as reported in public vacancy summaries).

Eligibility (as reported from scheme rules / Jan Seva Mitra lineage):
- Native of Madhya Pradesh
- Age 18–29 years
- Graduation or postgraduation; can join within two years of passing the degree

Apply: MP e-Seva / services portal — search “Mukhyamantri Young Interns for Good Governance Programme (CMYIGGP)” after Samagra ID profile registration (eseva.mp.gov.in / services.mp.gov.in). Confirm current open/closed status on portal; treated as closed for this scrape wave relative to district recruitment cycle timing unless portal shows open apply.

Note: Distinct from AIGGPA CM Fellow / CMYPDP PMU specialist recruitment (separate Zoho drive).
