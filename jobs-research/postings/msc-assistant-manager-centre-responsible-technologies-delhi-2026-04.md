 About Us

 MicroSave Consulting (MSC) is a boutique consulting firm that
has, for 25 years, pushed the world towards meaningful financial,
social, and economic inclusion. With over 350 staff of different
nationalities and varied expertise, we are proud to be working in
over 68 developing countries. We partner with participants in
financial services, enterprise, agriculture and health ecosystems
to achieve sustainable performance improvements and unlock enduring
value. Our clients include governments, donors, private sector
corporations, and local businesses. We can help you seize the
digital opportunity, address the mass market, and future-proof your
operations.

Job Description

 Work on high-impact engagements that shape Digital Public
Infrastructure systems at a population scale, influencing how they
serve millions. Your research will directly inform high-stakes
decisions, while you engage closely with senior stakeholders to
solve complex, real-world problems, especially for the poor and
other vulnerable groups.

 The Assistant Manager is expected to play a critical role in the
delivery of different client engagements of MSC. This will broadly
include the following:

Work on the intersection of technology, public policy, and
social development;

Work closely with the government and private sector
stakeholders in India and outside on different facets of technology
policy that may include digital ID and e-KYC, data protection and
privacy, regulatory sandboxes, and digital public goods (DPGs),
etc. ,;

Work on HealthTech, AgTech, and FinTech domains;

Work effectively and collaboratively on consulting engagements on
technology policy with the government and private sector
stakeholders in India and outside. This may include:

Understanding the problem and key requirements, conducting
in-depth issue analysis, framing the practical recommendations,
and, presenting the same to the client and relevant
stakeholders.

Develop high-quality notes, presentations, and reports as
required in the different consulting engagements.

Attend the client and official meetings in India and
outside.

Represent CRT and MSC at different workshops, conferences, and
events in India and outside.

Requirements

 The applicant should have a knack for technology policy that
includes data protection and privacy, digital ID, data sharing, and
regulatory sandboxes. They should also bring excellent
communication, writing, and presentation skills. The incumbent
should also have the ability to lead small assignments under
supervision, build client relationships, and contribute to business
development. Strong attention to detail, field orientation, proven
interpersonal skills, good spoken and written English.

 Educational Qualifications

MBA, master's in public policy, and other related streams. B.
Tech (preferred)
