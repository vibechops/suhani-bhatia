MSCI - ESG Ratings Associate - Financials
Experience: 3 - 6 Years
Location: Mumbai / Pune

Your team responsibilities
The ESG Ratings Operations team members researches and rates companies on a 'AAA' to 'CCC' scale based on its exposure to industry specific ESG risks and its ability to manage those risks relative to peers. The team analyzes data sourced through internal and external data suppliers and follows a rule-based methodology to determine the rating. The companies rated are spread across the developed and emerging markets and range in size from large to small scale.

- Your primary responsibility will be to review and analyze data sourced through internal & external vendors and apply a rules-based methodology to determine Ratings for companies based on its ESG performance. As a ratings analyst, you will focus on specific sectors-we are currently interested in hiring analysts for the BFSI sector. As a sector-specialist, you will be responsible for tracking and monitoring developments and trends in the sector and assessing the ESG impact of those on companies in the sector. Your specific responsibilities shall include:
- Deliver high quality ESG research for coverage on a timely basis.
- Application of quality control measures and coordination with other members of the team / coordinate with internal and external suppliers of the data sourced.

Your skills and experience that will help you excel:
- 3+ years of experience in Research/ Operations of Banks, Insurance companies or Asset Managers. ESG related exposure would be an added advantage.
- Strong analytical skills and ability to articulate viewpoints
- Strong Communication skills-both oral and written
- Comfortable working in a team environment across hierarchies, functions and geographies
- Ability to work under minimum supervision
