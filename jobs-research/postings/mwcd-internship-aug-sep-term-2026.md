PIB Delhi, 4 June 2026: MWCD invited applications for the August–September term under its Internship Programme.

Eligibility (PIB):
- Open to women students, scholars, social activists and teachers enrolled in or associated with any university, academic or non-academic institution
- Women candidates aged 21–40 years from non-tier-I cities and rural areas of India
- Apply online: http://wcd.intern.nic.in/

Programme structure: four terms per FY (May–June, August–September, November–December, February–March), each two months. Applications accepted two months prior to each term between the 1st and 10th of the month. Candidates selected once not eligible for subsequent terms.

For August–September 2026 term, application window was early June (1–10 of the month two months prior). Cycle closed. Rolling portal remains for later terms (see mwcd-internship-programme-rolling.md).
