Ministry of Woman and Child Development (MWCD) invites applications from women students/scholars/social activists/teachers enrolled in/associated with any university/academic/non-academic institution under the Internship Programmes of the Ministry.

Candidates willing to undertake pilot projects/micro-studies focusing on ongoing Ministry activities may apply through the portal. The Ministry provides qualitative exposure to its mandate and programme/policy analysis.

Eligibility:
- Women scholars, students, social activists and teachers from non-tier-I cities and rural parts of India
- Must be enrolled in/associated with any university/academic/non-academic institution
- Age limit between 21 years to 40 years

Selection: Selection Committee; Ministry may invite faculty/subject experts for the selection process.

Terms (PIB, 4 Jun 2026, August–September term notice):
- Four terms per financial year: May–June, August–September, November–December, February–March (each two months)
- Applications accepted two months prior to each term, between the 1st and 10th of the month
- Candidates selected once are not eligible to apply for subsequent terms
- Apply online: http://wcd.intern.nic.in/
