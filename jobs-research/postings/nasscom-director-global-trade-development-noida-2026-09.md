Director - Global Trade Development | nasscom

Support Global Trade Development (GTD) function of NASSCOM to position the collective Indian IT industry as a competitive, innovation driven, and policy responsive partner in global markets, while driving reforms that reduce friction and improve the ease of doing business for member firms worldwide. The role is responsible for driving industry thought leadership, enabling international market expansion, shaping policy and regulatory advocacy, and improving ease of doing business for member companies across the digital and technology ecosystem.

Location, Department Unit and Reporting

Location: Noida
Department: Global Trade Development
Reporting To: Vice President - Global Trade Development

1. Thought Leadership & Sector Positioning

- As part of GTD leadership team, work on a thought-leadership roadmap (innovation, AI, talent) that highlights the economic impact for the industry in key markets globally.
- Work in close coordination on member contributions while ensuring focus on building India as the leading tech innovation ecosystem globally.
- Represent the association at various forums, and policy dialogues and support the Vice President to shape narratives on India's digital services, data-governance approaches, and AI strategy.

2. Global Market Development & Trade Facilitation

- Map priority markets and sectors for Indian IT exports (e.g., US, EU, UK, APAC, GCC, emerging digital-economies) and design joint-go-market initiatives tailored to member capabilities.
- Coordinate with embassies, our missions globally, trade missions, multilateral agencies, and foreign regulators to open doors for Indian IT firms and reduce barriers to cross-border services trade.
- Monitor and advise on rules that affect member firms' global operations including visa regimes, and procurement.

3. Ease of Doing Business & Policy Advocacy

- Support industry-wide working groups with government (India and overseas) to identify and remove regulatory, compliance, and operational bottlenecks for Indian IT exporters.
- Draft and champion policy recommendations, model contracts, and standard positions on topics of focus, ensuring they reflect the collective country and member interest.
- Track and benchmark global "ease of doing business" and digital-trade indicators, feeding insights into advocacy and member-support programs.

4. Stakeholder Engagement & Membership Development

- Engage with member firms, sub-committees, and special-interest groups to surface common pain points, align on positions, and co-create solutions.
- Build strategic alliances with other global trade associations, standards bodies, think tanks, and multilateral platforms to amplify the Indian IT industry's voice.

5. Team & Reporting

- A key position within the GTD leadership team, the position reports to the Vice President & Head GTD. The role entails working with peers and internal and external teams to achieve mutually agreed goals.

Knowledge, Skills, Qualifications, Experience

- Education: Master's degree in economics, business, public policy, international relations, or a closely related field; MBA or advanced degree in technology-policy or digital-economy is preferred.
- Experience: 12-15+ years in technology, consulting, or industry association / trade-body environments, with at least 5 years in senior roles cutting at the intersection of thought leadership, market development and public policy
- Skills:
  - Strong ability to synthesize complex policy and market trends into clear, actionable positions acceptable to a diverse membership.
  - Excellent written and oral communication; experience in high-level public speaking, negotiation, and consensus-building across competitors.
  - Familiarity with trade-association governance.

Desired Attributes

- Deep understanding of global stakeholder engagement (governments, associations, think tanks, analysts), experience of working with various departments of Government of India (Dept of Commerce, MEA, MeitY) and understanding of cross border issues including global digital trade, mobility and procurement.
- Demonstrated track record of building coalitions, consensus-based positions, and successful advocacy campaigns across multiple companies.
- Strategic, diplomatic, and politically astute, with a bias for execution and a clear focus on measurable outcomes for the organisation.

This role is designed for a senior association leader who can bridge member companies, government, and global stakeholders to enhance the competitiveness and ease of doing business for the entire Indian IT ecosystem.
