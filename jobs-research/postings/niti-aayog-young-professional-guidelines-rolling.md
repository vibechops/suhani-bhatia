Distinct from dated 23-opening Consultant/YP cycle (see niti-aayog-yp-consultant-work-for-viksit-bharat-2026-09.md). NITI maintains a rolling resource pool for YP / Consultant Grade 1 / Grade 2 / Senior Consultant.

Portal FAQ (workforbharat.niti.gov.in): register → complete profile → notifications for interviews. Shortlisting considers profiles submitted by the 5th of every month at 5:30 PM. Positions cannot be applied to individually from dashboard listings; one profile matched from pool. Preferences locked after submission (education/experience/documents can be added).

Indian Express / programme summaries for Young Professional:
- Min experience: 1 year
- Upper age: 32 years
- Remuneration: ₹70,000/month
- Eligibility: Master's relevant subject or BE/B.Tech / 2-year PG Diploma in Management / MBBS/LLB/CA/ICWA or other professional degrees of 4+ years post-10+2
- Role framing: assist with policy research, data analysis, and programme execution

Legacy Work for India portal (workforindia.niti.gov.in) similarly describes rolling engagement of Consultants and YPs under 2023 Guidelines PDF.

Apply: https://workforbharat.niti.gov.in - JOIN NITI AAYOG / Register. Admin queries: recruitment-niti@gov.in
