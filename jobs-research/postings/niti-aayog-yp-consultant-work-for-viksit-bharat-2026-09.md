NITI Aayog is hiring Young Professionals and Consultant Grade 1 through the Work for Viksit Bharat resource pool portal. One profile submission is matched across suitable openings. Engagement: 2 years, extendable by one year at a time up to 5 years based on annual performance review.

Vacancy breakdown (23 openings reported for this cycle):
- Atal Innovation Mission (AIM): Young Professional ×10; Consultant Grade 1 ×3
- Communications: Consultant Grade 1 ×2
- Public Finance & Policy Analysis (PFPA): Young Professional ×2; Consultant Grade 1 ×1
- Green Transition, Energy, Climate & Environment: Young Professional ×2
- Mines & Minerals: Consultant Grade 1 ×1; Young Professional ×1
- Water & Land Resources: Young Professional ×1

Essential qualification (both positions): Master's degree in a relevant subject, OR BE/B.Tech, OR 2-year PG Diploma in Management, OR MBBS/LLB/CA/ICWA, OR any professional degree of 4 years or more after 10+2.

Experience:
- Young Professional: at least 1 year
- Consultant Grade 1: 3 to 8 years
- For Ph.D holders, up to 3 years of doctoral period may count as experience (if no other work was done in those years)

Age:
- Young Professional: up to 32 years
- Consultant Grade 1: up to 45 years

Remuneration:
- Young Professional: consolidated ₹70,000/month
- Consultant Grade 1: consolidated ₹80,000–₹1,45,000/month based on experience
- Inclusive of taxes; travel/stay reimbursement for approved official tours; 1.5 days paid leave per completed month

Selection (no written exam): profile submission → Screening Committee shortlist (≥3 candidates per requirement) → Consultancy Evaluation Committee (may interact/interview) → police verification before joining. Shortlisting cycle considers profiles submitted by the 5th of every month at 5:30 PM (portal FAQ).

Apply: https://workforbharat.niti.gov.in (Register → complete profile → submit). Contact: recruitment-niti@gov.in

Responsibilities (as summarized for the pool): support policy research and analysis; prepare reports/briefs/presentations; help design, track and monitor schemes/projects; work with data for evidence; coordinate with ministries/states/stakeholders; contribute to strategy for priority areas.
