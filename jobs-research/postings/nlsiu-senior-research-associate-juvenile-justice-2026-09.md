NLSIU invites applications for one (01) full-time contractual Senior Research Associate based at the NLSIU campus in Bengaluru. Appointment for one year, extendable based on performance and project requirements.

The Senior Research Associate leads the Juvenile Justice Clinic’s research strategy and ensures timely completion of planned research outputs.

Responsibilities include: plan and execute research agenda and outputs; identify critical issues in the juvenile justice system and develop policy positions, research reports, policy briefs and scholarly outputs; publish academic articles, policy briefs, blogs; oversee drafting of legal opinions and review national and state policies on juvenile justice.

Experience: 5-7 years of research experience including design and conceptualisation of research projects in human rights, child rights, or allied areas; experience supervising or mentoring research assistants; experience on human rights and child protection systems in India; excellent writing, analytical and communication skills; strong command of legal research databases and data collection/management/analysis; ability to analyse large datasets; maintain research trackers and documentation.
