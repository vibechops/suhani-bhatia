 Consultant – Skills & Workforce Development 
 Job Description 
   
 Location: New Delhi, India 
   
 Reports To: Associate Director, Renewable Energy 
   
 Years of Directly Related Experience : 5 – 7 years of relevant experience 
   
 Education Requirement : Master's degree Renewable Energy, Energy Management, Public Policy, Management, Economics, or another relevant discipline. 
   
 Duration : The position is for an initial period of one (1) year, with the possibility of extension for an additional year based on performance, project requirements, and mutual agreement. 
   
 About NRDC India: 
 
 NRDC India works to accelerate India's clean energy transition by advancing evidence-based policies, building partnerships, and supporting innovative solutions that improve energy access, strengthen climate resilience, and promote sustainable economic growth. 
   
 The consultant will work to strengthen India's clean energy workforce ecosystem and ensure that the country's skill development programmes are aligned with the rapidly evolving needs of the renewable energy sector. 
 
 
   
 
 
 Position Summary: 
 
 NRDC India is seeking a dynamic and motivated professional to support in strengthening the renewable energy skilling ecosystem in India. 
   
 The consultant will play a central role in reviewing existing skill development programmes, identifying emerging workforce requirements, supporting modernization of training curricula, and facilitating stronger collaboration between government, industry, academia, training institutions, and development partners. 
   
 The suitable candidate should possess a sound understanding of the renewable energy sector across the entire project lifecycle-including manufacturing, project development, financing, construction, commissioning, operations, and maintenance-and be able to translate evolving industry requirements into actionable recommendations for workforce development. 
 The role requires a proactive individual with excellent communication and stakeholder engagement skills who can independently lead assignments while working effectively with diverse stakeholders. 
   
 Key Responsibilities 
   
 Skills Development and Workforce Planning 
 
 Support in strengthening policies, programmes, and institutional mechanisms related to renewable energy skills and workforce development. 
 Support the review of existing renewable energy training programmes, curricula, competency standards, and certification frameworks by engaging relevant stakeholders to identify gaps, overlaps, and opportunities for improvement, and provide recommendations to inform updates by the relevant government agencies. 
 Recommend modernization of training programmes to reflect current industry practices, emerging technologies, and future workforce requirements. 
 Support development of frameworks for continuous updating of renewable energy training programmes in consultation with industry and academic institutions. 
 Contribute to long-term workforce planning for India's clean energy transition. 
 
   
 Industry and Stakeholder Engagement 
 
 Engage with renewable energy developers, manufacturers, EPC companies, technology providers, financial institutions, industry associations, Sector Skill Councils, training providers, and academic institutions to understand current and future workforce needs. 
 Build partnerships that strengthen collaboration between government, industry, and training institutions. 
 Support in organizing consultations, workshops, roundtables, and technical discussions to gather stakeholder inputs and disseminate best practices. 
 
   
 Technical Support 
 
 Track emerging trends and technological developments in the renewable energy sector, including Artificial Intelligence (AI), digitalization, automation, battery energy storage systems, green hydrogen, advanced manufacturing, smart grids, and other innovations influencing workforce requirements. 
 Assess how technological advancements are changing skill requirements across different segments of the renewable energy value chain. 
 Prepare technical notes, policy briefs, presentations, reports, and recommendations to support evidence-based decision-making. 
 
   
 Programme Management 
 
 Support planning, implementation, and monitoring of initiatives undertaken by the Skills Division under the Clean Energy Jobs programme. 
 Coordinate with central and state government agencies, industry partners, development organizations, and academic institutions to facilitate implementation of programme activities. 
 Track progress of assigned activities and prepare periodic progress reports and implementation updates. 
 
   
 Knowledge Management 
 
 Conduct research on domestic and international best practices related to renewable energy workforce development and skill ecosystems. 
 Develop knowledge products, case studies, briefing notes, and communication material to support programme implementation. 
 Support dissemination of project outcomes through workshops, publications, and stakeholder engagement activities. 
 
   
 Qualifications 
 
 Master's degree Renewable Energy, Energy Management, Public Policy, Management, Economics, or another relevant discipline. 
 Additional qualifications or certifications in renewable energy, workforce development, project management, or emerging technologies will be an advantage. 
 
   
 Experience 
 
 5–7 years of relevant professional experience in renewable energy, clean energy transition, workforce development, manufacturing, project development, consulting, programme management, or related sectors. 
 Experience working with government agencies, industry, consulting firms, development organizations, research institutions, or international organizations, with demonstrated experience in stakeholder engagement and programme coordination. 
 Experience in renewable energy skill development, vocational education, training programmes, curriculum development, competency frameworks, or workforce planning will be an advantage. 
 Preference will be given to candidates with experience in one or more of the following areas: 
 
 
 Renewable energy manufacturing and industry partnerships 
 Solar, wind, battery energy storage, green hydrogen, or other clean energy technologies 
 Skill development and vocational education 
 Curriculum development and competency/qualification frameworks 
 Government advisory assignments 
 Workforce analytics, labour market assessments, or workforce planning 
 Programme management and implementation 
 
   
 Required Knowledge and Competencies 
 The successful candidate should demonstrate: 
   
 Technical Knowledge 
 
 Strong understanding of the renewable energy sector in India. 
 Good understanding of the renewable energy project lifecycle, including manufacturing, supply chains, project development, financing, engineering, construction, commissioning, operations, and maintenance. 
 Familiarity with emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), digitalization, automation, advanced analytics, and their impact on future jobs and skills. 
 Understanding of India's renewable energy policy landscape and clean energy ecosystem. 
 
 Professional Skills 
 
 Strong analytical and research capabilities. 
 Excellent written and verbal communication skills. 
 Strong presentation and facilitation skills. 
 Excellent stakeholder engagement and relationship management skills. 
 Ability to synthesize technical information into practical policy and programme recommendations. 
 Strong organizational and project management skills. 
 Ability to manage multiple assignments simultaneously and meet deadlines. 
 
 Personal Attributes 
 
 Self-driven and proactive. 
 Demonstrated leadership and initiative. 
 Ability to work independently with minimal supervision. 
 Strong interpersonal and collaboration skills. 
 Adaptable and comfortable working in a dynamic, fast-paced environment. 
 High level of professionalism, integrity, and commitment to excellence. 
 
   
 Expected Outcomes 
 
 The consultant will support in achieving the following outcomes: 
 Strengthened institutional framework for renewable energy skills development. 
 Updated and industry-aligned renewable energy training programmes. 
 Enhanced engagement between government, industry, academia, and training institutions. 
 Improved understanding of emerging workforce requirements across the renewable energy sector. 
 Development of policy recommendations, knowledge products, and implementation frameworks that support India's clean energy workforce transition.
