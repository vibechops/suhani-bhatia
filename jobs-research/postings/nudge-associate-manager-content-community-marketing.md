Associate Manager, Content & Community, Marketing | The/Nudge
Location: Bengaluru, Karnataka | Full-time

About The/Nudge
The/Nudge is an action institute building resilient livelihoods to alleviate poverty by working with women, farmers, tribals and youth across rural development, agriculture, skilling and economic inclusion. It partners with governments, corporates, philanthropists and social entrepreneurs to create large-scale social impact across India.

The Role
The/Nudge is looking for an Associate Manager, Content & Community, Marketing to create compelling content and build engaging digital communities across multiple platforms. This full-time role involves developing content for campaigns, social media, websites, presentations and videos while leveraging AI tools, experimenting with new formats and collaborating with cross-functional teams to strengthen The/Nudge's brand and storytelling.

Responsibilities include:
- Create content for campaigns, social media, websites, presentations and videos.
- Publish and optimise content across LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube and other platforms.

Requirements:
- 5–9 years of experience in content marketing, digital marketing, social media or creative roles.
- Excellent writing, editing and communication skills.
- Familiarity with AI tools and modern content workflows.
- Experience using Canva or similar design platforms.
- Strong understanding of social media platforms and audience engagement.

Benefits noted on listing: Learning and development support through an L&D wallet and individual development plans; collaborative and people-first work culture; opportunity to work on high-impact campaigns and storytelling initiatives; exposure to India's leading social entrepreneurs, governments and development partners.
