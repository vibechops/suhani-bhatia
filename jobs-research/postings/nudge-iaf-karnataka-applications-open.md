The/Nudge Indian Administrative Fellowship is an 18-month impact fellowship for socially conscious senior professionals to partner with visionary civil servants to strategize and enable technological innovation in public systems.

Programme page note (scraped 2026-09-17): Applications for the Karnataka cohort — page states applications open; one banner also references applications close on Dec 18 (confirm on The/Nudge IAF page before applying).

Karnataka third cohort focus areas (from IAF Karnataka '25 cohort page): Commerce & Industries, Rural Development, Horticulture, Tourism, Health, and Women and Child Development (earlier ET Government coverage also cited Rural Development, Horticulture, WCD, Health & Family Welfare, Commerce and Industries).

As a Fellow (engaged as Consultant with The/Nudge Institute): based at the department office; work closely with senior bureaucrats (ACS / Principal Secretary / equivalent) on strategic projects; support pilot/on-ground implementation.

Fit statement from programme: if passionate about solving for India and have at least 15 years of experience leading large-scale transformations, this Fellowship will be a great fit.

Outcomes cited on site include funds mobilised and programme partners impacted (see current site figures). News: Karnataka Government and The/Nudge Launch Third Cohort (April 7, 2025 / ETGovernment).
