Part-time lawyer role advancing access to justice for women and children. Provide legal representation in cases referred through Nyaaya’s community-based legal empowerment programme; independent litigation with grassroots organisations across Bengaluru.

Responsibilities: civil/criminal matters relating to women and children; cases from community programme and Fellows; counselling and litigation strategy; mentor Samvidhaan Fellows; coordinate with partners and authorities; maintain case records and trackers.

Eligibility: LL.B.; Bar Council enrollment; 3-4 years litigation, legal aid, or rights-based practice. Kannada and English essential; Hindi advantageous.

Remuneration: INR 20,000–30,000. Deadline extended to 10 Sep 2026. Closed as of scrape.
