After running 5 successful cohorts of the Samvidhaan Fellowship in Bihar and Karnataka, we are delighted to announce that Nyaaya is launching the Fellowship in Maharashtra.

This one-year paid fellowship is designed for district-level lawyers committed to improving last-mile access to justice for women and children through full-time or part-time (24 hrs a week) engagement. Beginning with interventions across Mumbai, Mumbai Suburbs, Thane, and Palghar, we will gradually expand to other districts of Maharashtra.

The Fellowship will support community-based legal awareness, individual client counselling, strategic litigation, and rights-based interventions.

Eligibility: Lawyers with at least three years of litigation or community work experience, along with proficiency in Marathi, are encouraged to apply.

Apply now by filling out the application form here: https://lnkd.in/dkdb2mXY
