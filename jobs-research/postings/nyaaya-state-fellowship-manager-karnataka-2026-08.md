Nyaaya is an open-access digital resource providing simple, actionable, reliable legal information so citizens know their rights and can seek justice.

Role: Anchor the Samvidhaan Fellowship in Karnataka. Lead Fellows, oversee litigation and legal aid portfolio, build partnerships with lawyers and CSOs, ensure interventions deliver outcomes for underserved communities. Work with Access to Justice Lead, mentors and stakeholders.

Eligibility: Undergraduate law degree; minimum 4 years in litigation, legal aid, programme management, or related field. Skills: English and Kannada; litigation/court/legal aid ecosystems; team leadership and mentoring; stakeholder management; planning and reporting; MS Office/Google Workspace.

Location: Full-time Bangalore; occasional travel. Remuneration: INR 70,000–80,000 depending on experience.

Original deadline 30.08.2026; extended to 10 September 2026 (Legal Maestros). Apply via Vidhi/Nyaaya online portal.
