Assistant Consultant (India)
Oxford Policy Management (OPM)
New Delhi, India

OPM India is OPM’s largest international presence. We have had a full time office in Delhi for seven years, supported by regional offices in 6 states and a full time team of over fifty staff, who work across multiple projects, and scores of project staff who work full time on one specific assignment.

We are seeking talented and dynamic individuals to join our team at Assistant Consultant level. If you are passionate about working in development and public policy; are at the start of your career and / or have some years of relevant work experience in India or internationally these roles are appropriate for you.

Assistant Consultants contribute to OPM India’s efforts to influence policy reform and reduce poverty and disadvantage at the national and regional level through a range of analytical and problem-solving functions. They help both to deliver projects and to build our architecture for improved impact – through strategy, impact measurement and knowledge management. They travel the country and have the opportunity to contribute to policy research and reform, working in teams with senior colleagues. One week might involve working in Delhi with senior colleagues to design a questionnaire, another may involve supervising a field team in rural Bihar, while another will see them using STATA to clean the data and analyse the results. As they move towards a consultant role, they may take on responsibility for parts of projects and are expected to add to their record of publications.

Assistant Consultant: Key Qualifications
- Strong technical and analytical skills. Ability to conduct analysis in NVIVIO, STATA and other similar packages would be an advantage
- Relevant discipline (e.g. development studies, social development, social anthropology, political science or other appropriate social science discipline).
