Research & Outreach Associate

Pahlé India Foundation (PIF)

Location: Delhi (Hybrid)
Organization: Pahlé India Foundation (PIF)
Position Type: Full-time

About Pahlé India Foundation

Pahlé India Foundation (PIF) is a leading public policy think-and-action tank that works at the intersection of research, policy implementation, and partnerships to drive India's inclusive development agenda. PIF collaborates with government, industry, philanthropy, and knowledge partners to design evidence-based solutions that foster a Viksit Bharat.

Role Overview

Pahle India Foundation (PIF) is looking for a bright, versatile Research & Outreach Associate to support its corporate and government partnerships function. The role sits at the intersection of research, communications, and relationship-building, supporting the team in strengthening ties with existing corporate donors and funders, identifying and reaching out to new partners, and helping convert relationships into funded projects. The ideal candidate is a sharp generalist with strong research and writing ability, comfortable working across market research, outreach, events, and communications.

Market Research

- Conduct research on sectors, companies, and CSR/funding trends to identify prospective corporate partners and funding opportunities.
- Track regulatory, policy, and industry developments relevant to PIF's partnership and advisory work.
- Prepare briefing notes, landscape scans, and sector mapping documents to inform outreach strategy.

Funder Outreach & Partnerships

- Support outreach to existing and prospective corporate donors, funders, and partners.
- Assist in building and maintaining a pipeline of potential partners and funding leads.
- Coordinate meetings and follow-ups with corporate and government stakeholders.
- Provide research and logistical support in developing partnerships into funded project proposals.

Communications

- Draft concept notes, proposals, grants, presentations, and correspondence for partners and funders.
- Support the development of clear, polished written and visual communication material for PIF's Partners & Stakeholders

Events

- Assist in planning and coordinating partner meetings, roundtables, workshops, and other outreach events.
- Manage logistics, invitee coordination, and documentation for events.

Report Writing

- Draft donor updates, progress reports, meeting minutes, and internal documentation.
- Ensure accuracy, clarity, and consistency in all written outputs.

Social Media

- Support PIF's social media presence by translating dense reports into digestible and accurate content and helping maintain visibility of partnership work and events.
- Track engagement and help ensure consistent messaging across platforms.

Data management

- We are recruiting for both the Corporate Advisory & CoDED vertical. Working in CoDED will require you to have some proficiency with organising & managing central datasets, relevant to CoDED's economic and policy research
- Maintaining data integrity across large data-sets

Qualifications & Experience

- 0–4 years of relevant work experience.
- Educational background in Economics, Public Policy, Mass Communications, English Literature & Journalism are all eligible to apply

Skills & Attributes

- Excellent written and verbal communication skills with ability to write clearly and persuasively.
- Is able to keep an open line of communication with reporting managers.
- Strong research and analytical ability, with attention to detail.
- Proactive and comfortable with external-facing outreach and relationship-building.
- Is flexible and well-organised with the ability to manage multiple tasks (research, events, reporting) simultaneously.
- Interest in and willingness to develop sector-specific and policy knowledge.
- Proficiency with Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets & Slides) and familiarity with social media platforms (Linkedin, X)
- A sharp, curious, and self-driven individual who takes initiative and learns quickly.

What We Offer

- Direct mentorship from and close working relationship with senior leadership.
- Exposure to high-level national and international stakeholders.
- A collaborative, mission-driven team environment.
- Competitive compensation aligned with experience.
