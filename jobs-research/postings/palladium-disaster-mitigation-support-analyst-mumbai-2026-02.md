Disaster Mitigation Support
Palladium: Make It Possible
2 - 5 years
Mumbai
Posted: 05/02/2026

Project name:
PMU for Technical Support and Implementation of Comprehensive Disaster Planning, Management & Response, Government of Maharashtra

Project Overview
Palladium has been appointed as the Project Management and Technical Support Facility to the Government of Maharashtra for a 4-year period. Palladium will implement a first of its kind comprehensive approach in India towards addressing all the aspects of disaster management including Preparedness, Mitigation, Response, Relief and Rehabilitation.

The job profile of a Disaster Mitigation Support (Analyst) would involve the following responsibilities:
- Physical and financial progress monitoring of approved projects, including tracking of tenders.
- Creating & maintaining MIS tracker for the above works
- Collating monthly progress reports based on reports received from various implementing agencies
- Coordination and Collaboration:
- Serving as a focal point for coordination and collaboration among different departments, agencies, and organizations involved in disaster management within the district.
- Maintaining effective communication channels, facilitating regular meetings and consultations, and promoting partnerships to enhance the overall resilience of the district.
- Reporting and Information Management:
- Overseeing the collection, analysis, and dissemination of relevant data and information related to disaster management within the district.
- Maintaining databases, producing situation reports, and ensuring timely and accurate information flow to support decision-making processes.
- To identify and prepare the compendium of good practices of the districts and share with the department.
- Create detailed reports and summaries based on project progress, fund utilisation and tender status.
- Miscellaneous:
- To perform other duties as may be assigned by PMU & SDMA from time to time.

Essential Requirements
1. Understand, Speak, Read and Write in Marathi language.
2. Basic knowledge of project management and tenders for construction related works.
3. Strong understanding of project monitoring (physical and financial monitoring).
4. Experience with MIS/dashboards - development and implementation.
5. Experience of consulting of more than 2 years.
