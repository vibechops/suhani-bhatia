Nature of employment: Full-time Reporting to: Network Coordinator Location: New Delhi, Delhi About Peepul 6.5 million students. 291,000 teachers. 90,000+ schools. That’s the scale of what Peepul is changing — and this role sits at the centre of it. Peepul is an education non-profit working with state governments in Delhi and Madhya Pradesh to drive systemic reform at scale. We’ve received international recognition — including the Commonwealth Education Award for Innovation — and are backed by India’s leading CSR funders and global partners. Our model is simple: embed, deliver, measure anchored in system-level change, in partnership with governments. We are a growing organisation with an ambitious strategy and a strong evidence base. Join us in our mission to create a world where every child has equal access to high-quality education. Learn more about our transformative work at http://peepulindia.org Programme Overview The Exemplar Schools were Peepul’s very first intervention, serving as the foundation for all subsequent programmes and demonstrating what effective public education could look like. Launched in 2017, Peepul partnered with the South Delhi Municipal Corporation to turn around an under-resourced government school in Lajpat Nagar through a Public–Private Partnership (PPP). Over time, Peepul took on two additional schools in Delhi, creating a network of three Exemplar Schools. Today, these schools serve nearly 1,500 students and act as beacons of excellence and proof points for scale. They continuously inform and guide Peepul’s system-level programmes by demonstrating what an effective, inclusive, and joyful learning environment looks like in practice. About the Role The Academic Coordinator will lead academic excellence and daily school functioning, acting as a key academic and operational leader. The role focuses on improving student learning outcomes through teacher coaching, curriculum design, student support, school leadership, and stakeholder engagement in partnership with the Network Coordinator and Government School Leadership. They will contribute to network-level academic decision-making, alignment, and continuous improvement, and will collaborate closely with Academic Coordinators across the school network to standardize practices and share learnings. Responsibilities would include (but not limited to): Teaching and Learning • Coach and mentor teachers to strengthen curriculum and pedagogical practices through classroom observations, feedback, and professional development. • Design and deliver teacher training modules based on classroom needs and data insights. • Monitor teacher attendance and instructional quality, ensuring high classroom standards. • Leverage student assessment dashboards and data systems to monitor learning outcomes, identify trends, and guide instructional interventions and teacher support. • Support curriculum documentation and alignment across the school network. • Take ownership of at least one subject in any grade, of their choice as a primary teaching responsibility and step into classrooms when required to support struggling students, challenge high performers, or cover teacher absences. • Share best practices, tools, and learning from the school to inform network-wide improvement. Student responsibilities • Lead pastoral care and promote holistic student development through mindful and inclusive practices. • Ensure students make grade-level progress or demonstrate accelerated growth if below grade level. • Plan, administer, and review student assessments. • Drive enrolment, attendance, and retention targets. • Ensure strict adherence to Child Protection Policy and safeguard student well-being. • Support inclusive education practices for students with special educational needs. Leadership and Management • Co-manage school operations with the MCD Government Principal. • Develop academic calendars, school development plans, timetables, reports, and assessment reviews. • Line manage the school staff and support performance management processes. • Cultivate a positive school culture with consistent routines, high expectations, and respectful relationships. • Build partnerships with parents, community members, and external stakeholders. • Maintain strong relationships with government officials and support capacity-building of government staff.
