Position: Coordinator: FPO Value Chain Development
Last Date of Application: 25th August, 2026 (PRADAN site); related mirror also cited 25 June 2026 for an earlier cycle
Location: Madhya Pradesh

Responsibilities (from public JD mirrors):
- Engage with Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) in the region to establish market linkages for farm produce, supporting these FPOs to attain an average annual turnover of at-least INR 2 Cr.
- Establish & oversee related FPO enterprise operations
- Foster connections of FPOs with major distant markets/mandi players to enable bulk sales of raw produce
- Ensure price transparency, and support logistical arrangements
- Identify and establish B2B, retail and other business opportunities for value-added products developed by FPOs/FPCs
- Prepare a business development proposal and detailed road maps for mobilizing resources and its management
- Setting systems for input procurement and distribution, output aggregation, and training farmers on primary processing such as grading, sorting, and packaging
- Establishing system and processes for updating MIS and accounts, statutory audits, and finance management
- Facilitating process and develop systems for regular board meeting, statutory compliance and other governance procedures

Eligibility:
- BSC / MSc Agriculture or Allied, MBA in Agribusiness/Rural Management / Rural Development or Equivalent
- Minimum 3 years of relevant experience in Agri. Marketing/ Value chain Development / Business Development/ management and working with FPOs/ Large scale Farmers Groups or SHGs Program etc. Experience of working with women's groups preferred
- Profound understanding of Agri-commodity value chain & market determinants
- Knowledge & experience of statutory compliance management in FPOs
