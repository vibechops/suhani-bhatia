Employment Facilitator
Professional Assistance for Development Action (PRADAN)
Number of Vacancies: 1
Location: Bhubaneswar, Odisha (with travel across the state)
Apply by: 1st May 2026

Roles and Responsibilities:
- Build linkages with diverse stakeholders to sensitize them about the Youth Skilling Program.
- Support district teams in establishing placement and vocational training linkages.
- Organize district-level job drives by mobilizing potential employers and HR agencies.
- Design and deliver capacity-building programs on skilling and job readiness.
- Develop strategies for achieving placement targets of 5 districts in coordination with the central team.
- Identify and onboard different job opportunities for young women within Odisha through visiting different companies, HR agencies, and related stakeholders across Odisha.
- Intensify efforts to secure placements across diversified job roles within Odisha.
- Accompany candidates during job joining and vocational training placements, both within and outside Odisha, as needed.
- Maintain regular contact with candidates in training, those placed in jobs, training institutes, and employers to ensure sustained engagement.
- Create and maintain a database of job opportunities, updating real-time information in coordination with district teams.
- Support candidate tracking, post-placement support, and helpdesk systems.
- Ensure effective MIS, reporting, documentation, and convergence with government programs.
- Undertake any other assignment as assigned by the project team.

Eligibility Criteria Qualifications and Experience:
- Postgraduate degree in Rural Management, MBA, Development Studies, Economics, or related fields.
- 2 years of experience in employment promotion, skill development, or rural livelihoods development initiatives.

Skills and Competencies Required:
- Strong understanding of rural employment ecosystems and formal employment market dynamics, particularly for rural women.
- Strong facilitation and coordination skills.
- Ability to effectively engage with multi-stakeholders.
- Proficiency in Odia speaking and willingness for frequent mobility.

Proposed Salary Range: INR 35,000 – 40,000/- monthly CTC
Nature of Job: 1-year fixed-term contract, extendable
