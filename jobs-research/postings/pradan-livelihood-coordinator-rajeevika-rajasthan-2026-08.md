Livelihood Coordinator
Professional Assistance for Development Action (PRADAN)

Roles and Responsibilities:
- Support the district unit of Rajasthan State Rural Livelihoods Mission (Rajeevika) in implementing and achieving programme activities and objectives respectively.
- Coordinate and strengthen women’s collectives by facilitating livelihood promotion activities integrated with livelihood asset creation interventions
- Plan and facilitate capacity-building programmes for women’s collectives, community cadres, field staff, and other stakeholders, while providing regular field-based handholding support.
- Establish and demonstrate sustainable livelihood models within Cluster Level Federation (CLF) areas in collaboration with Rajeevika and CLF staff.
- Promote convergence with government schemes with community institutions for effective implementation of project activities.
- Prepare monthly work plans, progress reports, and project documentation in alignment with the district annual action plan, and ensure timely collection, analysis, and reporting of project data.

Eligibility criteria Qualifications and Experience:
- Full-time degree in Agriculture/Horticulture/Livestock/ Agriculture Engineering or related fields
- At least 2 years’ work experience in social/ aligned theme

Required Skills and Competencies:
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
- Good facilitation skills for working with community groups, women's collectives, and other community institutions.
- Proficiency in written and spoken Hindi and English.
- Good working knowledge of computers, including MS Office (Excel, Word, and PowerPoint) and email communication.
- Ability and willingness to work in remote rural areas and engage closely with rural communities through participatory approaches.
- Comfortable with extensive field travel
