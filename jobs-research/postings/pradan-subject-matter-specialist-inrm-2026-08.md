Subject Matter Specialist (INRM)
Professional Assistance For Development Action (PRADAN)
Apply By: 10 Aug 2026

Responsibilities:
- Coordinate with line departments at the district level to mobilize resources for effective implementation of INRM (Integrated Natural Resource Management) activities to achieve project outcomes.
- Assist technical personnel at Gram Panchayat, Block, and District levels in preparing Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) and prioritizing key INRM activities.
- Facilitate integration of watershed principles in planning and implementation under VB-G RAM G and other convergence programs

Eligibility criteria Qualifications and Experience:
- B. Tech in Civil, Natural Resource Management, Agriculture, Diploma in Civil or any relevant discipline with experience.
- 2 years of experience in NRM / watershed or rural livelihood programs

Skills and Competencies:
- Strong technical knowledge of Integrated Natural Resource Management (INRM) principles, including design and implementation of NRM structures.
- Sound understanding and hands-on experience in rural livelihood promotion, with a focus on sustainable practices and community driven approaches.
- Knowledge about government schemes and programs, including convergence with line departments to enhance program effectiveness.
- Skilled in preparation of Village Development Plans (VDPs), Detailed Project Reports (DPRs), and technical designs for natural resource management interventions.
- Demonstrated expertise in community mobilization, capacity building, and training, along with effective field-level implementation of NRM activities.
