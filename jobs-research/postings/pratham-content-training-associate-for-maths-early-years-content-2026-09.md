About Pratham
Pratham Education Foundation (Pratham) is one of the largest education NGOs in India with a record of innovative, rigorously evaluated programs that have inspired similar programs across three continents. With a history spanning 30 years, Pratham’s work today extends from programs for early and elementary years to girls and women, and youth skilling. Through programs covering 25 states and Union Territories, in an average year, Pratham usually reaches over 6 million children and youth. This is achieved through a combination of efforts: directly working with children and youth in communities, as well as through collaborations with state and district-level governments.
Pratham has received notable awards such as the Lui Che Woo Prize, WISE Prize for Innovation, Skoll Award for Social Entrepreneurship, the Henry R Kravis Prize in Leadership and the CNN-IBN Indian of the Year for Public Service. Pratham also received the 2021 Yidan Prize for Education Development and the Yashraj Bharati Samman in 2025.

About Math’s in Early Years
Pratham’s intensive work in the field of early childhood education for the past 30 years shows that children have an innate ability to understand Mathematical concepts such as estimation, approximation, comparison, etc. much before they come to school. We, at Pratham, intend to make use of the existing knowledge that children carry and strengthen it to build their foundational Math skills, particularly in the preschool years and early grades. The early years at Pratham comprise the age group of 3 to 8 years.
Pratham’s work in Mathematics includes but is not limited to:
Developing compelling course content and engaging learning materials for children.
Building and reinforcing teachers’ capacities via online and offline workshops.
Conducting research based on best pedagogical practices for Mathematics.
Testing content feasibility, tracking field progress, and documenting program outcomes.
Supporting the roll out of the content on the ground and identifying gaps and required course correction on a regular basis.
Role Description
The Early Years team in Pratham is seeking a Content & Training Associate who can support the creation and capacity building of educational content for Math for learners aged 3-8 years. We are looking for applicants with some experience in teaching Math to young learners. Applicants proficient in creating content using digital tools such as MS Word, and Google Suite will be preferred.
Key Responsibilities
Review textbooks and educational materials used in government schools and in Pratham for teaching Math by evaluating their content, structure, and instructional effectiveness.
Contribute to the development of creating activities, stories, poems, worksheets, and games related to Math.
Collaborate with other content developers, trainers, and teachers across various states at Pratham to brainstorm, research, and integrate pedagogical trends and best practices in Math.
Contribute to the development of high-quality training material to train educators teaching in low-resourced settings, ensuring alignment with our learning outcomes and standards.
Build capacities of teams across central and field locations through training.
Support relevant data collection and analysis to improve the design and implementation of projects.
Engage with government stakeholders and support Pratham’s state teams to plan & implement existing projects.
Support with communication efforts to improve the visibility of Pratham’s early years program – events, collaborations, conferences, advocacy, social media etc.
Support documentation of early years programs for internal & external stakeholders.
Perform additional tasks, as required, by the management.
Requirements
Bachelor’s or Master’s degree program in a relevant field.
1-2 years of work experience in the education sector.
Understanding of Maths and its related concepts for the 3-8 years age group.
Proficiency in English and Hindi language, with excellent listening and comprehension skills.
Strong organisational and time management skills, with the ability to prioritise tasks.
Excellent interpersonal and communication skills and ability to work with teams.
Adaptable and able to work in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
Passionate about promoting social change through education.
Willingness to travel is a must.
