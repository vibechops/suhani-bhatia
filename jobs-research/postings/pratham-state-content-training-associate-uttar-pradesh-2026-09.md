About Pratham
Pratham Education Foundation (Pratham) is one of the largest education NGOs in India with a record of innovative, rigorously evaluated programs that have inspired similar programs across three continents. With a history spanning 30 years, Pratham’s work today extends from programs for early and elementary years to girls and women, and youth skilling. Through programs covering 25 states and Union Territories, in an average year, Pratham usually reaches over 6 million children and youth. This is achieved through a combination of efforts: directly working with children and youth in communities, as well as through collaborations with state and district-level governments.
Pratham has received notable awards such as the Lui Che Woo Prize, WISE Prize for Innovation, Skoll Award for Social Entrepreneurship, the Henry R Kravis Prize in Leadership and the CNN-IBN Indian of the Year for Public Service. Pratham also received the 2021 Yidan Prize for Education Development and the Yashraj Bharati Samman in 2025.

About Role
The State Content and Training Associate will be responsible for supporting content development, designing and facilitating training programs, and strengthening the quality of program implementation across the state. The ideal candidate should have prior experience in content development and training.
Role & Responsibilities
The SCTA is responsible for the content training to the field team. Every year, state training organised for field team and SCTA needs to deliver training of Hindi to field team and work on their capacity building.
SCTA Hindi Role is very important in preparing the reports, PPTs, and any other document related work which is important for exploring our program with state government. Program report sharing with State head, Program Head and state government officer’s is very important part of job of SCTA-Hindi.
SCTA will be responsible for dealing all the Hindi content related matter with SCERT & Samagra Shiksha, Raipur, for smooth program implementation. Proofreading of content used and also finalised the TLM required for the program is important part of SCTA job role.
SCTA Hindi needs to travel to the different district, block & clusters of Chhattisgarh to help our district team and meet to district authorities for better program implementation. Monitoring of Program is very important so SCTA job profile includes extensive travelling.
SCTA Hindi is responsible for all the content related reporting, proofreading and content finalisation with the help of the national content team. SCTA will act as a bridge between state & national for content finalisation and training.
SCTA Hindi will report to the state head and Program Head for regular updates of the program. SCTA will act closely with the program head for smooth planning of the program and work accordingly.
Required Qualifications, Skills and Abilities
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills in the local language, including having a good listening ability with an emphatic outlook.
Must have training and counseling skills.
Comfortable with extensive field work and travel.
M.A Hindi / B.A / Graduate degree / Post Graduate Hindi.
Strong command over oral and written Hindi and English language.
