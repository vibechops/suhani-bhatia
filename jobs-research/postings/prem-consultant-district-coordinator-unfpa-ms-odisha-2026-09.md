Organisational Background:
People’s Rural Education Movement (PREM) is a secular, humanitarian, non-political and non-governmental organization working for the development of Adivasi (indigenous), Dalit, Fisher folk and other marginalized communities of Odisha and other states of India. PREM with the technical and financial support of United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA) is working on social empowerment of women through women SHGs of Mission Shakti department across all the districts of Odisha.

About the project
The collaborative project with Dept of Mission Shakti covering all 30 districts, aims to empower members of Women’s Self-Help Groups (WSHGs) by enabling women to lead safer, healthier, and more empowered lives by improving their Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights (SRHR), addressing Gender-Based Violence (GBV), and building their digital and financial literacy. Key objectives include promoting digital and financial skills for socio-economic development, raising awareness of rights and entitlements to health and wellbeing, and fostering partnerships with reputable institutions for knowledge management and technology transfer.

Job Description
The District Coordinator will be based in one of the implementing districts of Odisha and will primarily be responsible for planning, implementing, monitoring, and coordinating project activities at the district and block levels. This will involve working closely with the Department of Mission Shakti, the Department of Women and Child Development (WCD), and the Government of Odisha. Reporting to the State Program Manager (SPM) or any designated officials by PREM, the District Coordinator will also ensure the smooth execution of project interventions by liaising with district-level officials and WSHG federation leaders.
