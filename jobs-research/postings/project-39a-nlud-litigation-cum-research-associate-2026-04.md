JOB POST: Academic Fellow and Litigation Cum Research – Associate at NLU Delhi [Contractual Positions under Project 39A; Salary Upto Rs. 50k]: Apply by April 20

Applications are invited for Academic Fellow and Litigation Cum Research - Associate at NLU Delhi for the year 2026. The last date of application is April 20.

About the Project

Project 39A is one of the most distinguished and impactful centres at the National Law University Delhi (NLUD). Over the years, it has earned national and international recognition for its contributions to access to justice, legal aid, criminal justice reform, and empirical legal research.

The University has recently reconstituted the leadership of Project 39A and is in the process of expanding its team to strengthen its work in research-driven policy, training and capacity-building, and institutional reform within the criminal justice system.

Litigation Cum Research – Associate

Role and Responsibilities:

- Conduct legal and empirical research in areas related to criminal justice and access to justice
- Undertake fieldwork for the purposes of research
- Collate, analyse and publish findings of research
- Assist in preparing reports, policy briefs, and academic outputs
- Contribute to litigation strategy and legal drafting where required
- Brief and assist senior advocates and advocates on record
- Support Project 39A's engagement with courts, institutions, and stakeholders
- Contribute to developing, implementing and expanding Project 39A's research projects and databases related to the death penalty
- Present the work of P39A at appropriate for a. Undertake administrative or any other work that may be necessary to further the aims of Project 39A

Eligibility

- Graduate with a degree in law.
- Familiarity with criminal law and the Indian criminal justice system is a strong requirement.
- Apart from English, proficiency in at least one Indian language is mandatory.

Desirable:

- Prior experience in litigation, legal research, or policy work
- Publications or writing in relevant areas.

How to Apply?

Interested candidates can apply online via the link given at the end of the post.

Salary

- The selected candidates will receive a monthly remuneration funded by Project 39A.
- The selected candidates will receive a consolidated remuneration of INR 50,000 (Rupees Fifty Thousand) per month (subject to TDS deduction @10% under the Income Tax Act).
- The appointment will be for an initial period of one year, with the possibility of extension based on satisfactory performance and the availability of funds under Project 39A.

Deadline

Applications must be submitted by 20 April, 2026.

Fee

The candidates are required to pay non-refundable application fee of Rs.1,000/- (Rupees One Thousand Only) online.
