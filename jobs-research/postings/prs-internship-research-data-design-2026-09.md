Internships at PRS

PRS is a leading research group with the mission to strengthen India's democracy by helping Parliament and state assemblies be more effective. These institutions make laws, hold the governments accountable and allocate public funds. It is important that they have information and analysis to perform their functions well, and that citizens have channels of engaging with their elected representatives.

PRS offers rolling internships throughout the year. The PRS staff consists of a team of dedicated professionals with different areas of expertise, and is broadly divided into the research and outreach departments. While the research team analyses legislative and policy issues of topical interest, the outreach team tracks the working of the legislatures and legislators, manages citizen engagement and is PRS's interface with the MPs and MLAs. A prospective intern can apply for an internship in either/ both teams.

Structure of the internship

The duration of the internship is flexible, but typically lasts between four and eight weeks. Interns are expected to report to office for the internship, and there are no remote work options. Please note that no accommodation or remuneration will be provided to interns. This is strictly an unpaid internship.

PRS's work is guided by issues taken up in Parliament, and interns are expected to work with analysts on various research tasks, or with the outreach team to assist with legislator engagement, media output, or the Student Parliament programme. Based on the intern's specific skills and abilities, they may be assigned work as a (i) research intern, (ii) data intern, or (iii) design intern. (See below for specific skills expected in each category.)

The successful completion of a PRS internship requires the intern to pursue and complete an internship project. Interns may choose a project topic of their liking, in consultation with PRS team members.

Expectations from interns

All interns should have a deep interest in the working of Parliament and the legislative process in India. In addition, an intern should have the following:

- Research interns: (i) analytical and problem solving skills; (ii) strong writing skills; and (iii) proficiency in basic data handling using Microsoft Excel/Google Sheets.
- Data interns: (i) be well-versed with one or more of these programming languages –Python/Java/C++/Javascript; and (ii) have exposure or prior experience in data scraping, wrangling, pre-processing, analysis, and visualisation, and/or applying machine learning techniques.
- Design interns: Basic understanding of design principles and familiarity with visualising data and text. Prior experience working with Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, or similar software will be a plus.

Interested persons can fill in the application form. The form has to be filled out in one session, and there is no option of saving responses. The following documents must be submitted:

- write a short statement of purpose (not more than 500 words)
- upload an up-to-date resume in PDF format
- upload a writing sample on any policy issue (not more than 1000 words) in PDF format, or a relevant design work sample (up to two pages) in PDF format.

Applications will be accepted on a rolling basis throughout the year. Due to the large volume of applications, only shortlisted candidates will be contacted via email. If you are selected, you will be informed three weeks prior to the intended start date.

For any queries, please email us at internship@prsindia.org. Internship applications sent to this email will not be considered.
