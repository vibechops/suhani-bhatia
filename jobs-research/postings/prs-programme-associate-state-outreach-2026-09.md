Programme Associate

PRS is a leading research group with the mission to strengthen India's democracy by helping Parliament and state assemblies be more effective. These institutions make laws, hold the governments accountable and allocate public funds. It is important that they have information and analysis to perform their functions well, and that citizens have channels of engaging with their elected representatives.

PRS is looking to hire Programme Associates to strengthen its outreach activities in state assemblies.

Core responsibilities: As part of the outreach team at PRS, the programme associate/officer will play a key role in strengthening PRS' outreach activities, especially to state legislators and legislatures. He/She will be responsible for collating and maintaining information pertaining to the working of State Assemblies, including session information, legislative performance, Member participation, etc. In addition, he/she will co-ordinate policy workshops and events held from time to time in partnership with institutional partners.

Abilities required: (a) Strong execution skills; (b) Good writing and communication skills; (c) Good quantitative skills with an ability to analyse and represent data accurately; (d) Ability to work independently; (e) Ability to multi-task and deliver quality work in a timely way; (f) Keen interest in and understanding of Indian politics.

Qualifications sought: Bachelor's degree for the role of Programme Associate. A Master's degree or relevant professional degree, or a Bachelor's degree with at least three years of work experience for the role of Programme Officer.

Location: This is a full-time position based in New Delhi, with regular travel to various State Assemblies.

Emoluments offered: Flexible depending on qualifications and ability.

Application details: As part of your application please email the two documents listed below to talent@prsindia.org with the subject line: "Programme Associate" or "Programme Officer", as relevant.

(i) A Cover Letter that addresses a) Why you think PRS is a place you should build a career at, and b) How your qualifications and skills will contribute to the work of PRS;

(ii) an updated CV.
