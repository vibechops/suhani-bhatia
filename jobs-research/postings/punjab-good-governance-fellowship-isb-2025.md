Strategic collaboration between Government of Punjab and ISB (knowledge partner for recruitment, training, professional development). Fellows engaged by Government of Punjab under prescribed terms.

Duration: 2 years, full-time
Positions: 50 (per AdmissionsOn summary)
Stipend: ₹1.2 lakh per month (all-inclusive)
Training/Certification: Capacity building and ISB Executive Education course on Public Policy / Understanding Public Policy in India

Eligibility (aggregated from programme summaries):
- Postgraduate with at least 3 years relevant work experience, OR graduate with at least 4 years professional experience
- AssamJobsCentre listing elaborates postgraduate includes Master's any discipline, MBBS, Degree in Engineering/Technology/Law, CA, PG Diploma (1 or 2 years) from recognised institute/university
- Age between 18 to 35 years (AdmissionsOn)
- Strong motivation for public service and governance challenges

Selection: registration form with personal/professional details; eligible candidates evaluated through written tests and interviews by selection committee.

Deadline: 30 May 2025. Apply historically via isb.edu/punjab-good-governance-fellowship-programme (cycle closed). Contact (historical): ISB Mohali Campus; fellowship email cited on AdmissionsOn.
