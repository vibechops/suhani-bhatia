CMFP engages highly educated youth as Fellows to bring new ideas into Rajasthan state administration. Planning Department is administrative department; DES is implementing agency. Vision: empower young professionals to participate in governance via innovation, critical analysis, and improving programme performance.

FAQ eligibility (cmfp.rajasthan.gov.in/Home/Faq):
- Age not more than 28 years as on 01-01-2026 (not born before 01.01.1998)
- Post Graduation with 65%+ or CGPA 7/10, OR Bachelor's in professional course (Engineering/Medical/CA/CS/Law etc.)
- FAQ also states fellows selected from universities/academic institutions through Campus Placement; selection based on GD & Personal Interview (PIC decides campus selection modality). Criteria: Personality, Attitude, Communication Skills, subject knowledge and character strengths.
- ~50 Fellows; placement in CMO or Department allotted by CMO
- Duration: initially one year, extendable by one more year in blocks of six months

LinkedIn publicity (applications closed 8 Oct 2025 12:00 AM) also cited: stipend ₹60,000/month + ₹5,000 mobility/connectivity; duration 1 year extendable; some posts listed age 25–35 as of April 1, 2025 and different % cutoffs — prefer official FAQ for eligibility where conflict.
