 Field Mobilization Officer (Health & Education) Rs.35,000–Rs.40,000 per month | 9-Month Project | Mumbai & Maharashtra 
   
 Ratnanidhi Charitable Trust (RNCT) is one of India's leading charitable organizations, working in the fields of health, education, disability rehabilitation, vision care, nutrition, community development, and humanitarian services . 
   
 We are looking for a passionate, energetic, and hardworking Field Mobilization Officer to identify, survey, and mobilize beneficiaries for our large-scale programmes across Mumbai and Maharashtra. 
   
 Key Responsibilities 
 
 Conduct field surveys to identify deserving beneficiaries. 
 Mobilize patients and communities for RNCT's health, disability rehabilitation, vision care, nutrition, and education programmes. 
 Build strong relationships with hospitals, NGOs, schools, doctors, government departments, community leaders, and local authorities. 
 Organize awareness meetings and mobilization drives to maximize participation. 
 Coordinate with the RNCT team for successful implementation of outreach camps and projects. 
 Maintain accurate beneficiary records, survey data, photographs, and field reports. 
 Travel extensively across Mumbai and nearby districts of Maharashtra. 
 
 Eligibility 
 
 Bachelor's or Master's Degree in Social Work (BSW/MSW) , Sociology, Rural Development, or a related Social Science discipline. 
 2–3 years of experience in NGO fieldwork, healthcare outreach, community mobilization, or social development. 
 Excellent communication skills in Marathi and Hindi (English preferred). 
 Strong interpersonal, networking, and organizational skills. 
 Comfortable working independently in the field and interacting with diverse communities. 
 Willingness to travel extensively and work in challenging field conditions. 
 Two-wheeler with a valid driving licence will be an added advantage. 
 
 What We're Looking For 
 This is not a desk job . It requires extensive travel, meeting people, conducting field surveys, building relationships with local stakeholders, and ensuring that deserving beneficiaries receive life-changing support. We are looking for someone with empathy, integrity, enthusiasm, and a genuine passion for social service. 
   
 Project Duration 
 9-Month Contract (with the possibility of extension based on project requirements and performance). 
   
 Salary & Benefits 
 
 Rs.35,000–Rs.40,000 per month (depending on qualifications and experience) 
 Travel reimbursement as per organizational policy. 
 Opportunity to work with one of India's leading charitable organizations and create meaningful social impact.

 About Ratna Nidhi Charitable Trust: A Legacy of Impact 
   
 Picture a seed, a humble yet audacious dream planted 25 years ago in the heart of Mumbai. That seed has now grown into Ratna Nidhi, a sprawling tree of hope and transformation, with branches touching lives across India. We are an evolving dynamo built on family values of trust and compassion, aiming to empower communities in various domains. 
   
 Our Partners 
   
 Our work has garnered the support of esteemed organizations that share our commitment to societal betterment. These partnerships amplify our reach and impact, and they include: 
   
 - Google Foundation USA : Supporting our technology initiatives. 
 - Betic-IIT Mumbai : Collaborating on healthcare technology. 
 - World Bank : Strategy alignment for education projects. 
 - Essilorluxotica Foundation France : Providing high-quality vision care and eyeglasses. 
   
 Scale of 4 Key Projects 
   
 1. Mobility Aids : Over the past 20 years, we’ve reached out to more than 2.80 lakh patients, providing mobility aids like the award-winning Ratna Nidhi leg. 
   
 2. Food for Education : Serving freshly cooked meals to 2,000 school children daily across 18 low-income schools in Mumbai from our own kitchen 
   
 3. Mission Million Books : Distributed over 1 million educational books to more than 5,000 schools and colleges across India & training to school children in financial literacy, insurance & climate change 
   
 4. Vision 1 Million : In collaboration with Essilorluxotica Vision Foundation, we manufacture and donate high-quality prescription eyeglasses, benefiting countless school children in Maharashtra.Half a million students screened and 1 million to screen over next 3 years
