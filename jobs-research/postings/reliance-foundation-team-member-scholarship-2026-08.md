About Reliance Foundation

At Reliance Foundation, our vision is to build an inclusive India by pioneering a holistic model to address India’s multifaceted development challenges, and contribute to its collective aspirations. Central to our philosophy is the commitment to enhance the quality of life of people from marginalised and vulnerable communities, by empowering them and catalysing change through innovative and sustainable solutions. Our steadfast endeavour is to create replicable and scalable models of development through an integrated approach in the true Reliance spirit of maximising societal value for all, and making it a movement.

Job Responsibilities

- Develop program branding, marketing materials and digital strategy in close collaboration with the RF Communications team for the Reliance Foundation Scholarships

- Manage applicant outreach and engagement events during open application periods, including the delivery of webinars, workshops, and other events

- Create a database of highly ranked institutes in India that offer programmes relevant to the Scholarship

- Building relations and a long-term partnership with these institutes

- Manage student communications process during all stages of the programme including verification, evaluation and interview processes

- Coordinate updates on the Scholarships website, marketing materials, Q&A Chatbot, and Scholarship documentation yearly as needed

- Manage scholar and alumni engagement initiatives such as expert interactions, soft-skills trainings, networking events, volunteering, internship opportunities etc.

- Identifying experts to conduct interactions & trainings for the RF Scholars

- Liaising with HR teams within Reliance for internship opportunities

- Organising Networking events

- Develop and manage the Scholarship Alumni & Engagement portal

- Lead scholarship ambassadors# strategy and engagement

- Magnify the achievements and stories of RF scholars and amplify the reach of the Scholarships Programme through social media, feature articles in education publications, etc.

- Support the Scholarships team with end-to-end application and selection process as needed
