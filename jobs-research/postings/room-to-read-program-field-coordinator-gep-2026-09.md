Position Overview:
The Program Field Coordinator (PFC) will be responsible for supporting the implementation of Project Saksham across assigned tribal residential hostels within one or more districts. The PFC will work closely with Hostel Wardens, District Coordinators, Assistant Commissioners, and other Tribal Department officials to ensure high-quality implementation of the Life Skills curriculum.
Project Saksham is a collaborative initiative between the Department of Tribal and Scheduled Caste Development, Government of Chhattisgarh and Room to Read India. The project aims to strengthen the life skills, confidence, leadership, gender equality, and decision-making abilities of adolescent girls residing in Tribal Residential Hostels through a structured Life Skills curriculum and capacity-building of hostel warden. The PFC will provide regular mentoring, monitoring, coaching, documentation, and implementation support to hostel wardens while ensuring adherence to Room to Read's quality standards and project timelines. The position will report to the Program Associate/Program Officer. The base location for this position is Surajpur (Chhattisgarh).
Key Responsibilities:
Program Understanding and Preparedness:
Develop a comprehensive understanding of      Project Saksham, its objectives, implementation strategy, and monitoring      framework.
Gain thorough knowledge of the Life      Skills curriculum, Teacher Handbook, facilitator guides, and monitoring      tools.
Support district-level planning of      program activities in consultation with the Program Associate/Officer.
Ensure implementation follows the      approved project timelines and quality standards.
Participate in monthly reviews,      quarterly meetings, refresher trainings, and capacity-building workshops.
Field Implementation and Capacity Building:
Provide      regular handholding support to Hostel Wardens for effective facilitation      of Life Skills sessions.
Conduct      demonstration sessions whenever required.
Observe      Life Skills sessions and provide constructive feedback to wardens.
Support      wardens in annual and monthly planning of Life Skills sessions.
Assist      wardens in integrating Life Skills activities into hostel routines.
Facilitate      refresher trainings and mentoring sessions for wardens.
Support      newly appointed wardens through orientation and induction.
Quality Assurance:
Conduct      regular monitoring visits to assigned hostels as per project benchmarks.
Use      standardized observation tools to assess session quality and fidelity.
Provide      on-site coaching to improve facilitation skills.
Track      implementation indicators and recommend corrective actions.
Ensure      timely completion of Life Skills sessions according to the project      calendar.
Monitor      utilization of Teacher Handbooks and learning materials.
Identify      implementation challenges and coordinate solutions with supervisors.
Monitoring, Reporting and Documentation:
Collect      and verify monthly monitoring data from hostel wardens.
Ensure      timely submission of monitoring formats through the digital monitoring      system/QR-based reporting mechanism.
Maintain      accurate field records, attendance registers, visit reports, and      observation reports.
Document      success stories, case studies, photographs, innovations, and lessons      learned.
Prepare      monthly field reports highlighting achievements, challenges, and support      required.
Support      data validation exercises and periodic assessments.
Government Liaison and Coordination:
Maintain      effective coordination with Assistant Commissioners, District      Coordinators, Hostel wardens, and other Tribal Department officials.
Support      district-level review meetings and government consultations.
Facilitate      official visits by government representatives, donors, and Room to Read      teams.
Represent      Room to Read professionally during district meetings and project events.
Strengthen      partnerships with district administration to support smooth      implementation.
Community and Stakeholder Engagement:
Support      hostel-level events promoting girls' leadership, gender equality, and life      skills.
Facilitate      parent/community engagement activities whenever required.
Encourage      participation of local stakeholders in project initiatives.
Promote      ownership and sustainability of Project Saksham within hostels.
Organizational Responsibilities:
Participate      actively in organizational trainings, review meetings, and planning      exercises.
Adhere      to Room to Read's Child Protection Policy and Code of Conduct.
Maintain      confidentiality and professionalism in all interactions.
Perform      other project-related responsibilities assigned by the supervisor.
Qualifications:
Required:
Bachelor’s      / Master’s degree in social work, Education, Psychology, Sociology, Rural      Development, or related discipline.
Minimum      2–4 years of experience in the education or development sector.
Experience      working with adolescent girls, life skills education, gender, youth      development, or community mobilization.
Strong      communication skills in Hindi and the local language.
Basic      computer proficiency (MS Office, Google Forms, Excel).
Willingness      to undertake extensive field travel with own vehicle within assigned      districts.
Preferred:
Experience      working with Tribal Departments, Education Departments, Kasturba Gandhi      Balika Vidyalayas (KGBVs), Ashram schools, or residential hostels.
Experience      in teacher/warden mentoring and capacity building.
Familiarity      with digital monitoring systems and data collection tools.
Strong      facilitation, coaching, and interpersonal skills.
Ability      to build effective relationships with government stakeholders
