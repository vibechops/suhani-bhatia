About Us:
Rural Organization for Social Advancement (ROSA) is a grassroots non-profit organization committed to empowering rural communities through sustainable development, education, health, and livelihood programs. Founded with a vision to create a just and equitable society, ROSA works closely with marginalized groups, especially women, children, and youth, to promote social inclusion, gender equality, and self-reliance.
With a strong presence in rural areas of UP, ROSA implements community-led initiatives that address issues like education access, women's empowerment, child protection, health awareness, skill development, and environmental sustainability. Through partnerships, innovation, and capacity building, ROSA strives to bring long-lasting change to the lives of the underserved.

Job Description:
We are seeking a motivated and skilled Program Coordinator to join our team. The ideal candidate will be responsible for managing projects, developing reports, handling large team and communication with district level officials etc.
Key Responsibilities:
• Program Planning 
• Program Monitoring
• Training and team management 
Qualifications:
• Master’s Degree in Public Health (MPH), Nutrition, Public Health Nutrition, Community Health, Rural Development, or related field 
• At least 5 Years of experience in Health and Nutrition Project 
• Working computer knowledge & Skills (MS office) 
Age- 25 to 40 Years
Rs. 30000 (Thirty Thousand) Monthly and Local conveyance
