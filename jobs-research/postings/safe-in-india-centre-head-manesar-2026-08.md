Centre Head
Safe in India Foundation
Location: Manesar, Haryana
Apply by: 31 Aug 2026

Safe in India is hiring a Centre Head for our Worker Assistance Centre in Manesar to lead efforts in supporting injured workers, strengthening community outreach, and ensuring workers receive the benefits and dignity they deserve.

Responsibilities (from JD):
1. Manage all activities to run Worker’s assistance centre (WAC) in Gurgaon which include qualitative and quantitative delivery of Paramarsh (assisting workers with ESIC healthcare/compensation) and benefit delivery to the workers.
2. Main WAC activities, other than assisting injured and non-injured workers, include:
   - Quality Management for Data & Process: Ensure quality data collection and error free entry in the IT system (SSP). Accountability for data integrity. Monitor data entry and give feedback for corrections. Shadow team members while doing Paramarsh & file making.
   - Lead and provide direction and guidance to the Field Team and the Assistant Centre Coordinator (ACC) for smooth functioning of WAC activities e.g. Paramarsh, Hitlabh Files, Medical Board, Shramik Chaupal, Support Group Meeting etc.
   - Manage Labour welfare fund scheme related activities at the centre and help workers getting the scheme benefits
   - Coordinate with other centres and other pillar activities and implement them at WAC
3. Build effective relationship: Liaison with ESIC branch offices and Sub regional office, Labour welfare department, and other stakeholders for smooth running of program & case resolution.
4. Community Outreach and Engagement: Conduct outreach activities to reach ESIC registered workers and their families including the residential areas of workers.
5. Advocacy: Identify timely and surface critical issues for advocacy with ESIC at the Branch / Regional / HQ level. Create evidence from the regular work in the form of videos & testimonials.
