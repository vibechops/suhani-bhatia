 Job Description: Program Manager

About Saksham Trust

Saksham Trust is a National Award–winning non-governmental organization dedicated to the empowerment of persons with visual impairment (PVIs). Working at the intersection of advocacy and service delivery, Saksham supports PVIs through rehabilitation, education, training, and access to assistive technology.

Founded nearly 23 years ago by Mr. Dipendra Manocha and Ms. Rummi K. Seth, Saksham is driven by a strong belief that persons with visual impairment can achieve anything-with the right support and inclusive approaches. Today, Saksham operates across multiple locations, including Delhi, Noida, Punjab, and Himachal Pradesh.

Role Overview
The Program Manager will play a key role in planning, implementing, and monitoring projects across Saksham’s programs. The position involves close coordination with internal teams and external stakeholders, along with responsibility for documentation, reporting, and communication outputs.

Key Responsibilities
Plan, implement, and oversee program activities in line with organizational goals
Coordinate with stakeholders including partners, donors, and internal teams
Prepare reports, maintain documentation, and ensure accurate record-keeping
Develop engaging content for social media and communication materials
Support creation of videos and visual content for outreach and advocacy
Assist with additional responsibilities as assigned by the organization
Required Qualifications and Skills
Master’s degree in Social Sciences, Communication, or a related field from a recognized university
Strong documentation and reporting skills with attention to detail
Ability to write clear, engaging content for social media and other platforms
Working knowledge of design tools such as Canva and CorelDRAW
Basic to intermediate skills in video creation and editing
Strong multitasking and time-management abilities
Excellent written and verbal communication skills with strong interpersonal capabilities
Compensation
Salary will be negotiable and commensurate with the candidate’s qualifications and experience.

How to Apply
Interested candidates may send their resumes to arihant@saksham.org or info@saksham.org.
We look forward to receiving your application and exploring how you can contribute to Saksham’s mission.
