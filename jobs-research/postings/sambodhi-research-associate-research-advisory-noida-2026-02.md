# Hiring Alert | Research Associate | Research & Advisory | Sambodhi Research and Communications

Sambodhi Research and Communications is inviting applications for the role of Research Associate to support its research, evaluation, and advisory work.

This opportunity is suited for early-career professionals who want to work at the intersection of evidence generation, field research, and public policy design, while engaging directly with communities and development programmes.

About the Organisation

Sambodhi Research and Communications works on monitoring, evaluation, and advisory assignments for governments, donors, foundations, and corporates across sectors such as public health, nutrition, environment, sanitation, and development interventions.

Role Overview

The selected candidate will provide technical and operational support to research and evaluation projects across India, combining fieldwork, data analysis, and policy-focused research. The role is based in the Delhi-NCR region and involves significant travel.

Key Responsibilities

- Assisting in designing and implementing qualitative research methods including FGDs and in-depth interviews
- Monitoring fieldwork and ensuring data quality and consistency
- Coordinating with field teams and addressing data collection challenges
- Reviewing qualitative data and ensuring accuracy and completeness
- Transcribing and translating interviews and field data
- Conducting thematic data analysis using research tools and coding frameworks
- Supporting report drafting, research synthesis, and preparation of presentation materials
- Assisting with stakeholder and client reporting requirements

Eligibility & Preferred Profile

- Postgraduate degree in Rural Management, Social Sciences, Social Work, Public Policy, Economics, or related disciplines
- 0–3 years of experience in social research, monitoring and evaluation, or advisory roles
- Strong research, analytical, and communication skills
- Ability to work in team-based and field-intensive environments
- Willingness to travel extensively for project execution

Duration

- 6 months (project-based engagement)

Application Process

Interested candidates may share their resume at: hr@sambodhi.co.in

This role is particularly suitable for professionals seeking hands-on field research exposure, policy-impact work, and development sector experience early in their careers.
