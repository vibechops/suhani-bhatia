We're hiring an Associate/Senior Associate for Catalytic Finance at Samhita!

At Samhita, this role supports the expansion of our Catalytic Finance portfolio, including returnable grant and credit guarantee programmes. We're looking for someone who can quickly understand structured financial concepts and communicate them effectively to CSR leaders, foundations, financial institutions, and development partners.

As an Associate/Senior Associate for Catalytic Finance, you will:
- Support partnership proposals, concept notes, pitch decks, and fundraising collateral for CSR partners, foundations, and financial institutions
- Research and identify prospective corporate, philanthropic, and government partners, and manage outreach and relationship pipelines
- Create presentations, case studies, blogs, and thought leadership content on catalytic finance and financial inclusion
- Support programme execution, timelines, and stakeholder coordination

You should apply if you have:
- Strong written and verbal communication skills, with excellent presentation and proposal-writing abilities
- Ability to simplify complex financial concepts for diverse stakeholder groups
- 1-2 years of experience in consulting, investment banking, development finance, or impact investing, with strong financial analytical exposure

Location: Mumbai/Delhi/Bangalore (Remote)
