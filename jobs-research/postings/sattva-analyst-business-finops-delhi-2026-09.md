The Opportunity
As a key member of the FinOps team, you will work closely with a Business Unit at Sattva to drive change through data. You will collaborate with various teams and influence stakeholders, instill behaviour change and solve complex scaling problems with utmost urgency. The team’s operations are largely entrepreneurial in nature and require a high degree of innovation and agile decision-making.
This role rewards strong business understanding and execution-level thinking — you should be someone who can quickly grasp how a business process functions and where the operational risk sits, and then move a problem forward with minimal interventions.
Key Responsibilities
Data Management & Reporting
--Maintain and govern BU-level data — project status, resourcing, costs, invoicing — ensuring accuracy, consistency, and automation wherever possible
--Build and maintain dashboards and MIS reports that give BU leadership actionable, decision-ready insights at regular frequencies
--Track project and BU-level metrics against key targets, flagging bottlenecks proactively
--Maintain resource allocation data across the BU; track utilization and unallocated cost
--Support hiring forecasts against the project pipeline and track actuals against plan
Finance & Budgeting
--Support profit & loss budgeting and forecasting for the BU, working closely with central Finance team
--Track project costs (travel, third-party, other), invoicing, and collections; surface variances and risks early
--Support cash governance — cost approvals, vendor payments, and financial hygiene across projects
Project & Contract Onboarding
--Coordinate onboarding of new projects into internal systems — pricing, SLAs, contracts, metadata
--Liaise with Sales/BD and Finance to ensure clean, timely project setup and communication
Systems & Process
--Support with development and streamlining of BU processes (vendor management, travel booking, cost approvals) to improve efficiency and reduce operational drag
--Support adoption of tools and systems within the BU, troubleshooting bottlenecks with relevant teams
Stakeholder Coordination & Governance
--Act as a coordination point between BU leadership, central Finance, HR/Talent, and Delivery teams
--Document learnings and maintain a feedback loop to feed process improvements back into how the BU operates
Requirements:
--Bachelor's degree in business, finance, economics, or a related field
--0 - 2 years of experience in an internal, full-time operations, finance, or project management role at a company
--Strong hands-on skills in Microsoft or Google Workspace, with comfort in data analysis and building structured reports
--Working understanding of budgeting/P&L concepts, or clear evidence of being able to pick this up fast
--Solution centric and strong problem-solving skills
--Self-driven, curious and adaptable personality with the ability to work independently
--Excellent written and oral communication skills
--Passionate about driving organisational change and impact
--Curiosity, and a genuine willingness to listen and learn
