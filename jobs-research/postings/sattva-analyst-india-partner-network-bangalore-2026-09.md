About India Partner Network
India Partner Network (IPN) (
www.indiapartnernetwork.org
) is a tech-enabled platform incubated by Sattva Consulting to make institutional capacity building more accessible to small and mid-sized NGOs in India. Since its launch in August 2023, IPN has grown into an ecosystem of 
4,000+ NGOs, 6,000+ users, and a growing network of partners and service providers
. IPN supports NGOs through knowledge resources, capacity building events, assessments, service provider discovery, and ecosystem connections.
IPN is now entering its next phase of growth as it transitions into an independent Section 8 entity, 
ReadyNext IPN Foundation
. This role offers an early-stage opportunity to master digital platform operations while rapidly evolving into a well-rounded product professional. 
The Opportunity
We are looking for a high-ownership, problem solving Analyst to build the operational backbone of IPN platform products. If you have a basic background or deep curiosity in building digital products, this role is your playground.
This role sits at the intersection of execution excellence and product building. You will start by supporting first and later managing workflows and delivery across IPN products - Events, Directory, Knowledge Resources and additionally, Program support and stakeholder data. Over time, you will look at these operations through a product lens - identifying user friction, utilising low-code/no-code tools to automate processes, and translating ground realities into platform feature requirements. It is a unique trajectory designed to transition a sharp operational thinker into a data-driven product champion.
