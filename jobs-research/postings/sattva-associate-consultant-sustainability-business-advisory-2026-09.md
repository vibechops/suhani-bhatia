About Sustainability and Business Advisory
Our Sustainability and Business Advisory unit partners with Corporates and Funders to design and implement strategic interventions that support their sustainability goals. We craft focused strategies and forward-looking action plans, grounded in rigorous research and data-driven insights, to enable a meaningful transition towards net zero.
About the role:
The ideal candidate will have 3-5 years of experience in sustainability, ESG, impact, or related consulting/advisory roles. The role requires a strong consulting mindset, with the ability to conduct research and analysis, work with qualitative and quantitative data, develop actionable insights, and contribute to client engagements across diverse sustainability and business advisory themes.
Candidates with domain experience in one or more areas such as 
sustainability reporting, agriculture/agri-allied sectors, funds and funder advisory, ESG/GHG accounting, circularity, or gender
 will be preferred.
Key Responsibilities:
Support sustainability and business advisory engagements across diverse client projects and sectors.  
Conduct primary and secondary research, data analysis, benchmarking, and sector/market assessments to generate actionable insights. 
Support the development of sustainability strategies, frameworks, recommendations, and implementation roadmaps for clients. 
Gather, analyse, and validate qualitative and quantitative data from internal and external stakeholders. 
Contribute to projects across themes including sustainability reporting, ESG, climate and GHG accounting, circularity, agriculture/agri-allied sectors, gender, and funder/impact-related advisory.
Support the development of client deliverables, presentations, reports, frameworks, and advisory tools. 
Stay abreast of evolving sustainability, ESG, sectoral, and regulatory developments relevant to client work.
Role Requirements
Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Sustainability, Environmental Science, Business, Economics, Finance, Development Studies, or related fields. 
3-5 years of relevant experience in sustainability, ESG, impact, consulting, advisory, research, or related domains. 
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to work with both qualitative and quantitative information. 
Ability to conduct structured research, synthesise information, develop insights, and translate findings into clear recommendations. 
Strong orientation towards quality of deliverables, timelines, and attention to detail. 
Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
Experience in one or more of the following areas is preferred: Sustainability reporting, Agriculture / agri-allied sectors, Funds / funder advisory, ESG / GHG accounting, Circularity or Gender and inclusion
Experience working in a consulting, advisory, corporate sustainability, funder, development-sector, or research environment is an advantage.
