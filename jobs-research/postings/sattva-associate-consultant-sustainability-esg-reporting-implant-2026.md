Business Unit: Sustainability & Business Advisory

About Sattva 

We  Partner  to deliver social impact at scale 

Sattva Consulting is a global impact consulting firm from India. Since 2009 we have been engaging with communities, business, and government to achieve societal impact at scale.  

We believe that Civil society, Business, and Government all have a critical role to play in building an equitable and sustainable world, and we actively partner with all stakeholders to enable impact through our advisory & orchestration services, knowledge, and data platforms which we have built as public goods for the ecosystem and collaborative solutions and partnerships.  

Our teams are based in India, Singapore, Denmark, and UK, but as a global organization our work has spanned 25 countries and 5 continents. For more information, please visit:  www.sattva.co.in 

About Sustainability and Business Advisory 

Our Sustainability and Business Advisory unit partners with Corporates and Funders to design and implement strategic interventions that support their sustainability goals. We craft focused strategies and forward-looking action plans, grounded in rigorous research and data-driven insights, to enable a meaningful transition towards net zero. 

About the role: 
This role focuses on supporting organizations in strengthening their sustainability positioning through end-to-end sustainability reporting, ESG research, strategic communication, and capacity-building initiatives. The selected candidate will contribute to the development of GRI and BRSR-aligned sustainability reports, translate ESG insights into stakeholder-facing communication assets, support customized training module design, and advance research frameworks for leadership-level decision-making. The role requires a blend of ESG understanding, structured content development, and project coordination skills, with opportunities to work closely with client teams and senior stakeholders across sectors. 

Key Responsibilities: 

1. Sustainability Report Development 
- Work closely with senior client stakeholders (e.g., sustainability leaders, strategy and leadership teams) to  define reporting objectives, scope, structure, and disclosure priorities  based on organizational context and maturity. 

- Support the development of  sustainability and ESG reports  aligned with applicable frameworks such as GRI, BRSR, and other relevant standards, including narratives, key indicators, disclosures, and supporting documentation. 

- Manage and coordinate  ESG data collection and validation processes , working across functions to interpret data and support credible, consistent disclosures. 

- Coordinate with internal teams and external partners (including design and assurance stakeholders where relevant) to enable  timely, accurate, and high‑quality reporting outcomes  across the reporting lifecycle. 

2. ESG Communication & Content Development 
- Support the development of  stakeholder‑specific ESG messaging and communication approaches , ensuring alignment with sustainability goals, reporting disclosures, and overall organizational positioning. 

- Translate sustainability report outputs and broader ESG insights into  clear and engaging communication assets  across digital and offline platforms, including web content, presentations, social media, newsletters, and other formats as required. 

- Provide strategic input and execution support to ensure ESG communication initiatives are  coherent, effective, and aligned with organizational commitments and timelines . 

Role Requirements
