About Sustainability and Business Advisory
Our Sustainability and Business Advisory unit partners with Corporates and Funders to design and implement strategic interventions that support their sustainability goals. We craft focused strategies and forward-looking action plans, grounded in rigorous research and data-driven insights, to enable a meaningful transition towards net zero.
About the role:
This role focuses on supporting organizations in strengthening their sustainability positioning through end-to-end sustainability reporting, ESG research, strategic communication, and capacity-building initiatives. The selected candidate will contribute to the development of GRI and BRSR-aligned sustainability reports, translate ESG insights into stakeholder-facing communication assets, support customized training module design, and advance research frameworks for leadership-level decision-making. The role requires a blend of ESG understanding, structured content development, and project coordination skills, with opportunities to work closely with client teams and senior stakeholders across sectors.
Key Responsibilities:
1. Sustainability Report Development
Work closely with senior client stakeholders (e.g., sustainability leaders, strategy and leadership teams) to 
define reporting objectives, scope, structure, and disclosure priorities
 based on organizational context and maturity.
Lead and support the development of 
sustainability and ESG reports
 aligned with applicable frameworks such as GRI, BRSR, and other relevant standards, including narratives, key indicators, disclosures, and supporting documentation.
Manage and coordinate 
ESG data collection and validation processes
, working across functions to interpret data and support credible, consistent disclosures.
Coordinate with internal teams and external partners (including design and assurance stakeholders where relevant) to enable 
timely, accurate, and high‑quality reporting outcomes
 across the reporting lifecycle.
2. ESG Communication & Content Development
Support the development of 
stakeholder‑specific ESG messaging and communication approaches
, ensuring alignment with sustainability goals, reporting disclosures, and overall organizational positioning.
Translate sustainability report outputs and broader ESG insights into 
clear and engaging communication assets
 across digital and offline platforms, including web content, presentations, social media, newsletters, and other formats as required.
Provide strategic input and execution support to ensure ESG communication initiatives are 
coherent, effective, and aligned with organizational commitments and timelines
.
3. ESG Training & Capacity Building
Design and develop 
ESG training and awareness modules
 tailored to client industry contexts, value chains, and ESG maturity levels, adapting content for leadership, managerial, and operational audiences.
Translate ESG frameworks, regulatory expectations, and sustainability themes into 
practical business implications, operational considerations, and risk/opportunity perspectives
.
Support the design and delivery of 
structured learning journeys and engagement formats
 to build internal understanding, ownership, and integration of ESG priorities.
4. ESG Research & Framework Development
Lead and contribute to 
ESG research and analytical assignments

Strengthen and refine 
internal research methodologies, tools, and analytical frameworks
 to support sustainability strategy and advisory engagements.
Synthesize research outputs into 
clear, insight‑driven, decision‑oriented materials
 for senior and leadership‑level stakeholders.
Provide guidance, review, and mentoring support to 
junior team members
