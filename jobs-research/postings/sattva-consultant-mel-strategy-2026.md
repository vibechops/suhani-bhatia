 About Sattva 
 We Partner to deliver social impact at scale Sattva Consulting is a global impact consulting firm from India. Since 2009 we have been engaging with communities, business and government to achieve societal impact at scale. We believe that Civil society, Business and Government all have a critical role to play in building an equitable and sustainable world, and we actively partner with all stakeholders to enable impact through our advisory & orchestration services, knowledge and data platforms which we have built as public goods for the ecosystem and collaborative solutions and partnerships. Our teams are based in India, Singapore, Denmark and UK, but as a global organization our work has spanned 25 countries and 5 continents. For more information, please visit: www.sattva.co.in 

 
 About the Team 
Foundations team collaborates with a network of global philanthropic foundations, alongside multilateral aid agencies, aiming to inform, influence, and profoundly impact social and environmental outcomes resulting from their investments in diverse sectors such as education, child development, gender, health, and more. Our primary focus is on catalysing ecosystem transformation by rigorously solving problems, formulating strategic solutions which are logically sound, effective on the ground and ensuring all stakeholders are effectively engaged while their interests are covered.

 The Opportunity 
 As a Consultant in the Foundations team, you will bring deep MEL expertise to engagements that help our clients design, measure, and strengthen high-impact programs, while extending well beyond measurement into program strategy, design, and implementation. You will work alongside diverse teams across MEL, program strategy, delivery,, and business development, shaping solutions that create measurable, lasting impact for the foundations we partner with.

 Key Responsibilities 
 Project Management 
-- Independently build project plans for MEL engagements while contributing to broader program design and implementation roadmaps.
-- Actively track project milestones, manage multiple workstreams, and coordinate across program teams to ensure execution as per timelines.
-- Demonstrate strong time management and prioritisation skills, focusing effort on activities directly linked to program outcomes and impact.
-- Proactively identify and surface project and program-level risks, working with the project lead to design mitigation approaches.
-- Maintain comprehensive documentation of MEL frameworks, program knowledge assets, and learnings, ensuring critical information is well-documented and easily accessible.

 Problem solving & Study design 
-- Frame complex development problems accurately and translate them into clear, actionable lines of enquiry for the team and client.
-- Design MEL frameworks, theories of change, and results frameworks aligned to client outcomes, while contributing to the underlying program design where relevant.
-- Develop research and evaluation designs, finalise data collection tools, and define indicators across outcome and output levels, drawing on established sectoral frameworks.
-- Structure and lead analysis, synthesise information into clear insights, and form and test hypotheses that inform both measurement and program strategy.
-- Conduct rigorous secondary and primary research, developing insights and recommendations that strengthen assessments, program design choices, and course-correction decisions.
-- Translate data into informative insights and actionable recommendations, communicating these effectively to internal teams and external clients.
-- Go beyond the MEL brief where useful, identify program design gaps, strategic questions, or implementation risks surfaced through measurement work and recommend as well as deliver relevant solutions..

 Team Collaboration 
-- Lead a team of 2–3 Associates/Analysts to deliver high-quality project outputs on time, across MEL, research, strategy and delivery workstreams.
-- Collaborate with senior stakeholders within Sattva, contributing innovative ideas, frameworks, and approaches to support business growth and practice-building.
-- Give and receive constructive feedback respectfully, and coach junior team members on both technical MEL skills and broader consulting craft. Manage conflicts within the team openly and respectfully.

 Strategic Thinking & Sector knowledge 
-- Stay abreast of new trends in MEL practice as well as the broader sector or ecosystem the program operates in (e.g. education, health, livelihoods, climate).
-- Gather diverse perspectives, frameworks, and mental models across measurement, program strategy, and systems thinking, to sharpen design and solutioning.
