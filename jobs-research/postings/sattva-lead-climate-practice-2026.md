About Sattva 
We Partner to deliver social impact at scale Sattva Consulting is a global impact consulting firm from India. Since 2009 we have been engaging with communities, business and government to achieve societal impact at scale. We believe that Civil society, Business and Government all have a critical role to play in building an equitable and sustainable world, and we actively partner with all stakeholders to enable impact through our advisory & orchestration services, knowledge and data platforms which we have built as public goods for the ecosystem and collaborative solutions and partnerships. Our teams are based in India, Singapore, Denmark and UK, but as a global organization our work has spanned 25 countries and 5 continents. For more information, please visit: www.sattva.co.in 
Climate Practice 
Sattva's Climate Practice partners with corporates, philanthropists, foundations, multilaterals, governments and social enterprises to strengthen climate resilience, accelerate decarbonisation and energy transitions, advance circularity and sustainable infrastructure, and champion community-centred climate justice. The practice's work spans decarbonisation and energy transitions, circularity and material value chains, infrastructure and resilience, heat, water and vertical climate solutions, and climate justice — underpinned by a cross-cutting focus on climate finance. 
The Opportunity 
We are looking for a Lead to anchor and grow Sattva's Climate Practice. This is a senior leadership role for someone with strong conceptual understanding of climate issues and a market-oriented mindset, who can shape the practice's strategic agenda, deepen institutional expertise across climate domains, build trusted relationships with senior influencers across the climate ecosystem, and enable teams and clients to deliver high-impact climate solutions.
