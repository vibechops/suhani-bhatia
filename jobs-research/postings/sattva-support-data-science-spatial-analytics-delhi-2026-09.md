The Opportunity
You will contribute to a project that analyses large-scale and spatial datasets to inform government stakeholders. This role offers the opportunity to work at the intersection of data science, policy research, and geospatial analytics, applying analytical and problem-solving skills to address complex challenges. You will collaborate with a multidisciplinary team to generate insights that strengthen evidence-based decision-making and create lasting impact within the social development ecosystem.
Key Responsibilities:
Data Management & Cleaning
: Assist in cleaning, organising, and managing large datasets (survey, administrative, or satellite data) with a strong emphasis on accuracy and consistency.
Geospatial & Remote Sensing Analysis:
 Support spatial data analysis using GIS tools such as QGIS, ArcGIS, or Google Earth Engine, including basic raster processing and mapping tasks.
Programming & Data Processing:
 Write and maintain well-documented code in Python, R, or SQL for tasks such as data transformation, analysis, and automation of workflows.
Collaborative Research Support:
 Work closely with senior researchers and fellow interns to contribute to policy-relevant research projects, including background research and data analysis.
Key qualifications and experiences
Master’s, M.Phil., or Ph.D. in disciplines: Economics, Statistics, Computer Science, Geography, Urban Planning, Public Policy, Environmental Studies, or Related Quantitative Fields
Proficiency in Python / R / SQL / GIS tools (QGIS, ArcGIS, or GEE) along with any data visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI, Plotly, etc.) is a must
Any previous exposure to spatial econometrics/ machine learning/geospatial analytics/remote sensing will be advantageous
Previous internship experience in working with large datasets(surveys, admin data, or satellite/remote sensing) is preferred
Strong presentation skills with the ability to communicate complex ideas effectively
Exceptional critical thinking and problem-solving skills
Display strong interpersonal skills, humility, willingness to listen and excitement to learn at all times
Self-driven, curious and adaptable personality with the ability to produce accountable work
