About Sattva 

We  Partner  to deliver social impact at scale 

Sattva Consulting is a global impact consulting firm from India. Since 2009 we have been engaging with communities, business and government to achieve societal impact at scale.  

We believe that Civil society, Business and Government all have a critical role to play in building an equitable and sustainable world, and we actively partner with all stakeholders to enable impact through our advisory & orchestration services, knowledge and data platforms which we have built as public goods for the ecosystem and collaborative solutions and partnerships.  

Our teams are based in India, Singapore, Denmark and UK, but as a global organization our work has spanned 25 countries and 5 continents. For more information, please visit:  www.sattva.co.in 

About Sustainability and Business Advisory 

Our Sustainability and Business Advisory unit partners with corporates and funders to design and implement strategic interventions that support their sustainability goals. We craft focused strategies and forward-looking action plans, grounded in rigorous research and data-driven insights, to enable a meaningful transition towards net zero. 

About the role: 

We’re looking for an enthusiastic intern with a strong interest in sustainability and reporting. The ideal candidate will have some exposure to sustainability concepts and familiarity with global and regional frameworks such as GRI, BRSR, SASB, CSRD, TCFD, or ISSB. This role is a great opportunity for someone detail-oriented and eager to learn how to analyze sustainability data, support report development, data analysis and understand emerging disclosure requirements. 

Key Responsibilities: 
- Conduct data analysis and trend identification in the sustainability space. 
- Prepare reports summarizing market and client research findings.  
- Track and evaluate lead conversion metrics to measure success. 
- Assist in creating and refining presentations and marketing materials for client pitches. 
- Work closely with marketing, sales and operations leads 
- Support on live projects 
- Responsible for leveraging existing case studies and integrating them into relevant Go-to-Market (GTM) decks and tailor them for specific business needs. 
- Help streamline sales processes by documenting them and suggesting minor improvements.  
- Provide admin support during client interactions (e.g., scheduling meetings, sending follow-ups). 

Role Requirements: 
- Graduate in Sustainability, Environmental Science, Business, Finance, or a related field. 
- Good understanding of GRI, BRSR, SASB, CSRD, TCFD, and other sustainability standards. 
- Ability to analyze data and derive meaningful insights. 
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills 
- Ability to manage multiple threads and deadlines effectively. 
- Strong focus on data accuracy, compliance, and reporting integrity.
