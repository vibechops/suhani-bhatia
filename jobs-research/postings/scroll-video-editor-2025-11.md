Hiring: Video Editor
5 Nov, 2025
Application deadline: November 15, 2025

Scroll is looking for a Video Editor with three years of experience to join our video team. The editor will help build a consistent visual identity for our ongoing and upcoming video properties and strengthen our award-winning journalism across formats.

The candidate will collaborate closely with producers, designers, and reporters, bringing creativity, technical skill, and a strong work ethic to every project.

They will be required to:
- Edit long-form and short-form videos, including interviews, ground reports, and explainers.
- Perform sound design, music editing, and 2D motion graphics.
- Maintain narrative accuracy, pacing, and rhythm throughout long-form edits.

Scroll is a small newsroom that punches above its weight — producing ambitious, award-winning work with limited resources. If you believe you can produce technically sound, thoughtful video work that reflects these values, please send your resume and work samples to aryan@scroll.in with “Video Editor” in the subject line.

Note: This is a full-time, in-office position based in Delhi.
Application deadline: November 15, 2025.
