Hiring: Video Journalist
13 Aug, 2026
Application deadline: August 24, 2026.

Scroll is looking for a well-informed and talented early-career video journalist with two years of experience to join our video team. If you have a keen interest in news, a passion for visual storytelling, and a strong attention to detail, this is the perfect role for you.

Your primary responsibility would be to independently research, report, produce and edit short ground reports on a weekly basis.

Additionally, as part of the video team, you would be expected to:
1. Script, shoot and produce Scroll's video documentaries with other reporters.
2. Produce and edit both long-form and short-form flagship shows.

We require applicants to be skilled in shooting on-ground stories with cameras and editing softwares such as Premiere Pro.

This is not a remote position and the candidate will have to be based out of Delhi.

Scroll is a small newsroom that punches above its weight. We work with limited resources to produce ambitious award winning journalism. If you wish to tell visual stories that inform and inspire, please send your resume with a cover letter and three work samples to jobs@scroll.in with 'Video Journalist' in the subject line latest by August 24.

Applications will be considered on a rolling basis.
