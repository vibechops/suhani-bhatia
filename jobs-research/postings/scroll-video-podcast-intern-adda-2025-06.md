Hiring: Video podcast intern for Scroll Adda
9 Jun, 2025
Application deadline: June 21, 2025

Scroll is looking for a video intern for our new podcast, Scroll Adda with Shoaib Daniyal.

The work would entail:
▸ Organise the show’s raw footage, assemble and edit it into a conversation (video switching)
▸ Identify best bits from the show, edit and stylise it into vertical and horizontal social-media friendly content
▸ Support with research and guest coordination
▸ Work with journalists to ideate and execute video projects

Requirements:
▸ Proficiency in video editing software (preferably Adobe Premiere Pro)
▸ Basic knowledge of video codecs, formats and exporting for different platforms.
▸ A degree or diploma in video editing, or a related field is a plus. Final year students will also be considered as long as they can work full-time for the duration of the internship.
▸ A basic portfolio that can showcase your skills. This could include professional, academic or even personal work.

This is not a hybrid gig. The candidate will be required to work full-time at our Delhi office during the entire duration of the internship.
This is a paid internship.
Duration: Three months
Location: Delhi

To apply, send your CV to jobs@scroll.in with "adda internship" in the subject line.
Application deadline: June 21, 2025.
