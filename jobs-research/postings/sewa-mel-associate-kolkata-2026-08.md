WE’RE HIRING | MEL ASSOCIATE

SEWA Bharat is looking for a motivated MEL Associate to join our team in Kolkata, West Bengal.

This is an opportunity to work closely with programme and field teams to strengthen monitoring systems, generate evidence-based insights, support research and evaluations, and contribute to meaningful grassroots development.

Key Responsibilities:
- Develop and strengthen MEL systems and plans
- Support data collection, verification, analysis, and reporting
- Conduct and support qualitative and quantitative research
- Develop MEL indicators, research tools, and evaluation frameworks
- Support internal and external evaluations and research studies
- Prepare programme, donor, and management reports
- Document programme learning, challenges, outcomes, and impact
- Support knowledge-sharing, advocacy, and dissemination activities
- Work closely with programme teams, the national MEL team, consultants, and technology partners

What We’re Looking For:
- Postgraduate degree in Public Policy, Development Studies, Economics, Social Sciences, or a relevant field
- 2–3 years of relevant experience in MEL, research, programme monitoring, or a similar role
- Strong qualitative and quantitative research skills
- Experience with research design, sampling, indicators, data collection, and analysis
- Excellent writing, documentation, and analytical skills
- Strong communication skills in English and Hindi
- Willingness to travel and spend significant time in the field, including travel to Bihar

Location: Kolkata, West Bengal
Salary: ₹35,000–₹40,000 per month
Duration: 1 year, with the possibility of extension

How to Apply:
Share updated CV/resume and cover letter at moumita@sewabharat.org
