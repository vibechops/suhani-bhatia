Job Description: Research Associate – West Bengal
Job Location: West Bengal, Kolkata

Experience Required:
– 3 years or more of work experience in the informal economy or similar sectors.
– Experience working with women is preferred.

SEWA Bharat is a non-profit organization established in 1984, part of the national SEWA movement. It empowers women working in the informal economy through research and statistical analysis to address their rights, livelihoods, financial independence, education, health, and social security.

The ideal candidate will design pilots, oversee field monitoring, support internal and external teams, conduct data analysis using STATA, draft reports, develop survey instruments, manage timelines and budgets, and travel extensively across West Bengal and Jharkhand to support research activities. Strong communication skills in English, Hindi, and Bengali are essential.

Qualifications:
– Postgraduate degree in Social Sciences, Economics, or a related field with research experience.
– Proficiency in quantitative methods using STATA.
– Strong communication skills in English, Hindi, and Bengali (written and oral).

Work Requirements:
– Frequent travel to various districts in West Bengal and Jharkhand.
– Teamwork and time management for multiple tasks.

Salary: Rs 55,000-60,000/monthly, negotiable based on experience.
Application: Send resume with cover letter to jobs@sewabharat.org, subject line: ‘Application For Research Associate – West Bengal’.
