Research Consultant - SEWA Bharat

About SEWA Bharat:
Self Employed Women’s Association (SEWA) is a movement to empower women working in the informal economy. SEWA Bharat is part of the national SEWA movement. Established in 1984, it is a national federation of SEWA organisations of women working in the informal economy.

About The Project:
SEWA Bharat is undertaking a 5-month mixed-methods research study for NIUA in two low-income settlements in Dehradun, Uttarakhand. The study will examine how sanitation and water access affect health, dignity, time use, safety, and livelihood participation of under-resourced communities, especially women in the informal economy. SEWA Bharat is seeking a Research Consultant, preferably based in Dehradun, to lead the day-to-day execution of the study under the guidance of the Research Lead and Senior Coordinator – WASH and WEE.

Key Responsibilities:
- Support desk review, inception planning, and finalisation of study methodology and sites
- Develop research tools for household surveys, KIIs and FGDs
- Coordinate field preparation, including tool piloting and team orientation and training
- Oversee data collection across approximately 200–250 households in two settlements
- Conduct/supervise KIIs with officials, frontline workers, local representatives, and civil society actors and FGDs with women workers, adolescent girls, male residents, and vulnerable groups
- Document settlement-level sanitation, drainage, water access, waste disposal, and safety concerns
- Clean, organise, and analyse quantitative and qualitative data
- Prepare the inception note and final report and presentation of findings while incorporating feedback from SEWA Bharat and NIUA

Required Qualifications and Experience:
- Master’s degree in social sciences, public policy, urban studies, development studies, gender studies, public health, WASH, or related fields
- 4–5 years of experience in field-based research in the development ecosystem
- Experience with surveys, interviews, FGDs, and qualitative documentation
- Understanding of WASH, gender, women’s livelihoods, public health, or urban governance
- Strong writing, analysis, coordination, and communication skills
- Ability to work respectfully with low-income communities, especially women workers
- Fluency in Hindi and English required

Compensation: INR 60,000-70,000 (including taxes, as applicable) per month for five months
Duration: 5 months
