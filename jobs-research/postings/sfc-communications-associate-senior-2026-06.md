Communications Associate/ Senior Communications Associate
Sustainable Futures Collaborative (SFC)
New Delhi | 3-5 years (listing also states 2-5)

SFC invites applications for a Communications Associate/ Senior Communications Associate (designation subject to the candidate’s experience and skills). The candidate will support the Communications Leads in developing and implementing SFC’s communications strategies across our thematic areas and in institutional storytelling. This will involve core communications activities such as creating and editing compelling narratives of our work, disseminating research findings among diverse readers and stakeholders, managing the SFC website, social media, and other communications assets, and organising events. The ideal candidate will have broad communications experience and a strong understanding of research and policy communications. SFC is a dynamic and evolving organisation, and the candidate will be crucial in shaping our communications and will work closely with a creative and dedicated team.

Responsibilities (from listing):
• Research dissemination: Develop and implement targeted communication plans to effectively disseminate SFC research findings to existing and new, unexplored audiences including media and academia
• Content creation and editing: Develop collateral, press releases, social media content, newsletters, and videos, as required, ensuring consistent messaging and adherence to the brand voice.
• Media and engagement: Engage with media on our research and broader body of work, build and maintain strong media relationships. Participate in media, policy, and communications events and engagements.

Requirements:
• 2-5 years of experience in a communications role, ideally within a research organisation, non-profit, or related
• Excellent writing, copyediting, and proofreading skills.
• Awareness of WordPress, social media tools and MailChimp, SEMRush is a plus.
• A strong sense of design and multimedia editing is a plus.
