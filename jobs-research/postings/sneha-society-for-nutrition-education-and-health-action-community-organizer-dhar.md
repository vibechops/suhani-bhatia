 WE ARE HIRING | COMMUNITY ORGANIZER

SNEHA (Society for Nutrition, Education and Health Action) is a Mumbai-based non-profit organization working to improve the health, nutrition, safety, and wellbeing of women and children in vulnerable communities.

About EHSAS Program
The EHSAS Program focuses on adolescent and youth wellbeing through:
Health & nutrition
Gender equality and sexuality
Mental health
Prevention of violence against women and children
Youth participation and empowerment
Position: Community Organizer

Location: Dharavi, Mumbai
Qualification: SSC/HSC
Experience: 2–5 years in community-based projects
Reports to: Program Officer

Key Responsibilities
• Mobilize adolescents, youth, and parents and conduct community outreach.
• Conduct group sessions and community-based campaigns.
• Support planning and implementation of adolescent and parent sessions.
• Coordinate with health post staff and community stakeholders.
• Support adolescent-friendly health services and community-based mental health interventions.
• Maintain accurate data entry, documentation, and activity reports.

Key Skills
• Strong communication and teamwork
• Willingness to learn
• Empathy and a non-judgmental approach
• Patience and resilience while working with diverse communities
• Willingness to travel within Mumbai and work occasionally on weekends
