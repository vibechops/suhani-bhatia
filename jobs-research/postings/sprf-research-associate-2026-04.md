SPRF India is a New Delhi public policy think tank engaging young researchers on Governance, Economy, Human Rights, Security, and Environment.

Key responsibilities: produce commentaries, issue briefs, discussion papers, and AV content; social media for outputs; assist proposals and literature reviews; support long-term projects; brainstorm new initiatives; review drafts; support events; co-manage Curated Voices editorial duties.

Qualifications: relevant Bachelor's/Master’s; 2-3 years research experience (think tank/academia/nonprofit preferred); qualitative/quantitative methods; excellent communication; SPSS/STATA/R a plus.

Hours: Mon–Fri 9:00–5:00. Hybrid. Report to Research Team Lead.

Apply: resume, cover letter, research samples to recruitment@sprf.in subject “Research Associate - Your Name.” Deadline: 20 April 2026. Closed.
