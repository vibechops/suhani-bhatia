### Job Description

We are looking for a motivated and dynamic professional to support the **Hunger Free Bharat Initiative for a partner organization**. The role involves coordinating food distribution activities in underserved communities and driving CSR partnerships, fundraising, and corporate engagement to support the program's growth and impact.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Minimum 5 years of experience in CSR, corporate fundraising, partnership development, business development, or social impact initiatives.  
*   Proven experience in developing and drafting CSR proposals, fundraising proposals, and partnership pitches for corporate donors and funding organizations  
*   Prepare presentations, proposals, and reports for potential CSR partners and donors.  
*   Coordinate food distribution drives in slum and underserved communities.  
*   Identify and connect with areas and beneficiaries in need of support.  
*   Build and maintain relationships with NGOs, volunteers, community leaders, and local stakeholders.  
*   Reach out to corporates, foundations, and organizations for CSR partnerships and funding opportunities.  
*   Support fundraising initiatives and donor engagement activities.  
*   Assist in planning and executing food donation and community outreach campaigns.  
*   Maintain records of outreach activities, partnerships, donations, and program impact.  

  

### Requirements

**Education**

*   MBA, MA, MSW, or a related field.  

**Experience**

*   Candidates must have a minimum of 5 years of experience...  
*   Proven experience in developing and drafting CSR proposals.  
*   Experience in corporate fundraising and CSR partnerships is preferred.  
*   Prior experience working with NGOs, development organizations, or social sector projects will be an advantage.  

**Skills**

*   Strong communication, networking, and relationship-management skills.  
*   Ability to engage with corporates and external stakeholders professionally.  
*   Interest in social impact, community development, and grassroots initiatives.  
*   Self-driven and willing to undertake field visits when required.  
*   Proficiency in MS Office (Excel, Word, PowerPoint) and report preparation.
