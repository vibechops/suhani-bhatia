Role Purpose

The ESG Analyst will play a critical role in supporting Swiggy’s ESG (Environmental, Social & Governance) reporting and data management agenda. The role is designed to strengthen internal ESG capabilities, reduce reliance on external consultants, and ensure high-quality, timely, and accurate reporting aligned with SEBI, BRSR, GRI, and global standards.

Key Responsibilities

- Data Management & Reporting
- Collect, validate, and analyze ESG data across business functions (Scootsy + Swiggy).
- Prepare inputs for annual BRSR, ESG ratings submissions (MSCI, DJSI, Sustainalytics, etc.), and investor queries.
- Ensure accuracy, completeness, and audit readiness of ESG disclosures.
- Enable value chain reporting and digitize ESG data management
- Compliance & Assurance
- Track SEBI, MCA, and other regulatory requirements on ESG/BRSR and ensure compliance.
- Establish assurance ready systems and processes across Swiggy’s ecosystem.
- Support external assurance processes by coordinating with internal teams and auditors.
- Maintain documentation and evidence for ESG disclosures.
- Cross-Functional Collaboration
- Liaise with Finance, HR, Operations, Legal, and other functions to ensure timely data collection.
- Act as a point of contact for ESG data owners and support SPOCs in adhering to timelines.
- Sustainability Strategy & Projects
- Support the design and execution of ESG initiatives and projects (climate action, diversity & inclusion, community engagement, etc.).
- Assist in preparing decks, reports, and updates for leadership, investors, and board committees.
- Analytics & Insights
- Monitor peer and industry ESG performance to identify gaps and opportunities.
- Generate insights to strengthen Swiggy’s ESG positioning.

Qualifications & Experience

- Education: MBA, or Master’s in Economics, Statistics, or Finance.
- Experience: 1–5 years in ESG reporting, compliance, statutory audit, or sustainability data management.
- Strong analytical, data management, and reporting skills.
- Familiarity with ESG frameworks (BRSR, GRI, SASB, TCFD, etc.).
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities.

Competencies

- Strong attention to detail and accuracy in data handling.
- Ability to work across diverse teams and drive alignment.
- Project management skills to manage multiple deliverables under tight timelines.
- Proactive, ownership-driven, with a strong commitment to sustainability and compliance.
