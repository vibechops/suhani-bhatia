The Ken’s Journalism Leadership Programme 2026
By Arundhati Ramanathan | 5 Mar 2026

We are seeking curious, self-driven, and ambitious graduates who want to kick-start their journalism careers in a vibrant, dynamic, AI-resilient newsroom.

In a world of news outlets obsessed with churning out countless pieces of “content” now enabled by AI, we believe bylines are sacred, and we want to be indispensable to our subscribers every single day by helping them sift through noise. As part of this journey, we are accepting applications to the fourth batch of our Journalism Leadership Programme (JLP) for new graduates.

AI is disrupting many careers. In such an environment, we believe journalism remains far more resilient than most careers. (There is no agentic replacement yet for good, old-fashioned, boots-on-the-ground reporting.) This is also a great chance for curious, self-driven, and ambitious graduates to kick-start their journalism careers in a vibrant, dynamic, and feedback-driven newsroom.

What is JLP?
You will join The Ken as a full-time Trainee Writer in Bengaluru, Mumbai, or New Delhi as part of this programme. Under the guidance of The Ken’s editorial leaders, you will navigate the complexities of a rapidly evolving media landscape to emerge as a credible voice in your respective beats.

How many graduates can join this programme?
Up to four new graduates will be accepted into this programme this year. It starts in June 2026.

What rewards and salary can you expect?
The starting salary ranges from Rs 6.5 to Rs 8.5 lakh per annum.

Who can apply?
Graduates and postgraduates from the class of 2025–26 across disciplines.

We strongly encourage graduates from the following institutions to apply:
- Shri Ram College of Commerce, New Delhi
- St. Stephen’s College, New Delhi
- Ashoka University, Sonipat
- St. Xavier’s College, Kolkata
- Loyola College, Chennai
- St. Xavier’s College, Mumbai
- Ahmedabad University, Ahmedabad
- Krea University, Andhra Pradesh
