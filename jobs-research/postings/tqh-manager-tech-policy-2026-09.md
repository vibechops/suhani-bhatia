Manager, Tech Policy

As TQH expands its footprint, we seek dynamic and energetic individuals to join our mission of redefining public policy in India. We work on cutting-edge research and advocacy problems, while engaging with a wide variety of policy issues and interacting with a gamut of stakeholders.

As one of the few consulting firms in the space, we offer strong multidisciplinary research expertise and couple it with solid experience of effective communication and advocacy. If our work sounds exciting to you, apply to work with us!

We are specifically looking for someone with a strong foundation in areas such as data privacy, platform liability, and regulation of emerging technologies, to lead and manage projects in tech policy. Prior experience in tech policy is essential, given the nature of upcoming staffing needs.

The role involves leading projects while owning client relationships and playing a key role in designing advocacy strategies.

Qualifications required

- Degree from a reputed university in law/ public policy/ related fields or experience in public policy research, advocacy and government partnerships
- 6-8 years of work experience
- Strong analytical skills
- Ability to present information in a structured and insightful manner
- Excellent communication skills and an eye for detail
- Ability to work in a dynamic environment with a strong desire to learn new skills
