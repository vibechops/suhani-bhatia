 Organisation's Profile

 Transform Schools is working to ensure that every child realises their full potential in school and beyond. Anchored in India's national priorities and the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs 4, 5, and 10), our mission is to improve learning outcomes, gender equity, and life opportunities for secondary-grade students aged 13–18.  

 We co-design and implement evidence-based education programmes with State governments, with a focus on strengthening foundational learning, building equitable learning environments, and preparing students for transitions beyond school. Our work combines pedagogical innovation, teacher development, data-driven systemic reform to ensure every adolescent, especially those from underserved communities, can thrive. As co-convenors of the State of Secondary Education (SOSE) global collaborative, we are also shaping a global agenda to position secondary education as a societal minimum standard, mobilising research, investment, and practice across the Global South.  

 A key part of our work is the Learning Equaliser Programme, an integrated learning, gender, and life skills initiative for students in Classes 8 and 9. The programme combines targeted academic instruction with gender-transformative education and life skills development to address both learning gaps and the social barriers that affect adolescents? agency, aspirations, and well-being. Through structured instruction, participatory learning methods, and engagement with teachers, parents, and communities, the programme aims to create supportive ecosystems that enable adolescents of all genders to exercise voice, confidence, and leadership.  

 Our work spans two institutional vehicles: People For Action, a charitable society that delivers CSR and grant-funded programmes, and The Transform Trust, which leads outcomes-linked engagements.  

 With a strong footprint across multiple states and a commitment to scale, we aim to reach 30 million children by 2030, building a future where quality secondary education is a reality for all.  

 Job Purpose

 Transform Schools is developing the Learning Equaliser (LE) approach. This school-based programme seeks to enable adolescents to question restrictive norms, build agency, and engage in more equitable relationships with peers, teachers, and families.  

 Transform Schools is seeking a Programme Officer – Gender and Life Skills to support the effective implementation and quality delivery of gender-focused programme interventions across the assigned region.  

 The role will work closely with Programme teams, regional teams and a network of facilitators to ensure that gender-related programme activities are delivered consistently, effectively and in accordance with the programme design. The incumbent will provide day-to-day guidance and support to facilitators, monitor field implementation, identify implementation challenges and support timely resolution.

 The role requires a working understanding of gender norms, gender-equitable behaviours and adolescent development, with the ability to translate these concepts into practical programme implementation. The position is not expected to lead the organisation's overall gender strategy but will serve as a programme specialist who ensures that gender components are effectively integrated into field implementation.  

 The Senior Programme Officer will also contribute to facilitator capacity building, programme planning, field monitoring, documentation, reporting and learning. The role will work closely with the State Lead and other technical and implementation teams to strengthen programme quality and ensure that gender outcomes are meaningfully reflected in programme delivery.
