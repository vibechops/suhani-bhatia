TII by Access Livelihoods (social enterprise incubator). Two programmes:

RRE Fellowship (13 months): 30-day reflection at Access Livelihoods Hyderabad then enterprise placements; ongoing reflection workshops and practicum. Stipend ₹18,000/month (12 months); completion incentive ₹30,000; excellence incentive ₹50,000; certificate after 12-month term. Program fee ₹16,000 + 18% GST; application fee ₹1,000. Training duration cited as 45 days in one section / 30-day reflection in another — confirm with Access Livelihoods.

SEP (2 years): 8-month reflection in Hyderabad + 16-month practicum; stipend ₹25,000/month (12 months); completion incentive ₹75,000; certificate after 24 months. Program fee ₹8,00,000 + 18% GST; application fee ₹1,500.

Eligibility: Bachelor's or Master's any discipline; age 20–30; passion for social impact/entrepreneurship; leadership/problem-solving/analytical skills; willingness to work grassroots; full-time on-field availability; complete Hyderabad training; available for entire duration.

Dates (final phase): Applications open 1 March 2025; deadline 20 June 2025; acceptance decisions 20 June 2025. Status: Expired on Lawctopus.
