 WE ARE HIRING NOW 
   
 Programme Associate - Decentralised Renewable Energy (DRE) 
   
 Location: Raipur, Chhattisgarh 
 

Apply by: 25 September 2026 (Rolling Selections) 
   
 About the Role 
 TRI is looking for a Programme Associate – Decentralised Renewable Energy (DRE) to support community-led clean energy solutions in rural geographies. The role will involve assessing rural energy needs, designing and piloting DRE solutions, working with women's collectives, Panchayats, and community institutions, and building partnerships to scale gender-responsive and climate-resilient approaches. Women who have taken a lifecycle career break are especially encouraged to apply. 
   
 Why TRI? 
 
 Women-Friendly Workplace: Safe, inclusive environment actively welcoming career returners of all backgrounds. Because talent has no expiry date. 
 Close-Knit, Proximal Teams: Shoulder-to-shoulder fieldwork with committed development professionals. Every colleague is present. 
 Intellectual Rigour & Practice: Analytical depth meets ground-level craft. Every assignment demands both thinking and doing. 
 Best-in-Sector Compensation: Matching the top compensation benchmarks in Indian development. We respect your worth. 
 Work-Life Balance Leader: Sustainable practice rhythms, personal time and family commitments are built into the operating culture. 
 
 What Will You Do? 
 
 Assess rural energy needs across households, livelihoods, agriculture, health, and public infrastructure, prioritising gender-responsive, climate-resilient solutions. 
 Conduct demand assessments and energy diagnostics to guide programme design and policy engagement. 
 Design, pilot, and scale decentralised renewable energy solutions—including solar, biomass, mini-grids, clean cooking, storage, and productive-use technologies. 
 Partner with women's collectives, Panchayats, and community institutions to build awareness, ownership, leadership, and participation in DRE planning, procurement, operation, and governance. 
 Develop and maintain an inventory of DRE technologies, service providers, and implementation models. 
 Build partnerships across government, DRE manufacturers, renewable energy service companies, financiers, CSR, and development organisations, including convergence with government schemes and financing mechanisms. 
 Prepare policy notes, learning documents, case studies, and recommendations; facilitate consultations, workshops, community meetings, and multi-stakeholder reviews. 
 
 Who Are We Looking For? 
 
 Master's degree in Energy, Environment, Climate Change, Rural Development, Public Policy, Engineering, or a related field. 
 5–8 years of relevant experience in decentralised renewable energy, rural development, climate action, or allied sectors. 
 Prior experience working with government programmes, CSR initiatives, or multi-stakeholder partnerships is desirable. 
 Experience in gender-responsive programming and community-led models is an added advantage. 
 Strong verbal and written communication skills. 
 
 Employment Terms 
 This is a third-party payroll position and is initially set for 18 months duration.
