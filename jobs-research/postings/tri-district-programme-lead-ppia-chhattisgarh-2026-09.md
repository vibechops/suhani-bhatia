Position: District Programme Lead [Public Policy in Action (PPiA)]
Location: Select Districts of Chhattisgarh
Duration: Initially set till March 2027 (rolling selections; immediate joining)
Emoluments: Rs.75,000/- per month (third-party payroll position)

ABOUT THE ROLE
A District Programme Lead - Public Policy in Action (PPiA) is a collaborative, people-centered problem-solver committed to public service. Stationed at the District Headquarters, the District Programme Lead (PPiA) plays a pivotal role in supporting the local administration to achieve transformative district development over the programme duration.

Women who have taken a lifecycle career break are especially encouraged to apply.

Eligibility Criteria:
- Experience: Minimum of two years of full-time work experience.
- Education: Graduate degree from a recognised university, in any discipline.
- Age: Must be below 30 years of age.
- Mobility: Willingness to travel and work in rural areas.

Key Responsibilities:
- District & Block Planning: Assist the district administration in preparing robust District and Block Action Plans aligned with TADP and ABP priorities.
- Data & Monitoring: Strengthen data ecosystems through improved data flow, analysis, and visualization.
- Scheme Delivery: Enhance delivery efficacy of government schemes.
- Ground-Truthing: Conduct ground-truthing of schemes and share real-time feedback.
- Lakhpati Didi Support: Provide strategic and operational support to the Lakhpati Didi programme under DAY-NRLM, aimed at enhancing SHG household incomes.
- Scheme Convergence: Facilitate convergence of multiple government schemes.
- Participatory Governance: Integrate Village Prosperity & Resilience Plans with Gram Panchayat Development Plans (GPDP).
- Knowledge Management: Document innovations, case studies, and success stories.
- Capacity Building: Organize and lead training workshops for district- and block-level officials, field staff, and CBOs.
- Partnerships: Liaise with local institutions and technical partners.
