Programme Associate – Decentralised Renewable Energy (DRE)
Location: Raipur, Chhattisgarh
Apply by: 25 September 2026 | Rolling Selections
Apply at: www.trif.in/careers

At TRI, we’re looking for a Programme Associate – Decentralised Renewable Energy (DRE) to support our work on community-led clean energy solutions in rural geographies.

In this role, you will assess rural energy needs across households, livelihoods, agriculture, health, and public infrastructure; conduct energy diagnostics; design, pilot, and scale decentralised renewable energy solutions; work closely with women’s collectives, Panchayats, and community institutions; and build partnerships with government, DRE service providers, financiers, CSR and development organisations.

We offer best-in-sector compensation, a close-knit and collaborative work environment, a strong focus on work-life balance, and the opportunity to combine intellectual rigour with meaningful field-level practice.
