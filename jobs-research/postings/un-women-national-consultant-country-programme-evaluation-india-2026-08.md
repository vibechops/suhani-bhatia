UN Women is seeking a National Consultant to support the Country Portfolio Evaluation in India. The consultant will work closely with the evaluation team, contributing to various phases including inception, data collection, and reporting.

Background:
UN Women Strategic Plan 2022-2025 impact areas: Governance and Participation in public life; Women Peace and Security, Humanitarian Action, and Disaster Risk Reduction; Ending Violence Against Women; Women's Economic Empowerment.

The UN Women Country Office (CO) is based in Delhi, India. The India CO is undertaking a Country Portfolio Evaluation and requires one national consultant to support the conduct of this evaluation.

Roles and Responsibilities:
Evaluation Inception phase: reviewing relevant documents; providing translation/interpretation support; contribute to inclusive Evaluation methodology.
Evaluation Data collection: support interviews, FGDs, and consultations by taking detailed notes; contribute to evaluation analysis; translation/interpretation.
Reporting phase: provide inputs to the preliminary findings.

Deliverables and Payment Terms:
- Inception phase: 1 September to 31 October 2026 – 20% Payment
- Data Collection phase: 1 November 2026 to 30 November 2026 – 70%
- Reporting stage: 1 December to 10 December 2026 – 10%

Education: Master's degree in a relevant field (e.g., gender studies, development studies, sociology, political science, or related disciplines).

Experience:
- Minimum 7 years of experience conducting research, evaluations, reviews, or assessments with development organizations.
- Demonstrated expertise in gender equality, women's rights, and women's economic empowerment.
- Knowledge of national development policies, strategies, and frameworks and experience working in India.
- Experience in and/or strong knowledge of Tamil Nadu, including its development context; candidates must not have been involved in the design or implementation of UN Women-supported interventions covered by the evaluation.
- Language proficiency in English required; ability to communicate in Tamil.

Application deadline: August 09, 2026 (Closed). Apply via UN Women careers site.
