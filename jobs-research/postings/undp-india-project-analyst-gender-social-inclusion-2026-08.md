Office/Unit/Project Description
The Plastic Waste Management Programme (PWM) is designed to minimize negative impacts of plastic pollution and its risks to environment and human health in India. The aim is to enhance sustainable plastic waste management (segregation/collection/recycling) practices through a socio-technical model in line with Swachh Bharat Mission in India, while ensuring compliance with regulations and ensuring improved use of resources. The project targets institutionalization of waste pickers (safai saathis) through improved governance mechanisms, capacity building and improved social conditions. The project also targets community engagement to enhance responsible waste disposal behaviour, and cross-stakeholder engagements for improved action on plastics.
To achieve the project objective and outcomes, the project is structured in 4 components:
Component 1 aims to strengthen policy frameworks, institutional systems, and data capabilities to improve national and urban governance of plastics and waste management.
Component 2 focuses on design and implementation of pilots for improved materials management in close coordination with project stakeholders through demonstration of scalable BEP/BAT models
Component 3 is directed mainly to improve socio-economic conditions for waste pickers by enhancing social protection, formal recognition, and gender‑responsive support systems through institutionalized service delivery mechanisms.
Component 4 aims towards developing knowledge management, monitoring and communication mechanisms based on in-built adaptive feedback system during the project duration.
Duties and Responsibilities
Main Purpose:
The Project Analyst, Gender and Social Inclusion, will support the integration of gender equality and social inclusion across UNDP’s waste, chemicals, and circular economy programmes, with a focus on improving livelihoods, safety, and access to social protection for informal waste workers. The role will work with government, private sector, industries, and civil society partners to ensure inclusive programme design, implementation, and monitoring, building on learnings from Project Utthaan and related initiatives.
Duties and Responsibilities:
Gender equality and social inclusion (GESI) Integration in Project Design and Implementation:
Lead the integration of GESI across project design, planning, and implementation by applying relevant frameworks and tools, and ensuring that inclusion dimensions are systematically embedded in project activities and outputs.
Social Inclusion Implementation and Programme Strengthening:
Coordinate and oversee the implementation of social inclusion interventions for informal waste workers, focusing on enhancing access to social protection, occupational safety, financial inclusion, and sustainable livelihoods.
Concurrently, undertake analysis of project components and field-level implementation challenges to identify inclusion gaps, particularly for women, and support the development and operationalization of context-specific solutions to strengthen programme delivery across Circular Economy and Chemicals Waste Management initiatives.
Field Coordination & Implementation Support
Provide technical backstopping and coordination support across project locations to ensure quality, consistency, and timely implementation of activities.
Facilitate issue resolution and strengthen linkages between field teams and central project team.
Partnerships & Convergence:
Establish and maintain strategic partnerships with government counterparts, Urban Local Bodies (ULBs), civil society organizations (CSOs), and private sector stakeholders.
Facilitate convergence with relevant schemes and policies to enhance coordination and strengthen inclusive systems and service delivery.
Support engagement and coordination with vendors and service providers, including providing technical inputs to procurement and partnership processes.
Monitoring, Evaluation and Impact Measurement:
Support the development and operationalization of GESI-responsive indicators within project results frameworks and Annual Work Plans (AWPs).
Contribute to baseline assessments, data collection processes, and periodic reviews to monitor progress, assess impacts, generate insights, and inform adaptive management.
Data, Reporting and Insights:
Compile, analyse, and synthesize qualitative and quantitative data to generate actionable insights on inclusion outcomes.
Contribute to the preparation of high-quality project reports, donor communications, and knowledge outputs to support evidence-based decision-making.
Policy, Capacity & Knowledge Development:
Provide technical inputs to policy analysis, programme guidelines, and proposal development.
Design and support capacity-building initiatives for stakeholders and develop knowledge products, including case studies and tools, to promote learning, replication, and scaling up of inclusive practices.
The incumbent performs other duties within their functional profile as deemed necessary for the efficient functioning of the Office and the Organization.
