Main Purpose:
The Project Analyst, Gender and Social Inclusion, will support the integration of gender equality and social inclusion across UNDP's waste, chemicals, and circular economy programmes, with a focus on improving livelihoods, safety, and access to social protection for informal waste workers. The role will work with government, private sector, industries, and civil society partners to ensure inclusive programme design, implementation, and monitoring, building on learnings from Project Utthaan and related initiatives.

Duties and Responsibilities:
- Lead the integration of GESI across project design, planning, and implementation by applying relevant frameworks and tools.
- Coordinate and oversee the implementation of social inclusion interventions for informal waste workers, focusing on enhancing access to social protection, occupational safety, financial inclusion, and sustainable livelihoods.
- Undertake analysis of project components and field-level implementation challenges to identify inclusion gaps, particularly for women.
- Provide technical backstopping and coordination support across project locations.
- Establish and maintain strategic partnerships with government counterparts, ULBs, CSOs, and private sector stakeholders.
- Support the development and operationalization of GESI-responsive indicators within project results frameworks and AWPs.
- Compile, analyse, and synthesize qualitative and quantitative data to generate actionable insights on inclusion outcomes.
- Provide technical inputs to policy analysis, programme guidelines, and proposal development; design capacity-building initiatives.

Institutional Arrangement: Reporting to Project Specialist, Circular Economy and Chemicals Waste Management (NPSA 10).

Min. Education requirements:
- Advanced university degree (master's degree or equivalent) in Gender Studies, Development Studies, Public Policy, Social Sciences, or related field is required, or
- A first-level university degree (bachelor's degree) in the areas mentioned above in combination with an additional 2 years of qualifying experience.

Min. years of relevant work experience:
- Applicants with a Master's degree are not required to have professional work experience.
- Applicants with a Bachelor's degree are required to have a minimum of two (2) years of relevant professional experience working on gender equality, social inclusion, livelihoods, informal sector integration, social protection, or related development programmes.

Required Language(s): Fluency in English and Hindi is required.
