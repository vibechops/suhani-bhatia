Mission and Objectives
UNFPA's strategic plan focuses on three transformative results: ending preventable maternal deaths, ending unmet need for family planning, and ending gender-based violence and harmful practices.

Context
The UNFPA office in Odisha partners with the state government across sectors including health, education, disaster resilience, social justice, gender equality, and population dynamics.

Task Description
Under the direct supervision of the UNFPA Head of State Office (Odisha) and in close coordination with the UNFPA India Country Office Communications and RM/Partnership Unit:

A. Communications
- Support the execution of the state-specific 360-degree communications and media strategy.
- Draft, edit, and design communication materials (e.g. factsheets, presentations, leaflets, infographics) using tools such as Canva.
- Develop compelling content documenting UNFPA's work, including success stories, human-interest pieces, and testimonials.
- Develop and maintain a database of media contacts in Odisha.
- Support the organisation and coordination of events, campaigns, workshops, press conferences, and media briefings.
- Maintain an organised repository of communication assets.

B. Resource Mobilisation Support
- Contribute to the implementation of the resource mobilisation strategy of UNFPA India.
- Research and map potential donors, foundations, and funding opportunities.
- Assist in drafting concept notes and funding proposals.
- Support private sector engagement efforts, including due diligence processes and landscape mapping.

Eligibility: Age 18–80; Required Experience 3 years; Nationality: national, legal resident, or refugee status in country of assignment.
Required Education: Master's degree in Communications, Public Relations, Journalism, Social Work, Social Sciences, or a related field.
Skills: At least 3 years relevant professional experience in communications, media relations, or public relations; excellent writing/editing; Canva required.

Benefits include Monthly Volunteer Living Allowance: INR 70,158.41; entry lump sum USD 350; etc.
