Mission and Objectives
UNFPA is the lead UN agency for delivering a world where every pregnancy is wanted, every childbirth is safe, and every young person's potential is fulfilled. UNFPA’s strategic plan focuses on three transformative results: ending preventable maternal deaths, ending unmet need for family planning, and ending gender-based violence and harmful practices. UNFPA has been operating in India since 1974.
Context
The India Country Office is now in its 10th Country Programme (CP-10). CP-10 envisions an India where every woman and young person, including those from the most vulnerable groups, enjoys gender equality, fully realises sexual and reproductive health and reproductive rights, and fully contributes to sustainable development.
Guided by the UNFPA Strategic Plan 2022–2025, the Programme of Action of the International Conference on Population and Development (ICPD), and the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, the country programme will galvanise efforts to end preventable maternal deaths, unmet need for family planning, and gender-based violence and harmful practices, including in humanitarian situations.
The country programme was developed in consultation with national and state governments, civil society, United Nations organisations, and other stakeholders, including women- and youth-led organisations, based on the principles of inclusion, human rights, and gender equality.
In this context, the UNV Communications Specialist (Content Writer and Editor) will play an active role in:
* Ensuring and maintaining high-quality UNFPA written documentation.
* Communicating with impact to enhance the visibility of UNFPA leadership.
* Documenting programmatic interventions for advocacy and outreach.
The UNV Communications Specialist (Content Writer and Editor) will be part of an exciting and dynamic team with opportunities to learn, contribute, and co-create solutions that have lasting impact.
Task Description
As part of the UNFPA Policy and Partnerships Unit and under the direct supervision of the Communications and Media Specialist, the UNV Communications Specialist (Content Writer and Editor) will be responsible for:
* Creating, editing, and copy-editing high-quality communication materials, including opinion editorials, speeches, factsheets, talking points, web articles, press releases, and social media content.
* Building and managing high-profile partnerships with influencers and content creators to advance UNFPA’s agenda and increase engagement.
* Documenting and designing multimedia stories to showcase programmatic success and insights in support of programme goals.
* Developing communication strategies and supporting visual concepts that effectively translate complex ideas into accessible and engaging narratives tailored to target audiences.
* Conducting research to ensure communication and advocacy materials are data-driven, contextually relevant, and strategically aligned.
* Monitoring trends to identify content gaps and propose innovative approaches.
* Supporting the coordination of events, campaigns, and workshops to highlight UNFPA India’s work and strengthen partnerships across the ecosystem.
* Ensuring all written work is original, authentic, and appropriately cited.
* Assisting with planning and coordination of workshops, campaigns, and stakeholder engagement events.
* Ensuring consistency and coherence across all communication platforms while maintaining alignment with UNFPA India’s brand and style guidelines.
* Supporting additional writing assignments and other tasks as required by the Communications and Media Unit.
* Working independently and collaboratively to meet deadlines and deliver impactful, high-quality content.
Eligibility Criteria
Age: 18–80 years
Required Experience: 3 years
Nationality: Candidate must be a national, legal resident, or hold refugee status in the country of assignment.
Assignment Requirements
Education
Bachelor’s degree in Communications, Journalism, Public Relations, Advertising, Development, International Relations, Social Sciences, Public Administration, or a related field.
Languages
* English – Fluent (Required)
* Hindi – Fluent (Desirable)
Competencies and Values
* Strong sense of collaborative work, excellent communication, and interpersonal skills.
* Works collaboratively with colleagues to achieve organisational goals and values others’ ideas and expertise.
* Willingness to learn from others and place team objectives before personal interests.
* Integrity, commitment to the UNFPA mandate, and valuing diversity.
* Self-management and emotional intelligence.
* Analytical and strategic thinking, results orientation, and commitment to excellence.
* Knowledge sharing and continuous learning.
Skills and Experience
* At least three years of relevant experience in communications.
* Proven experience in English and Hindi writing and editing.
* Familiarity with development sector language and style, and understanding of publication requirements.
* Experience in report writing, speech writing, talking points development, editing, and formatting of reports, documents, and presentations.
* Experience in policy communication and research.
* Knowledge and use of leading social media platforms including X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, and others.
* Demonstrated understanding of design tools and software such as Canva and Adobe Creative Suite (desirable).
* Photography experience and basic video editing and production skills.
* Prior relationships with influencers and media partners are an advantage.
* Working knowledge of UNFPA mandate issues is an asset.
Additional Skills
* Excellent writing and editing skills with strong attention to detail and the ability to adapt writing styles for different audiences.
* Ability to build and manage effective partnerships, identify risks, and mitigate them.
* Ability to work independently and follow through on assignments.
* Diplomacy, tact, and patience.
* Ability to manage multiple tasks in a deadline-driven environment.
* Creativity and confidence in pitching communication ideas and strategies.
* Openness to critical feedback and differing points of view.
* Excellent attention to detail.
Area of Expertise
Communication
Living Conditions and Remarks
New Delhi is the national capital of India and is situated in the northern part of the country. The city is home to numerous businesses, international organisations, and diplomatic missions, making it highly cosmopolitan and culturally diverse.
As this is a national UN Volunteer assignment, the selected candidate will be responsible for arranging their own housing and living arrangements. National UN Volunteers are expected to be culturally sensitive and adapt to local traditions and practices.
The contract will last for the period indicated above, with possible extensions subject to funding availability, operational necessity, and satisfactory performance. There is no expectation of automatic renewal.
Benefits and Entitlements
The applicable Volunteer Living Allowance is provided monthly to cover housing, utilities, and normal living expenses.
Benefits include:
* Monthly Volunteer Living Allowance (VLA): INR 70,158.41
* Entry Lump Sum (one-time payment): USD 350 equivalent in INR
* Relocation Costs: USD 175 at the beginning and end of assignment, if applicable
* Exit Allowance: USD 120 per month served, payable upon successful completion of the assignment
* Medical and Life Insurance (Cigna Private Insurance)
* Annual Leave: 2.5 days accrued per calendar month
UNV will provide the selected candidate with the Conditions of Service, including the Code of Conduct, upon offer of assignment.
