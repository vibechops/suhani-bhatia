Individual Consultant (Local) - Young and Emerging Evaluator, UNFPA India 10th Country Program Evaluation

In line with the 2024 UNFPA Evaluation Policy, the UNFPA India CO is planning to conduct an evaluation of its tenth CP (2023-27). The purpose of the Country Programme Evaluation (CPE) is to strengthen accountability to stakeholders, support evidence-based decision-making, and improve learning.

The young and emerging evaluator (YEE) must be under 40 years of age.

Competencies, skills:
- Bachelor’s degree in development studies, population studies, economics, monitoring and evaluation, social sciences, or any other relevant discipline;
- Certificate in evaluation or equivalent qualification;
- Less than 10 years of work experience in monitoring and evaluation, research or social studies in the field of international development;
- Minimum - Master's Degree - 2 year(s) experience OR Bachelor's Degree - 4 year(s) experience
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills;
- Fluent in English; other languages an advantage

The consultancy will be for a total of 132 days between February-September 2026 throughout the evaluation process.
The YEE is required to work in the India country office in New Delhi.
