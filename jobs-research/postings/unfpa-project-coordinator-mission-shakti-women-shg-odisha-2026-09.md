Terms of Reference for Individual Consultant 

 Project Coordinator – Social Empowerment of Women Self Help Groups of Mission Shakti 

 Hiring Office: UNFPA Odisha – Bhubaneswar

 Purpose of the Consultancy 

 To support the UNFPA Odisha Office in the effective coordination, implementation support, monitoring and evaluation (M&E), documentation, and expansion of the project “Social Empowerment of Women Self Help Groups of Mission Shakti”, with direct engagement with implementing partners, vendors, and Mission Shakti Department stakeholders.

 The consultant will also play a key role in managing incentive disbursement data, developing knowledge products, and identifying opportunities for programme expansion.

 Working under the guidance of the Head of Office (HoO), the consultant will play a central role in maintaining momentum, building partnerships, and ensuring smooth coordination throughout project implementation.

 Scope of Work 

 I. Coordination and Stakeholder Engagement 

 
- Serve as the primary coordination focal point between UNFPA, the Mission Shakti Department, implementing partners, vendors, and other relevant stakeholders.

- Lead coordination meetings, joint planning exercises, and structured follow-ups with stakeholders.

- Ensure regular communication, planning, and troubleshooting across all implementing geographies.

- Facilitate the execution of TORs, contracts, and procurement processes involving vendors and partners.

- Prepare and share project briefs and updates with Government counterparts and UNFPA.

- Organise and support review meetings, field visits, and consultations with partners and stakeholders.

 
 II. Monitoring and Evaluation 

 
- Design and implement project monitoring tools and frameworks to track progress against key indicators.

- Conduct routine field visits and virtual check-ins with implementing partners to monitor activity implementation, outputs, and data flow.

- Review M&E reports from partners and flag risks or inconsistencies to the HoO.

- Support third-party evaluations, surveys, and impact assessments as required.

 
 III. Documentation and Knowledge Management 

 
- Compile progress reports capturing activities, outcomes, challenges, and recommendations.

- Draft policy notes, case studies, and human-interest stories from the field.

- Maintain and regularly update a centralised knowledge repository, including MIS, photo documentation, tools, and training resources.

- Contribute to the development of presentations, publications, and knowledge dissemination materials for internal and external use.

 
 IV. Implementation Support and Incentive Data Management 

 
- Provide regular technical support to implementing partners in planning and executing project deliverables.

- Assist in managing the incentive structure under the programme, including tracking disbursements, verifying data, and addressing anomalies.

- Maintain oversight of incentive claim processing, validation, and reporting with Mission Shakti and UNFPA finance teams.

 
 V. Expansion and Scale-up Support 

 
- Identify promising areas and interventions, as well as scalable practices emerging from implementation.

- Liaise with state-level departments and partners to explore and facilitate replication across new geographies or programme areas.

- Provide analytical inputs and briefs to inform scale-up discussions and planning.

 
 VI. Additional Responsibilities 

 Carry out any other related duties as requested by UNFPA.
