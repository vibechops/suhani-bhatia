State Head of Office
Level: NO-C
Position Number: 34489
Location: Jaipur
Full/Part time: Full-Time
Fixed term/Temporary: Fixed Term
Duration: One year (renewable)

The Position:
The State Head of Office (HOO) ensures the effective management of UNFPA activities across result areas within the respective State to accelerate achievement of the ICPD Programme of Action and Agenda 2030. You will lead the programme and operations staff members within the State and will collaborate with the Country Office (CO) management and technical staff.

How you can make a difference:
UNFPA Strategic Plan 2026-2029 focuses on four interconnected outcomes: ending the unmet need for family planning; ending preventable maternal deaths; ending gender-based violence and harmful practices; and adapting to demographic change through evidence and rights-based policies.

Job Purpose:
You will ensure the effective management of UNFPA activities in the areas of sexual and reproductive health and reproductive rights, adolescents and youth empowerment, and gender equality, women empowerment, and gender-based violence, integrating demographic resilience into policies and programmes in your assigned State.

A. Programme Management and Coordination:
- Lead the implementation of the UNFPA country programme in your assigned State, firmly anchored on rights-based, gender centric and results-based approaches.
- In close coordination with the State Government and collaboration with other partners oversee the management of the UNFPA programme in full compliance with Corporate policies and procedures.
- Oversee the achievement of programme results applying appropriate monitoring and oversight mechanisms.
- Lead in the formulation of the State level annual work plans; Monitor and supervise day-to-day implementation.
- In consultation with the CO team, lead humanitarian preparedness and response interventions in the State, ensuring adequate attention given to the needs of women and girls during emergencies.

B. Policy Dialogue and Technical Support:
- Conduct policy dialogue with Government counterparts, UN agencies and other development partners.
- Provide technical guidance to ensure that programmes are rights-based, gender-responsive and inclusive.

C. Advocacy, Partnerships and Resource Mobilization:
- Establish and nurture partnerships building strategic alliances towards ending preventable maternal deaths, ending unmet need for family planning and ending gender-based violence in all its forms including child marriage and gender-biased sex selection.
- Understand State level budget cycle and advocate for increases in State budgetary allocations towards maternal health, family planning and addressing gender-based violence.

D. State Office Management & Leadership:
- Manage UNFPA office in your assigned State; Supervise and guide State Office staff.

Education: Advanced degree in public policy, international development, public health, population studies, Law and human rights, or other related social science disciplines.

Knowledge and Experience:
- Five years of progressively responsible professional experience in the field of development management, including programme design, appraisal, budget management, and human resources management.
- Understanding of and experience in mainstreaming human rights and gender equality dimensions into programme design and implementation.
- Demonstrated expertise in advocacy and policy dialogue.

Languages: Fluency in English is mandatory. Working knowledge of Hindi and/or other local language is desirable.

Compensation: NOC-Step 1, competitive net salary of INR 49,26,329 per annum with health insurance and other benefits as applicable.
