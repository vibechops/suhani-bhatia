The Position
The Programme Analyst, based in the UNFPA State Office, contributes to the effective management of UNFPA activities across result areas within the respective State to accelerate the achievement of the ICPD Programme of Action and Agenda 2030. You will work with the State Head of Office and technical specialists in the planning, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of UNFPA interventions in the State.

You will report directly to the State Head of Office.

Job Purpose
You will support the effective management of UNFPA activities in the areas of sexual and reproductive health, adolescents and youth, gender, and population dynamics. You will contribute to project formulation, monitoring and reporting on results, ensure compliance with UNFPA's programming and budgetary policies and procedures, and support the development of partnerships and advocacy in support of the ICPD Programme of Action.

Programme Management and Coordination
- In close coordination with government and civil society counterparts, contribute to the design and implementation of the UNFPA Country Programme.
- Provide strategic and programmatic support for the development, implementation, monitoring and reporting of work plans.
- Analyse progress, identify bottlenecks and propose corrective solutions.
- Establish collaborative relationships with technical government counterparts, implementing partners and other state-based UN agencies.
- Draft mandatory quarterly and annual state-level programme and financial reports.

Policy Dialogue and Technical Support
- Support the State Head of Office in analysing and preparing materials for policy dialogue.
- Ensure programme implementation complies with UNFPA's corporate technical and programme policies and guidelines.
- Assist in implementing knowledge management strategies to capture lessons learnt and best practices.

Advocacy and Resource Mobilisation
- Contribute technical inputs to communications materials on UNFPA's transformative results and the ICPD Programme of Action.
- Contribute to state office resource mobilisation efforts, including concept note and proposal development.

Education
- Advanced degree in international development, public policy, public health, population studies, or another related social science discipline.

Knowledge and Experience
- Two years of professional experience in development management.
- Practical experience in programme design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation, including budget management.
- Good understanding of, and experience in mainstreaming, human rights and gender equality in programme design and implementation.
- Excellent written and oral communication skills.
- Fluency in English is mandatory. Working knowledge of one of the Indian Official Languages is desirable.

Compensation: NO-B Step 1, competitive net salary of INR 3,531,435 per annum, health insurance and other applicable benefits.
