Hiring Office: UNFPA Odisha, Bhubaneswar
Duration: 11 months (full-time)

Purpose of the Consultancy
UNFPA Odisha has partnered with the Government of Odisha to support the development of the Demography Intelligence Unit (DIU), an initiative that uses geospatial technology and artificial intelligence to strengthen evidence-based policymaking through demographic data analysis.

Scope of Work includes develop state demographic understanding; coordinate with technology partners; strengthen quality assurance and user experience; support policy development and technology innovation (including migration, geriatric health and disability); DigiShakti LMS initiatives.

Education: Master of Science, Master of Technology, or a related postgraduate degree specialising in Data Science, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Geospatial studies, or a closely related field.

Experience:
- Minimum three years of experience in similar programmes or projects.
- Experience working with government institutions and/or UN agencies is desirable.
- Proficiency in Python, QGIS and ArcGIS for Earth Observation analytics.
- Experience with deep learning tools, including TensorFlow, Keras and PyTorch.
- Fluency in written and spoken English required; Odia an advantage.
