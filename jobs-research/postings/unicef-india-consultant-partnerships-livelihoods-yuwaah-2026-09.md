TERMS OF REFERENCE
The consultant will support in strengthening ecosystem partnerships for youth livelihoods including wage employment, self-employment and entrepreneurship, for enabling sustainable opportunities for young people at scale. This will involve driving government engagement for the continued scale-up of YuWaah-supported livelihood models, along with managing identification and partnership-building for other new scalable wage employment, self-employment and entrepreneurship models, largely with government but also with other stakeholders, for their scale-up and sustainability. The role will also involve development of an outcome-based financing (OBF)/pay-by-results approach and facilitating related partnerships to strengthen livelihood outcomes within government systems in select states as well as at the center.

Technical requirements
:
As part of their application, candidates should submit a proposal on the following: Design a government engagement and scale-up strategy (3-5 pager) for adoption of a specific livelihood model of your choice – wage employment, self-employment, or entrepreneurship (e.g., youth, rural communities, persons with disabilities, young women, etc.) by a state or central government scheme in India. The model should use outcome-based funding as an approach to improve program/scheme outcomes and overall impact. (Use of ChatGPT or other LLM tools is discouraged; candidate needs to submit a proposal based on original thinking.) The strategy must: identify realistic government partner(s) and the scheme/budget line for adoption; be scalable to multiple geographies; be cost-effective and sustainable; include a clear engagement pathway, key milestones and risk mitigation approach; and specify measurable outcomes (e.g., adoption milestone, beneficiaries reached).
