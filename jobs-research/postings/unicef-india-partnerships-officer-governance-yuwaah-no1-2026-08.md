How can you make a difference?
In recent years, UNICEF’s work to support the unique needs of young people has gained
momentum as the challenges and opportunities facing adolescents and youth have become increasingly prominent. UNICEF is committed to delivering for all children and young people through its overarching goal of realizing the rights of every child and adolescent, especially the most disadvantaged.
Building on its work with and for adolescents, and aligned with the United Nations Youth Strategy, UNICEF launched Generation Unlimited (GenU), a global multi-stakeholder partnership dedicated to expanding opportunities for young people aged 10–24 years. The India chapter of GenU, known as YuWaah, brings together government, private sector, civil society, UN agencies, philanthropic organizations and young people to support young people’s transition from learning to earning and active citizenship.
Since its launch in 2019, YuWaah and its partners have reached more than 100 million young people across India through initiatives focused on skilling, employability, entrepreneurship, social impact and civic participation. As YuWaah enters its next phase of growth, there is an increasing need to strengthen engagement with strategic partners, deepen collaboration across sectors, and ensure effective functioning of its governance and partnership platforms. This includes supporting key mechanisms such as the Board and Leaders Council, managing partner engagement and reporting processes, convening ecosystem stakeholders, and strengthening collaboration with
government institutions and UN agencies.
Purpose of the Job:
The proposed position will support the effective functioning of YuWaah as a multistakeholder partnership platform. The incumbent will contribute to the coordination of governance mechanisms, partnership management and reporting processes, stakeholder engagement, and highlevel convenings. The role will also support engagement with government stakeholders, UN agencies and other development partners to advance collective action for youth development and strengthen the visibility, effectiveness and impact of YuWaah’s partnership ecosystem.
Roles & Responsibilities:
A) Governance, Partnership Management and Ecosystem Engagement
• Support the coordination and operationalisation of YuWaah’s governance mechanisms, including the Board, Leaders Council and other strategic engagement platforms.
• Support relationship management and engagement with YuWaah partners across the private sector, philanthropy, civil society, youth networks and other ecosystem stakeholders.
• Coordinate partner reporting, stakeholder communications and knowledge-sharing processes.
• Support the planning and delivery of high-level convenings, consultations and partnership engagement opportunities, including Youth Central and other strategic platforms.
• Contribute to the development of partnership materials, briefing notes, presentations and reports.
B) Government Relations, UN Partnerships and Advocacy
• Support engagement and coordination with government stakeholders, UN agencies and other development partners to advance YuWaah’s priorities.
• Facilitate collaboration, information-sharing and joint initiatives across partners and stakeholders.
• Support advocacy, knowledge management and thought leadership efforts related to youth development, employability, entrepreneurship and inclusion.
• Contribute to strengthening equity and inclusion considerations across YuWaah partnerships and initiatives, particularly for underserved and marginalized young people.
C) Other Responsibilities
• Support cross-functional initiatives, special projects and emerging priorities of YuWaah as required.
• Undertake other partnership, governance stakeholder engagement and coordination responsibilities as assigned by the supervisor.
