Design and Communications Internships (Impact Collective)

Vidhi provides internship opportunities to graduate and post-graduate students on a rolling basis throughout the year. Students enrolled in recognised law programmes or allied courses in humanities and public policy are welcome to apply. People interested in learning more about the philanthropic sector must apply.

The interns for the Communications Team will be required to:

-Assist the Communications Team in relation to developing social media creatives (carousels, static posts, simple reels);

-Format and layout of reports, briefs, and explainers;

-Create event collateral (invites, banners, standees, certificates); and

-Any other tasks related to Design and Communications

We will not entertain any applications submitted less than two months prior to the start date. For example, if the internship begins on May 1st, the application must be submitted by March 1st. Applications are reviewed quarterly. When applying, please indicate which quarter you are applying for, such as January to March.

Successful applicants will be notified 45 days before the start of the internship. Please avoid following up regarding the status of your application via email. Owing to the large volume of applications, we are unable to write personally to everyone who applies, informing them of the status of their application.

In addition to filling out the required details in the application form and providing their resume, applicants are also required to submit their creative portfolio that highlights their design work. The internship will last for three months and will be conducted in a hybrid format, with two in-office workdays each week.
