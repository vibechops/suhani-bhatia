Internships

Vidhi, Bengaluru provides internship opportunities to undergraduate students on a rolling basis throughout the year. Students enrolled in recognised law programmes or allied courses in humanities and public policy are welcome to apply.

Interns will have the opportunity to:

1. Work with the Vidhi Karnataka Team in the area of urban governance, on the issues of housing, water, municipal finance, technology, public transport and general administration
2. Work with the Judicial Reforms (JALDI) Team on areas of judicial process reengineering, gender diversity in the judiciary, and court infrastructure
3. Interact with legal academics and practising lawyers, engage with civil society organisations
4. Conduct research on cutting-edge issues of law and policy
5. Assist with drafting research papers and reports

Terms:

1. Two interns shall be selected each month, one for each team.
2. The internships shall be full-time and in physical mode only, unless stated otherwise.
3. The selected interns will be required to undertake one research project for the course of the 4-week internship based on any area of work the team is currently engaged in. They will have to submit the research question to the Internship Coordinator and submit a paper/report at the end of the internship in order to be entitled to a certificate.

How to apply

The application for the internship should be submitted by clicking on the button below.

It is preferable that candidates apply two months prior to their preferred month of internship. In any case, we will not entertain any applications submitted after the 5th of the previous month. For example, if the internship is to begin on 1st May, the internship application must be submitted by the 5th of April.
