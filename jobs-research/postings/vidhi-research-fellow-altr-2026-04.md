The Vidhi Centre for Legal Policy (“Vidhi”) is an independent think tank doing legal research to make better laws and improve governance for the public good.

Research Fellows at Vidhi work closely with their teams to conduct legal and policy research, draft publications, and contribute to evidence-based law and governance reform.

In this role, the Fellow will begin their term with the Applied Law and Technology Research (ALTR) team working on legal reforms for the digital economy, while also being part of an institutional pool of researchers who work with other teams during their tenure, based on organisational needs and individual strengths.

Essential requirements:
- An undergraduate and/or postgraduate degree in law.
- At least 1-2 years of demonstrable work experience.
- Excellent research and writing skills.
- Excellent communication skills.

This is a full-time position based out of New Delhi.
Remuneration will be competitive, in line with experience and existing structure at Vidhi.

Application: online portal. CV (max two A4 pages), statement of motivation (max 500 words), and an unpublished original writing sample of about 1000 words on an issue relevant to tech policy demonstrating research and analysis skillset.
