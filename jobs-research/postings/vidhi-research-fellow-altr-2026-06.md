Research Fellows at Vidhi conduct legal and policy research, draft publications, and contribute to evidence-based law and governance reform.

This Fellow begins with the Applied Law and Technology Research (ALTR) team on legal reforms for the digital economy (privacy, data governance, AI ethics and governance, digital public infrastructure, IT governance), while also joining an institutional pool of researchers who may work with other teams.

Key responsibilities: high-quality research on Indian and international legal/policy frameworks; research and legislative drafting assistance; independent research projects; reports, notes, presentations; meetings with government officials and stakeholders; consultations.

Essential: undergraduate and/or postgraduate law degree; at least 1-2 years demonstrable work experience; excellent research, writing and communication. Desirable: prior public policy experience.

Full-time, New Delhi. Competitive remuneration. Apply via Vidhi online portal (CV ≤2 pages, 500-word motivation, two references, 500–700 word tech-policy writing sample). Deadline: 21 June 2026. Marked Expired on Lawctopus.
