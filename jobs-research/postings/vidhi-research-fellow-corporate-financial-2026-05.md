The Corporate Law and Financial Regulation Team works on legal reforms in the financial sector and corporate affairs (bankruptcy, company law, competition law, financial regulation).

The Research Fellow undertakes high-quality research, analysis and legislative drafting for engaged and origination projects; coordinates meetings with government officials and stakeholders; prepares reports, research notes and presentations.

Essential: undergraduate or postgraduate law degree; at least 2 years demonstrable experience in corporate law and financial regulation (LLM counts as experience); excellent presentation, legal research, writing and communication; ability to independently manage research topics.

Desirable: insolvency, securities, competition, company law (including transactional) and commercial laws experience.

Full-time, New Delhi. Deadline: 10 May 2026. Apply via Vidhi portal with CV, 500-word motivation, ≤1000-word writing sample on corporate/financial regulation, two professional referees.
