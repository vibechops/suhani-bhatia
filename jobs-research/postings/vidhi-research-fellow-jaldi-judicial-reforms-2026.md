Research Fellow

ABOUT VIDHI CENTRE FOR LEGAL POLICY: The Vidhi Centre for Legal Policy ("Vidhi") is an independent think tank doing legal research to make better laws and improve governance for the public good. Vidhi is committed to producing legal research of the highest standard with the aim of informing public debate and contributing to improved governance. Vidhi works with Ministries of the Government of India and State Governments, the judiciary, as well as other public institutions, providing research, drafting support, and recommendations to improve governance. Vidhi also conducts and freely disseminates independent research in areas of legal reform which it believes is critical to India's future.

Vidhi is an equal opportunity employer and neither practices nor tolerates any kind of discriminatory behaviour.

ROLE DESCRIPTION: Research Fellows at Vidhi work closely with their teams to conduct legal and policy research, draft publications, and contribute to evidence-based law and governance reform.

In this role, the Fellow will begin their term with the JALDI (Justice, Access and Lowering Delays in India) team, working on issues related to judicial reform and access to justice and contributing to ongoing research and reform efforts on judicial administration and access to justice, while also being part of an institutional pool of researchers who work with other teams during their tenure, based on organisational needs and individual strengths.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Essential requirements:

- Undergraduate degree in law
- Excellent legal research and writing skills
- Excellent communication skills
- Ability to work on multiple tasks and work on tight deadlines when necessary
- Comfortable working with interdisciplinary teams
- Willingness to travel

Desirable requirements:

- 1-3 years of demonstrable work experience
- A demonstrable interest in judicial reform or access to justice
- Knowledge or experience of monitoring and evaluating projects
- Ability to speak and communicate in different Indian languages
- Ability to analyse data

LOCATION AND REMUNERATION

- This is a full-time position.
- Preference will be given to those willing to work in Bangalore. However, applicants willing to work in Delhi, Mumbai, or Chennai are also welcome to apply.
- Remuneration will be competitive, in line with experience and the existing structure at Vidhi.

APPLICATION PROCESS

- Latest Curriculum Vitae (CV) (not exceeding two A4 pages).
- Essay (maximum of 500 words) outlining three areas of reform which the applicant believes can help improve access to justice in India.
- Names and contact details of two references.
