JOB POST: Senior Resident Fellow at Vidhi Centre for Legal Policy [Climate & Ecosystems; Exp of 5 Years]: Apply by May 17, 2026!

Vidhi Centre for Legal Policy is inviting applications for the Job post of Senior Resident Fellow in any of the offices of Vidhi.

About Vidhi

The Vidhi Centre for Legal Policy ("Vidhi") is an independent think tank doing legal research to make better laws and improve governance for the public good. Vidhi is committed to producing legal research of the highest standard with the aim of informing public debate and contributing to improved governance.

Vidhi works with Ministries of the Government of India and State Governments, as well as other public institutions, providing research and drafting support at various stages of law-making. Vidhi also conducts and freely disseminates independent research in areas of legal reform which it believes is critical to India's future.

Role Description

The Climate & Ecosystems team at Vidhi primarily works towards strengthening nature conservation through law. It is working to enable an ecosystem for legal and policy reforms in the conservation sector. The team undertakes original research and works in close association with various government and non-government stakeholders. The Senior Resident Fellow will be an integral part of the Climate and Ecosystems Team and will be expected to undertake the following responsibilities:

- Ideate and execute public dissemination strategies including social media content, and other materials for disseminating research.
- Conduct high-quality legal and policy research on forests, biodiversity, wildlife, climate, and related ecosystems.
- Draft research reports, design and coordinate public consultations, and collect empirical information.
- Supervise ongoing projects and ensure research quality.
- Draft research proposals, oversee project timelines, and report to funders.
- Manage the team and collaborations with external stakeholders.
- Organize and participate in meetings and consultations with stakeholders and partner organizations.
- Travelling to different States for research and consultation, which may include remote locations.

Eligibility

Essential Qualifications:

- Masters degree in law or a relevant field with at least 5 years of demonstrable work experience in environmental laws and policy research.
- Strong research and analytical skills.
- Excellent writing and editing skills for research reports and policy documents.
- Experience in managing teams, drafting research proposals, and coordinating with funders.
- Experience in organising and managing public events, including discussions, workshops, and roundtable meetings.
- Ability to multitask and deliver high-quality work within tight deadlines.
- Proficiency in office productivity tools (MS Excel, PowerPoint, Canva).

Desirable Qualifications:

- Experience working with research institutions or non-profits working in forest, wildlife, or related areas.
- Experience working in partnership with government officials, legislators, civil society organisations, and local communities.
- Publications in peer-reviewed journals or other reputed research organizations on relevant subjects.
- Experience in public speaking and moderating discussions.

Location

This is a full-time position which may be based out of any of Vidhi's offices (Bengaluru, Mumbai, New Delhi, and Chennai), with the final location to be discussed and confirmed at the time of joining.

Renumeration

- Remuneration will be competitive, in line with experience and existing structure at Vidhi.
