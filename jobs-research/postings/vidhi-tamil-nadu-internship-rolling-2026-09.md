Internships

Vidhi Tamil Nadu works on law and policy issues impacting Tamil Nadu. There are no verticals/ teams in Vidhi Tamil Nadu.

Vidhi Tamil Nadu provides internship opportunities to students pursuing graduate and post-graduate courses in recognised law programmes or allied courses in humanities and public policy. Internships are offered on a rolling basis throughout the year.

Interns at Vidhi Tamil Nadu will have the opportunity to:

- Conduct research on cutting-edge issues of law and policy issues impacting Tamil Nadu;
- Assist with drafting reports;
- Interact with academics and practising lawyers.

We will not entertain any applications submitted less than two months prior to the start date. For example, if the internship is to begin on 1st May, the internship application must be submitted by the 1st of March.

Successful applicants will be notified 45 days before the start of the internship. Please avoid following up regarding the status of your application over email. Owing to the large volume of applications, we are unable to write personally to everyone who applies informing them of the status of their application.

Apart from filling in the details required in the linked form and providing their resume, applicants are also required to submit a writing sample on any legal or policy issue. Please ensure that the writing sample does not exceed 3000 words.
