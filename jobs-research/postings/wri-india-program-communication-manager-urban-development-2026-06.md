Program Communication Manager – WRI India (Urban Development / Sustainable Cities)

You will support the execution of the strategic communication priorities for WRI India’s Sustainable Cities program. You will translate rigorous research and on-groundwork into high-impact narratives and multi-format communication products for online and offline communication, strengthening WRI India’s positioning at the sub-national, national and global levels.

You will be required to craft compelling messaging across multiple projects, themes, and partners, while leading end-to-end execution of communication products and other deliverables. You will support evidence-based communications of WRI India’s diverse work under urban development pillar—spanning urban water and climate resilience, economic planning and development, public spaces, and governance—translating them into cohesive, integrated storytelling tailored for varied audience sets and channels.

What you will do (from listing):
Review and strengthen all communications outputs across Urban Development (UD) projects, ensuring alignment with WRI India brand, editorial, and design standards across social media platforms and offline collateral.

Editorial, Media, Knowledge Products & Partnerships (35%):
Support the editorial and research direction for the program: plan and co-create blogs, impact stories, case studies, op-eds, research publications, and other donor-facing products in line with WRI India standards.

Strong editorial skills: excellent writing, editing, and narrative structuring in English; Hindi or other Indian language proficiency like Kannada/Marathi is an added advantage.

About WRI India:
WRI India, an independent charity organisation registered as India Resources Trust, provides objective information and practical proposals to foster environmentally sound and socially equitable development. Through research, analysis, and recommendations, WRI India puts ideas into action to build transformative solutions to protect the earth, promote livelihoods, and enhance human well-being. Know more: wri-india.org
