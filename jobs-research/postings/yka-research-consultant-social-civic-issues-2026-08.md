Title: Research Consultant – Social and Civic Issues, Youth Ki Awaaz
Location: Hybrid (Delhi preferred)
Type: Project Based Consultancy
Probation: 3 months from date of joining

About Youth Ki Awaaz
Youth Ki Awaaz (YKA) is India’s largest civic participation platform for young people – a space to tell their own stories, engage with civic issues through polling and data, and turn their voices into actions that shift public narratives and policy. Over 18 years, YKA has built a writer network, a campaigns ecosystem, and a suite of civic intelligence products including Yoot, our WhatsApp-based polling tool, and Chorus, our discourse analysis framework.

About Yoot
Yoot is YKA’s WhatsApp-based polling product – a civic participation infrastructure that lets young people across India weigh in on issues that matter to them, in a language and format they already use. With 12,000 active users and a 100,000-user target, Yoot is one of YKA’s fastest-growing products and a key part of our commercial intelligence offering. This is not a tool in maintenance mode – it is a product being actively scaled.

The Role
We are looking for a research professional with a social sciences background to join our Research team, working with YOOT, intersection of digital technologies and the YKA community.
This role is for a professional with at least 5 years of relevant experience who is excited to work at the cutting edge of projects on analysis of youth realities on YOOT
You will be working on a specific project which requires expertise in social science research, quantitative inquiry, adolescent behaviour, digital cultures, and gender and sexuality.
The ideal candidate for this position is a curious and motivated self-starter with a keen interest in using research to examine social behaviour, cultural norms, and lived experiences to generate actionable insights.
A commitment to social justice and equity is essential.

Subject Matter Expertise
Adolescent digital cultures, online behaviours, and peer-to-peer communication (essential)
Sexual and reproductive health and rights (SRHR), gender, consent, relationships, and identity (essential)
Social norms, youth behaviour, digital media, and informal learning ecosystems
Additional: Digital well-being, online safety, misinformation, body image, LGBTQIA+ inclusion, youth mental health, and the role of digital platforms in shaping adolescent beliefs, attitudes, and decision-making.

Responsibilities
Research: Contribute to original research and analysis on identified issues; Undertake literature reviews, develop research tools, oversee data collection, stakeholder mapping, and other research activities; Produce research outputs, including reports, and contribute to the development of relevant curriculum as per the findings and learnings.
Events and external engagements: Support YKA in representing the research in workshops, panels, conferences and other events; Support partner communication from the research front; Support outreach, coordination, and relationship-building with civil society actors, and allied stakeholders
Project administration: Manage day-to-day administrative tasks pertaining to the implementation of research and advocacy projects; Track deliverables, timelines, and facilitate clear communication across teams; Maintain project calendars and anticipate reporting timelines

Required Qualifications
A master’s degree in research, public policy, law, economics, sociology, development studies, international relations, political science, or related fields (Good to have) – a PhD will be appreciated but is not essential to apply for this role
A minimum of five years of relevant professional experience
Experience of developing research tools such as questionnaires, review of literature
Understanding of research ethics – especially with minors.
Excellent writing and analytical skills, with the ability to translate complex findings into actionable insights for policymakers, civil society, academia, and other stakeholders
Experience of research report writing and ability to understand cultural intersections with prominent issues
Ability to conduct statistical analysis on large scale data sets, along with weighing and raking of data
Strong project management and operational systems thinking along with comfort navigating ambiguity and building processes from scratch
Demonstrated interest in working on adolescent/ youth-related issues
Ability to work collaboratively across networks and interdisciplinary teams
Fluency in English and at least one additional Indian language, preferably Hindi
An understanding of civic issues in India and genuine curiosity about what young people think about them
Content, campaign and/or digital marketing experience (good to have)
A social sciences background (must have)

How To Apply
Send your CV, 2 samples of previously written research reports (authored by you) and a short cover note (no more than 300 words) careers@youthkiawaaz.com with the subject line ‘Research Consultant – Social and Civic Issues’.
