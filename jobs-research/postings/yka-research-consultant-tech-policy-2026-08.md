Title: Research Consultant – Tech Policy, Youth Ki Awaaz
Location: Hybrid (Delhi preferred)
Type: Project Based Consultancy
Probation: 3 months from date of joining

Important: Candidates without experience in quantitative research, statistical/data analysis, and technical tools such as R, Python, or equivalent will be rejected at the screening stage.

About Youth Ki Awaaz
Youth Ki Awaaz (YKA) is India’s largest civic participation platform for young people – a space to tell their own stories, engage with civic issues through polling and data, and turn their voices into actions that shift public narratives and policy. Over 18 years, YKA has built a writer network, a campaigns ecosystem, and a suite of civic intelligence products including Yoot, our WhatsApp-based polling tool, and Chorus, our discourse analysis framework.

About Yoot
Yoot is YKA’s WhatsApp-based polling product – a civic participation infrastructure that lets young people across India weigh in on issues that matter to them, in a language and format they already use. With 12,000 active users and a 100,000-user target, Yoot is one of YKA’s fastest-growing products and a key part of our commercial intelligence offering.

The Role
We are looking for a research & policy professional to join our Research team, working with YOOT, intersection of digital technologies and the YKA community.
This role is for a professional with at least 5 to 8 years of relevant experience who is excited to work at the cutting edge of projects on analysis of youth realities on YOOT.
You will be working on a specific project which requires expertise of tech policy, understanding of the Indian digital ecosystem and regulations.
The ideal candidate for this position is a curious and motivated self-starter with a keen interest in using research to influence policy discourse and public narratives on tech policy in India. A commitment to social justice and equity is essential.

Subject Matter Expertise
Data governance and policy, in national, regional, and international governance (essential)
AI & Platform regulation, child safety (essential)
Digital public infrastructure, climate justice, and tech policy
Additional: Digital futures of work, workers’ rights in the platform economy, working conditions in digital value chains, and sustainable digital infrastructures

Responsibilities
Research: Contribute to original research and analysis on issues related to data, platform, and AI governance; Undertake literature reviews, develop research tools, oversee data collection, stakeholder mapping, and other research activities; Produce research outputs, including reports, policy briefs, background papers, explainers, and other public-facing commentary; Contribute to drafting submissions, consultations, and responses to policy developments
Events and external engagements: Support YKA in representing the research in workshops, panels, conferences and other events; Support outreach, coordination, and relationship-building with researchers, policymakers, civil society actors, and allied stakeholders; Support partner communication from the research front
Project administration: Manage day-to-day administrative tasks pertaining to the implementation of research and advocacy projects; Track deliverables, timelines, and facilitate clear communication across teams; Maintain project calendars and anticipate reporting timelines

Essential Qualifications:
A master’s degree in research, public policy, law, economics, sociology, development studies, international relations, political science, or related fields. A PhD will be appreciated but is not essential.
A minimum of five years of relevant professional experience
Experience in quantitative research, statistical/data analysis, and technical tools such as R, Python, or equivalent
Experience of developing research tools such as questionnaires
Understanding of research ethics especially with minors.
Excellent writing and analytical skills, with the ability to translate complex findings into actionable insights for policymakers, civil society, academia, and other stakeholders
Experience of research report writing and ability to understand cultural intersections with prominent issues
Ability to conduct statistical analysis on large scale data sets, along with weighing and raking of data
Strong project management and operational systems thinking along with comfort navigating ambiguity and building processes from scratch
Demonstrated interest in technology policy, digital governance, AI, and development, with the capacity to translate findings across multiple thematic areas
Ability to work collaboratively across networks and interdisciplinary teams
Fluency in English and at least one additional Indian language, preferably Hindi
An understanding of civic issues in India and genuine curiosity about what young people think about them
Content, campaign and/or digital marketing experience
A social sciences background will be desirable

Send your CV, 2 samples of previously written policy reports (authored by you) and a short cover note (no more than 300 words) careers@youthkiawaaz.com with the subject line Research Consultant – Tech Policy
