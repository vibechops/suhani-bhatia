The Policy in Action Program is a month-long engagement for young professionals and university students exploring public policy and development. It delivers skills in policy analysis, advocacy and leadership, plus live projects for policymakers (MPs, MLAs, ministries and government institutions).

November–December 2026 edition: virtual; 14 November to 13 December 2026. Sessions run by YLAC founders Rohit and Aparajita (also co-founders of The Quantum Hub). Mentorship by TQH public policy managers.

Eligibility: 20–30 years; Indian citizens only (including those living abroad for online editions). No academic/experience prerequisites.

Deadlines: First/scholarships deadline 23 August 2026; Final deadline 27 September 2026.

Fees: Rs. 16,000 (+ GST @18%), payable after selection. Need- and merit-based scholarships 50–100% available (scholarship essay + two references; scholarship applications by 23 Aug 2026).

Time commitment: ~12–15 hours/week outside classroom sessions plus mandatory weekend sessions. Projects: policy research and/or advocacy for real policymaker briefs.

Apply via links on https://theylacproject.com/policy-in-action-program/
